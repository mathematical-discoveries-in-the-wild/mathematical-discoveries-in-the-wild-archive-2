# 1607.05350 — ETF integer span is a lattice iff the frame is rational

Status: literature_already_answered (full characterization of lattice
existence).

Model: GPT5.6.

Source: Böttcher, Fukshansky, Garcia, Maharaj, and Needell, *Lattices from
tight equiangular frames*, arXiv:1607.05350, introduction and Proposition 2.1.

Supporting answer: Böttcher and Fukshansky, *Lattices and quadratic forms from
tight frames in Euclidean spaces*, arXiv:1704.07735, Proposition 1 and
Theorem 3.

The source proves that rationality of the reciprocal-cosine parameter alpha is
necessary. The addendum explicitly says the reverse implication was
overlooked, proves it, and gives the exact equivalence between alpha being a
positive integer, the ETF integer span being a lattice, and the norm-form
having separated values.

The characterization resolves lattice existence, not the source's broader
request to classify every geometric property of the resulting lattices.
