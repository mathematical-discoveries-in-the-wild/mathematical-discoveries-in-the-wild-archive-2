\section*{Status and exact sources}

\textbf{Status:} \texttt{literature\_already\_answered} for one question in
the introduction of Erschler--Ozawa, arXiv:1609.08585 \cite{EO}.

The source passage is on PDF page~3, immediately after Corollary~2.5.  The
answer is stated on PDF page~2 of the distinct later paper of
Brieussel--Zheng, arXiv:1706.00707 \cite{BZ}, and its construction is detailed
in Subsection~3.3.

\section*{Source question}

For a symmetric finitely supported generating probability measure $\mu$ on a
finitely generated group $G$, Corollary~2.5 of \cite{EO} proves that $G$ has
Shalom's property $H_{\mathrm{FD}}$ if either
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}
 \left\|\mu^{*n}-\mu^{*(1+\delta)n}\right\|_1<2
 \quad\text{for some }\delta>0,
\]
or
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}\mu^{*n}\!\left(B_G(c\sqrt n)\right)>0
 \quad\text{for every }c>0.
\]
The authors then record the existence question: they know no group satisfying
$H_{\mathrm{FD}}$ while failing the assumption of Corollary~2.5.

\section*{Exact later answer}

Brieussel--Zheng first recall precisely this Erschler--Ozawa criterion and
then state on PDF page~2:
\begin{quote}
Examples of groups with property $H_{\mathrm{FD}}$ not satisfying the
assumption of Erschler--Ozawa's corollary can be obtained from their
construction.
\end{quote}
Here this sentence is paraphrased only by suppressing cross-references; the
mathematical assertion is exact.

The construction in Subsection~3.3 is a diagonal product $\Delta$ formed from
finite wreath-product factors.  Its FC-center $Z^{\mathrm{FC}}(\Delta)$ has
quotient
\[
 \Delta/Z^{\mathrm{FC}}(\Delta)
 \cong (A\times B)\wr\mathbb Z,
\]
with $A\times B$ finite.  The weakly mixing harmonic-cocycle factorization
used there shows that $\Delta$ has the stronger property $H_{\mathrm T}$,
hence $H_{\mathrm{FD}}$.  Varying the scales in the same construction gives
the random-walk behavior needed to lie outside the sufficient hypothesis.
Thus the later paper supplies the existence examples requested in \cite{EO};
this is a literature answer, not a new theorem of the present run.

\section*{Scope limitation}

This identification answers only the existence question following
Corollary~2.5.  It does not settle the source's separate comparison with
Ozawa's shifted-convolution condition, either question about F\o lner balls,
the geometric detectability of torsion, or the status of
$(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)\wr\mathbb Z^2$.  Indeed \cite{BZ} still lists the last
problem as open.
