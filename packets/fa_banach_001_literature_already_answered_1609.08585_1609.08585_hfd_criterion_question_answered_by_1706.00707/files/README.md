# An \(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\) group outside the random-walk criterion

Status: `literature_already_answered` (one question from the introduction)

Original source: Anna Erschler and Narutaka Ozawa, *Finite-Dimensional
Representations constructed from Random Walks*, arXiv:1609.08585, PDF page 3.

Answer source: Jeremie Brieussel and Tianyi Zheng, *Shalom's property
\(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\) and extensions by \(\mathbb Z\) of locally finite groups*,
arXiv:1706.00707, PDF page 2 and Subsection 3.3.

## Identification

Erschler--Ozawa prove Corollary 2.5: either of two random-walk hypotheses is
sufficient for Shalom's property \(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\). They then say that they do
not know any group with \(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\) which fails the assumption of that
corollary.

The later Brieussel--Zheng paper explicitly states on PDF page 2 that examples
with \(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\) not satisfying the Erschler--Ozawa assumption can be
obtained from its diagonal-product construction. The construction is an
FC-central extension of a finite-lamp lamplighter group; the cited factorization
theorem for weakly mixing harmonic cocycles gives the stronger property
\(H_{\mathrm T}\), hence \(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\). This is an explicit answer by a
distinct later paper.

## Scope

This packet clears only the existence question above. It does not claim to
settle the source's comparison with Ozawa's shifted-convolution criterion, the
geometric detectability of torsion, the question about Foelner balls, or whether
\((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)\wr\mathbb Z^2\) has property
\(H_{\mathrm{FD}}\).
