# Conjecture 6.8 is answered by Rubin's Part II

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Source question: B. Rubin, *Funk, Cosine, and Sine Transforms on Stiefel and
Grassmann manifolds*, arXiv:1009.2184, Conjecture 6.8 on PDF page 38.

Supporting answer: B. Rubin, *Funk, Cosine, and Sine Transforms on Stiefel and
Grassmann manifolds, II*, arXiv:1103.1161.  The introduction on PDF page 2
explicitly says that Part II answers the pointwise questions from Part I.
Theorem 3.1(iv) (PDF pages 7--8) supplies pointwise analytic continuation and
smoothness; the paragraph preceding Theorem 6.4 and Theorem 6.4 (PDF pages
13--14) give the normalized meromorphic family and pointwise composition
formula; Theorem 6.1 (PDF pages 12--13) gives pointwise Funk inversion.

Thus all four clauses of the source's pointwise Conjecture 6.8 are covered.
As usual, its phrase “the polar set” must mean the universal possible/excluded
polar set: actual poles may cancel for a particular input (and necessarily do
for the zero function).
