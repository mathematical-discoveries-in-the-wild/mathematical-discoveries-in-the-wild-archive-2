# Supporting-paper evidence

Supporting paper:

- Igor A. Vestfrid, *Stability of almost surjective epsilon-isometries of
  Banach spaces*, Journal of Functional Analysis 269 (2015), 2165--2170.
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2015.04.009>
- Theorem used: Theorem 2(iii).

The author-copy full-text record was checked on 2026-08-11.  Its Theorem
2(iii) states that if `tau(f)<1/2`, then there is a surjective linear isometry
`V:X -> Y` satisfying `||f(x)-Vx|| <= 2 epsilon` for every `x`.  The next
sentence explicitly attributes the `tau(f)=0` problem to Vaisala and says that
Theorem 2(iii) answers it affirmatively, even under `tau(f)<1/2`.

The publicly indexed author-copy endpoint was:

<https://www.researchgate.net/publication/289649371_Stability_of_almost_surjective_e-isometries_of_Banach_spaces>

The direct PDF endpoint exposed by that record returned HTTP 403 when fetched
both in the restricted environment and after network escalation.  Therefore
no supporting PDF is represented as locally archived in this packet.

An independent later confirmation was also checked: L. Cheng, Q. Shen,
W. Zhang, and Y. Zhou, *More on stability of almost surjective
epsilon-isometries of Banach spaces*, Science China Mathematics 60 (2017),
275--284, DOI 10.1007/s11425-016-5155-0, Theorem 2.5.  It proves the
equivalence of `tau(f)=0` with existence of a surjective linear isometry within
`2 epsilon`.  This confirmation is not needed for the explicit 2015 answer.
