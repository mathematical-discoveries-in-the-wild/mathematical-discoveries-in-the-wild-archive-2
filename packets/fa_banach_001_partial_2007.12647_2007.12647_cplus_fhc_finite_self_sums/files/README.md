# Finite self-direct-sums for regular C-type FHC operators

This packet gives a new partial result for the question in arXiv:2007.12647
asking whether frequent hypercyclicity of `T` forces frequent hypercyclicity
of `T oplus T`.

For `C_{+,1}`-type operators with positive amplification margin

`limsup (delta_k - tau_k) / Delta_k > 0`,

every finite self-direct-sum is frequently hypercyclic. The proof synchronizes
the published periodic-shadowing construction across finitely many
coordinates. It applies in particular to the chaotic frequently hypercyclic,
non-hereditarily frequently hypercyclic example of arXiv:2409.07103, ruling
that family out as a counterexample to the self-sum problem.
