# Diliberto--Straus convergence for two-point fibres

This packet gives a candidate strong partial answer to the open question
following Theorem 2.3 of arXiv:1603.07073.

Main result: if both algebra quotients have fibres of cardinality at most
two and satisfy the source's C-property, the Diliberto--Straus residual
norms converge to `dist(h,A1+A2)` without assuming `A1+A2` closed.  The
midrange maps are averages over continuous involutions, and a
Laurent-polynomial estimate gives `O(n^{-1/2})` decay on either summand.

The packet also gives an explicit compact space and two involution-invariant
algebras whose algebraic sum is not closed, so the theorem includes cases
outside the source's Theorem 2.3.

The unrestricted question for arbitrary fibres is not claimed solved.
Eight focused upgrade attempts and their obstructions are recorded in the
run attempt log.
