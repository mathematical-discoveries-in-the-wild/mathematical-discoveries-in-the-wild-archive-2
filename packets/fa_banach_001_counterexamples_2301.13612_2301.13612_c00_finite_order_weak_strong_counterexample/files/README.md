# Finite-order weak--strong counterexample in a generalised Schwartz space

This packet answers the specific follow-up in Question 5.3.9(i) of arXiv
`2301.13612` negatively. For every finite `k>=1`, the generalised Schwartz
space `c_00` with its product-subspace topology admits a scalarwise smooth
curve which is not `C^k` as an `c_00`-valued curve.

The packet also records the already-known positive Banach-space answer:
Schur Banach spaces, including `ell^1`, satisfy the full finite-order
weak--strong principle.

Status: candidate full counterexample, likely valid subject to human review.
