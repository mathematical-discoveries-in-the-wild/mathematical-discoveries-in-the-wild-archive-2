\section*{Source Question}
The source is K.~R. Davidson, A. Dor-On, O.~M. Shalit, and B. Solel,
\emph{Dilations, inclusions of matrix convex sets, and completely positive
maps}, arXiv:1601.07993 \cite{DDOSS}.

For a convex body $K\subseteq\mathbb R^d$, let
\[
 \theta(K)=\inf\{C>0:\mathcal W^{\max}(K)
 \subseteq C\mathcal W^{\min}(K)\}.
\]
On page 35, Remark 7.21, the source says that it is not known whether the
factor $d$ can be improved for a particular $d$-dimensional convex set and
asks, in particular, whether $d$ is optimal for $K=[-1,1]^d$.

\section*{Supporting Answer}
The supporting paper is B. Passer, O.~M. Shalit, and B. Solel,
\emph{Minimal and maximal matrix convex sets}, arXiv:1706.05654
\cite{PSS}. It cites the source as reference [6] and explicitly identifies
the formerly open cube problem on page 2.

On page 29 it proves
\[
 \mathcal W^{\max}([-1,1]^d)
 \subseteq \sqrt d\,\mathcal W^{\min}([-1,1]^d)
\]
and states that the factor $\sqrt d$ is optimal. Hence
\[
 \boxed{\theta([-1,1]^d)=\sqrt d.}
\]
Theorem 6.9 on page 32 gives the broader sharp formula
\[
 \theta(B_{p,d})=d^{1-|1/p-1/2|},\qquad 1\leq p\leq\infty,
\]
whose $p=\infty$ case is exactly the cube.

Thus the source's factor $d$ is not optimal for the cube when $d>1$; the
exact constant is $\sqrt d$. Equivalently, every $d$-tuple of self-adjoint
contractions has a commuting self-adjoint dilation with coordinate norms at
most $\sqrt d$, and the uniform factor cannot be decreased.
