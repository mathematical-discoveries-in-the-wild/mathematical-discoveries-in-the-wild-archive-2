# Evidence and provenance map

## Primary problem statement

- L. Zou, *Inequalities related to Heinz and Heron means*, J. Math. Inequal. 7 (2013), 389–397, DOI 10.7153/jmi-07-34.
- Remark 4.2, equation (4.2), and the following Conjecture state the all-unitarily-invariant-norm claim with the standard Heinz sum `A^nu X B^(1-nu) + A^(1-nu) X B^nu`.

## Lane target

- Y. Kapil, C. Conde, M. S. Moslehian, M. Singh, and M. Sababheh, *Norm inequalities related to the Heron and Heinz means*, arXiv:1709.09813; Mediterr. J. Math. 14 (2017), Article 213.
- Its “On Zou's questions” paragraph recalls the conjecture but crosses `A` and `B` in the second summand. Direct comparison with Zou's primary PDF establishes that this is a transcription mismatch.
- The target paper gives a negative answer to Zou's separate positive-definite-function question; that same-paper answer is outside the new result.

## Related later paper checked

- F. Gao and X. Ma, *Norm inequalities related to the Heinz means*, J. Inequal. Appl. 2018 (2018), Article 303, DOI 10.1186/s13660-018-1892-7.
- This paper proves related inequalities by contractive-map methods but does not state or supply a full answer to Zou's Conjecture (4.2).
