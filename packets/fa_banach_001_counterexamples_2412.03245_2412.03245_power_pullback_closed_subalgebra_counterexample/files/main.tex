\section*{Source conjecture}

Behera--Maurya--Muthukumar \cite{BMM} define an algebra automorphism to be a
bijective linear multiplicative self-map.  They explicitly allow subalgebras
of $\Hinfty(\D)$ which are not closed.  Their concluding conjecture is:

\begin{quote}
Every algebra automorphism of every subalgebra of $\Hinfty(\D)$ is a
composition operator induced by a disc automorphism.
\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.97\textwidth]{figures/open_problem_crop.png}
\end{center}

The construction below disproves the conjecture even after imposing closedness
and requiring the automorphism to be unital and isometric.

\section*{Idea of the proof}

The map $z\mapsto z^m$ realizes the disc as an $m$-sheeted quotient of itself.
Pullback along this map identifies $\Hinfty(\D)$ isometrically with the closed
subalgebra of functions depending only on $z^m$.  Every disc automorphism acts
on the quotient algebra, so conjugation gives an automorphism of this
subalgebra.  A non-rotational M\"obius map does not lift through the branched
cover $z\mapsto z^m$: its pullback has $m$ simple zeros, whereas an $m$-th
power can only have zeros whose multiplicities are divisible by $m$.

For $a\in\D$, write
\[
 \tau_a(w)=\frac{a-w}{1-\overline a w}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[power-pullback counterexamples]\label{thm:main}
Fix an integer $m\ge2$ and $a\in\D\setminus\{0\}$.  Define
\[
 \A_m=\{g(z^m):g\in\Hinfty(\D)\}.
\]
Then $\A_m$ is a closed unital subalgebra of $\Hinfty(\D)$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:T}
 T_{a,m}\big(g(z^m)\big)=g\big(\tau_a(z^m)\big)
\end{equation}
is a unital isometric algebra automorphism of $\A_m$.  There is no analytic
self-map $\varphi:\D\to\D$ such that
\[
 T_{a,m}f=f\circ\varphi\qquad(f\in\A_m).
\]
In particular, $T_{a,m}$ is not induced by a disc automorphism.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $p_m(z)=z^m$ and define
\[
 J_m:\Hinfty(\D)\longrightarrow\Hinfty(\D),\qquad J_mg=g\circ p_m.
\]
Because $p_m(\D)=\D$,
\[
 \|J_mg\|_\infty
 =\sup_{z\in\D}|g(z^m)|
 =\sup_{w\in\D}|g(w)|
 =\|g\|_\infty.
\]
Thus $J_m$ is an isometric unital algebra monomorphism.  Its range is exactly
$\A_m$, and an isometric range is closed.

Composition by $\tau_a$ is an isometric algebra automorphism of
$\Hinfty(\D)$.  Therefore
\[
 T_{a,m}=J_m C_{\tau_a}J_m^{-1}
\]
is a well-defined unital isometric algebra automorphism of $\A_m$, and the
displayed formula is precisely \eqref{eq:T}.

Suppose, toward a contradiction, that $T_{a,m}=C_\varphi|_{\A_m}$ for some
analytic self-map $\varphi$ of $\D$.  Apply both maps to the element
$u(z)=z^m=J_m(w)$ of $\A_m$.  We obtain
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lift}
 \varphi(z)^m=\tau_a(z^m).
\end{equation}
The zeros of the right side are exactly the $m$ solutions of $z^m=a$.
They are distinct and nonzero.  Since $\tau_a$ has a simple zero at $a$ and
$m z^{m-1}\ne0$ at each of these roots, every zero of
$\tau_a(z^m)$ is simple.

On the other hand, if $\varphi$ has a zero of multiplicity $r\ge1$, then
$\varphi^m$ has a zero of multiplicity $mr$.  Hence every zero multiplicity
on the left side of \eqref{eq:lift} is divisible by $m\ge2$.  This contradicts
the simple zeros on the right side.  No such $\varphi$ exists.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[the elementary nonclosed version]
The literal conjecture already fails on the polynomial subalgebra
$\mathbb C[z]\subset\Hinfty(\D)$: the map $p(z)\mapsto p(z/2)$ is an algebra
automorphism with inverse $p(z)\mapsto p(2z)$, but $z/2$ is not a disc
automorphism.  Theorem \ref{thm:main} shows that nonclosedness or discontinuity
is not the issue.
\end{remark}

\section*{Scope, verification, and novelty status}

The theorem is a direct counterexample to the source conjecture as stated.
It also rules out the natural repairs ``closed unital subalgebra'' and
``continuous/isometric automorphism.''  It does not classify which embedded
copies or quotient-type subalgebras have the extension property.

The proof is purely algebraic and uses only surjectivity of $z\mapsto z^m$,
the standard automorphisms of $\Hinfty$, and zero multiplicities.  Exact-title,
exact-conjecture, arXiv-id-plus-correction, and power-pullback searches were
run in the local parsed arXiv corpus and arXiv-indexed web results through
2026-08-11.  No later correction or prior answer was found.  Novelty confidence
is medium because the argument is elementary.  Human review should focus on
the source's explicit allowance of nonclosed subalgebras and on the conjugacy
formula defining $T_{a,m}$.
