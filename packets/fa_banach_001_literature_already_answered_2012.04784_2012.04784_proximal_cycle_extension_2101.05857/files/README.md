# arXiv:2012.04784 — proximal-mapping extension in later literature

Status: `literature_already_answered (proximal-mapping extension only)`

Original source:

- Salihah Alwadani, Heinz H. Bauschke, Juan Pablo Revalski, and Xianfu
  Wang, *The difference vectors for convex sets and a resolution of the
  geometry conjecture*, arXiv:2012.04784.
- Exact location: concluding future-research paragraph, arXiv PDF page 25.

Answering source:

- Salihah Alwadani, Heinz H. Bauschke, and Xianfu Wang,
  *Attouch--Théra Duality, Generalized Cycles and Gap Vectors*,
  arXiv:2101.05857.
- Exact locations: introduction, PDF pages 1--3; Theorems 4.2--4.4,
  pages 13--15; Theorem 7.2, pages 23--24.

The original paper proposes extending its projector geometry to proximal
mappings.  The later paper carries this out: it develops cycles, gap vectors,
and fixed-point-set translations for cyclic compositions of proximal
mappings; constructs always-existing, unique generalized cycle and gap
vectors; identifies their relationship to classical cycles; and gives a
forward--backward computation scheme.

This is intentionally a scoped resolution.  The original paper separately
asks for an algorithm for its projector gap vector that avoids Seeger's
algorithm.  In the projector specialization, the later algorithm still
requires projection onto `closure(C + Delta)`, precisely the non-explicit
projection at issue in the original paper.  This packet therefore does not
claim that the Seeger-free algorithmic question is answered.
