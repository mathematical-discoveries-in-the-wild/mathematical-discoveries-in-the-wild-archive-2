\section{Original question}

Freeman, Schlumprecht, and Zs\'ak ask in Problem~16, arXiv PDF page~18,
of \emph{Banach spaces for which the space of operators has
$2^{\mathfrak c}$ closed ideals}~\cite{source}:

\begin{quote}
What is the cardinality of the lattice of closed ideals of the space of
operators on Schreier space?
\end{quote}

\section{Separate later answer}

Manoussakis and Pelczar-Barwacz explicitly say in the introduction of
\emph{Small operator ideals on the Schlumprecht and Schreier spaces} that
their finite-order Schreier result solves Problem~16 of the source
paper~\cite{answerpaper}.  Their Theorem~4.4, arXiv PDF page~14, states that
for every $N\geq1$ there is a family $(\mathcal I_A)_{A\subset\mathbb R}$ of
small closed operator ideals on $X[\mathcal S_N]$ satisfying
\[
  \mathcal I_A\subset \mathcal I_B \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\subset B.
\]
Thus there are $2^{\mathfrak c}$ distinct small closed ideals.  This is also
the upper bound for the total number of closed ideals on a separable Banach
space, so the entire lattice has cardinality exactly $2^{\mathfrak c}$.

\section{Scope and textual caveat}

The result answers the classical Schreier-space case $X[\mathcal S_1]$ and,
more strongly, every finite order $X[\mathcal S_N]$.  It does not give a full
lattice classification and does not cover infinite-order Schreier spaces.

The displayed theorem contains an evident one-word typo: after fixing ideals
on the Schreier space $X[\mathcal S_N]$, one sentence says ``on the
Schlumprecht space.''  The opening and final sentences of Theorem~4.4, its
proof, the section heading, the abstract, and the introduction all identify
the space as $X[\mathcal S_N]$.  This packet therefore records the
unambiguous Schreier-space reading and flags the typo explicitly.

This is an exact literature answer, not an original result.  A bounded search
through 9 August 2026 checked the run indexes, both arXiv ids, exact question
wording, and later Schreier-space closed-ideal literature.  The run contains a
different packet using arXiv:2008.12362, but not this Problem~16
identification.
