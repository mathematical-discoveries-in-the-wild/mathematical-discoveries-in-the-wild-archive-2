## Related finite-dimensional context

- Q. Wang, G. Li, L. Qi, and Y. Xu, *New classes of positive semi-definite
  Hankel tensors*, arXiv:1411.2368.
- W. Ding, L. Qi, and Y. Wei, *Inheritance properties and sum-of-squares
  decomposition of Hankel tensors: theory and algorithms*, arXiv:1505.02528.

These papers record finite positive-semidefinite Hankel tensors that are not
strong. They are novelty context only; the packet proves its own base case and
does not use their theorems. The all-degree recursive completion is the new
step under review.
