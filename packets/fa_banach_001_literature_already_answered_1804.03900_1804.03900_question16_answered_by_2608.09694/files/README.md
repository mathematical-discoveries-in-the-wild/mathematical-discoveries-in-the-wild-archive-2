# Literature answer: mean Li–Yorke without distributional chaos

status: literature_already_answered

original question: N. C. Bernardes Jr., A. Bonilla, and A. Peris,
*Mean Li-Yorke chaos in Banach spaces*, arXiv:1804.03900, Question 16,
PDF page 10.

trigger paper: Jian Li, Xinsheng Wang, and Jianjie Zhao,
*Mean Li-Yorke chaos for a sequence of operators on Banach spaces*,
arXiv:2511.06207, introduction.

answer paper: Jian Li and Jie Li,
*Power growth of mean-L-stable operators on Banach spaces*,
arXiv:2608.09694, Proposition 6.1, PDF pages 23–24.

## Identification

Question 16 asks whether a Banach (or Hilbert) space operator can be mean
Li–Yorke chaotic without being distributionally chaotic.  Proposition 6.1 of
arXiv:2608.09694 constructs an operator T_1 on a Banach space X_1 that is
dense mean Li–Yorke chaotic while every pair is distributionally asymptotic.
The authors explicitly state that Proposition 6.1 answers Question 16.

The new paper also proves the Hilbert-space contrast: under its Hilbert-space
theorem, dense mean Li–Yorke chaos forces dense distributional chaos.

This is an exact later-paper resolution, not a new proof produced by the run.
