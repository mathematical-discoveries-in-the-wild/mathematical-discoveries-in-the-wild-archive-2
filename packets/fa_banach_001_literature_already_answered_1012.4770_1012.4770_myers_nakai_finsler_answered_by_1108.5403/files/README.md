# Literature status: Myers--Nakai for Banach--Finsler manifolds

Status: `literature_already_answered`

## Source question

Mar Jimenez-Sevilla and Luis Sanchez-Gonzalez, *On some problems on smooth
approximation and smooth extension of Lipschitz functions on Banach-Finsler
Manifolds*, arXiv:1012.4770.

The Introduction on PDF page 3 asks whether an infinite-dimensional version of
the Myers--Nakai theorem can be obtained for some class of infinite-dimensional
complete Finsler manifolds.

## Later answer

J. A. Jaramillo, Mar Jimenez-Sevilla, and Luis Sanchez-Gonzalez,
*Characterization of a Banach-Finsler manifold in terms of the algebras of
smooth functions*, arXiv:1108.5403, gives the requested version.

Theorem 2.3 on PDF page 8 treats complete, uniformly bumpable (C^k) Finsler
manifolds modeled on (k)-admissible Banach spaces. It characterizes weak
(C^k) Finsler equivalence by isometric algebra isomorphism of the algebras
(C_b^k(M)). Every algebra isomorphism is induced by a weak (C^k) Finsler
isometry, which is a (C^{k-1}) Finsler isometry when (k\ge2).

Corollaries 2.7 and 2.8 on PDF page 11 give explicit infinite-dimensional
classes: uniformly bumpable manifolds modeled on WCG Banach spaces in the
(C^1) case, and complete separable manifolds modeled on spaces with a
Lipschitz (C^k)-smooth bump function.

## Scope

At (k=1) the conclusion is weak (C^1) equivalence, not an unrestricted
smooth Finsler isometry theorem; the sequel explicitly attributes this to the
lack of a general Finsler Myers--Steenrod theorem.

The two `we do not know` signals in arXiv:1012.4770 concern a separate
renorming-uniformity hypothesis in the smooth approximation/extension
machinery. They are not answered by arXiv:1108.5403 and are not claimed solved
in this packet.
