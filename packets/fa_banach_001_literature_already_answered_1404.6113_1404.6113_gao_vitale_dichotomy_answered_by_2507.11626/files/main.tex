\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries The Gao--Vitale intrinsic-volume dichotomy\\is answered negatively}\par
\vspace{0.5em}
{\large Literature-status note for arXiv:1404.6113}\par
\vspace{0.5em}
August 11, 2026
\end{center}

\textbf{Status.} Exact later-paper answer.  This packet records a literature
identification and claims no new mathematical result.

\section*{Source question}
Remark 2.1 on source PDF page 12 of Kabluchko--Zaporozhets~\cite{KZ}
repeats Gao and Vitale's conjecture.  If $K$ is a convex GB-compact set in a
separable Hilbert space and
\[
 m_k(K):=(k+1)\frac{V_{k+1}(K)}{V_k(K)},
\]
then the proposed dichotomy is
\[
 \lim_{k\to\infty}m_k(K)>0
 \qquad\text{or}\qquad
 m_k(K)=O(k^{-1/2}).
\]
The remark verifies the second alternative for the two Sobolev/Lipschitz
examples studied there, but does not settle the universal assertion.

\section*{Exact later answer}
Dospolova--Germanskov--Zaporozhets~\cite{DGZ} restate the identical assertion
as Conjecture 3.5 and explicitly disprove it on their PDF page 9.  The decisive
ingredients are their Theorems 3.1 and 3.4.  Theorem 3.1 says that the order
$\rho(K)$ of the Steiner entire function of a GB-set can attain every value in
$[0,1]$.  Theorem 3.4 proves
\[
 \limsup_{k\to\infty}\frac{\log m_k(K)}{\log k}
 =1-\frac{1}{\rho(K)}.
\]
Choose any realized $\rho(K)>2/3$.  Then
$1-1/\rho(K)>-1/2$, so for every $\varepsilon>0$ the bound
\[
 m_k(K)=O\!\left(k^{,1-1/\rho(K)-\varepsilon}\right)
\]
fails.  Taking $\varepsilon$ small enough that
$1-1/\rho(K)-\varepsilon>-1/2$ rules out $m_k=O(k^{-1/2})$.
At the same time $\rho(K)<1$ may be chosen, in which case the displayed
limsup is negative and monotonicity of $m_k$ forces $m_k\to0$.  Thus neither
branch of the Gao--Vitale dichotomy holds.

\section*{Scope and provenance}
The match is exact: arXiv:2507.11626 names the assertion ``Conjecture 3.5
(Gao--Vitale (2001))'' and explicitly obtains counterexamples by combining its
order-realization and decay theorems (in particular, take $2/3<\rho(K)<1$).
Hence the open-problem signal in arXiv:1404.6113 should be
classified as \emph{literature already answered (negative)}, not as a new
counterexample produced in this run.
