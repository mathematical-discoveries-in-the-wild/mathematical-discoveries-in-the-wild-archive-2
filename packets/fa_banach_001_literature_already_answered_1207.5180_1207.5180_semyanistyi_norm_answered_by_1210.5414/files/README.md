# Semyanistyi weighted norms — literature resolution

This packet identifies arXiv:1210.5414 as the direct answer to Section 5,
item 3 of arXiv:1207.5180. The later paper proves the sharp weighted norm of
the fractional k-plane transform, and ordinary Banach-space duality supplies
the corresponding dual theorem. Its gamma-factor formula converges to the
sharp zero-order norm as alpha tends to zero.
