\section*{Classification}

\textbf{Status: literature already answered.}  The question isolated below was
answered affirmatively by a separate paper of the same authors, posted one
week after the source preprint.  This note records the implication and makes
no claim of a new proof.

\section*{Source question}

Fern\'andez-Polo and Peralta, \emph{On the extension of isometries between the
unit spheres of a C$^*$-algebra and $B(H)$}, arXiv:1701.02916, prove that every
surjective isometry
\[
 f:\Sphere(B(K))\longrightarrow \Sphere(B(H))
\]
extends to a surjective complex-linear or conjugate-linear isometry.  They also
obtain the analogous conclusion for $\ell_\infty$-sums of $B(H)$ spaces.  At
the end of the introduction they identify the next natural question: whether
Tingley's extension problem has a positive answer for Cartan factors and
atomic JBW$^*$-triples.

Here Tingley's problem asks whether a surjective isometry between unit spheres
extends to a surjective real-linear isometry of the ambient Banach spaces.

\section*{Answering paper}

Fern\'andez-Polo and Peralta, \emph{Tingley's problem through the facial
structure of an atomic JBW$^*$-triple}, arXiv:1701.05112, was posted on
18 January 2017, seven days after arXiv:1701.02916.  Its abstract already
announces the required statement, and its main result (Theorem 2.9 in the
published numbering) is:

\begin{quote}
If $E$ and $B$ are atomic JBW$^*$-triples and
$f:\Sphere(E)\to\Sphere(B)$ is a surjective isometry, then there is a unique
real-linear isometry $T:E\to B$ whose restriction to $\Sphere(E)$ is $f$.
\end{quote}

Surjectivity of $T$ follows immediately from
$T(\Sphere(E))=f(\Sphere(E))=\Sphere(B)$.  Thus the result is precisely an
affirmative answer for arbitrary pairs of atomic JBW$^*$-triples.

Every Cartan factor is an atomic JBW$^*$-triple (and an arbitrary atomic
JBW$^*$-triple is an $\ell_\infty$-sum of Cartan factors), so the theorem also
covers the Cartan-factor part of the source question.

\section*{How the later result connects to the source}

The connection is explicit rather than inferred only from terminology:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The answering paper cites arXiv:1701.02916 as the recent $B(H)$ result.
\item Its introduction says that its purpose is to extend that result to
atomic JBW$^*$-triples.
\item Its theorem has exactly the sphere-isometry hypothesis and real-linear
extension conclusion requested by the source question, for the full stated
class.
\end{enumerate}

The later paper was published as \emph{J. Math. Anal. Appl.} \textbf{455}
(2017), 750--760.  Its DOI is \url{10.1016/j.jmaa.2017.06.002}.

\section*{Scope and novelty}

This packet classifies only the source's stated Cartan-factor/atomic-JBW$^*$
question.  It does not classify the unrestricted Tingley problem for arbitrary
Banach spaces, and it does not rebrand the later authors' theorem as a new
result.  The resolution is unusually direct: same authors, a separate paper,
an explicit continuation statement, and a theorem covering the entire source
class.
