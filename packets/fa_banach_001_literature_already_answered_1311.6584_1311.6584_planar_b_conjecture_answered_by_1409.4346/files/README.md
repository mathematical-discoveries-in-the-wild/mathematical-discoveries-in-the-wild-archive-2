# Planar (B)-conjecture for even log-concave measures

Status: `literature_already_answered (full planar case)`

Source: Amir Livne Bar-on, *The (B) conjecture for uniform measures in the
plane*, arXiv:1311.6584 (2013), “Examples and open questions,” PDF page 2.

Later answer: Christos Saroglou, *More on logarithmic sums of convex bodies*,
arXiv:1409.4346 (2014; Mathematika 2016), Corollary 3.3 on PDF page 5.

## Identification

The source says that the (B)-conjecture for general centrally symmetric
log-concave measures “is not settled yet, even in two dimensions.”  Saroglou
later proves in Corollary 3.3 that the log-Brunn–Minkowski inequality and the
(B)-conjecture hold in the plane for every even log-concave density.  This is
exactly the full-dimensional planar question.  Lower-dimensional centrally
symmetric log-concave measures reduce to their linear support and the same
dimension-one argument.

The supporting paper explicitly knows the relationship: in its introduction
it cites arXiv:1311.6584 as reference `[Li]`, states that Livne Bar-on had
independently proved the planar uniform-measure case, and announces the
extension to all even log-concave densities.

## Scope

This closes the planar part of the source's open statement.  It does not solve
the general higher-dimensional (B)-conjecture, which remains open for arbitrary
even log-concave measures.  The result is therefore recorded as a full answer
to the explicit “even in two dimensions” subproblem, not as a solution in all
dimensions.
