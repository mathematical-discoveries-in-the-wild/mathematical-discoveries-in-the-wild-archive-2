# Bounded novelty search

Search date: 11 August 2026.

## Claim searched

An explicit positive-definite absolutely homogeneous gauge with nonconvex
unit ball whose original Thy polarization satisfies CSB and axiom (An 11), in
particular the gauge

\[
q(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)=r(1+\tfrac1{10}\cos4\theta).
\]

## Sources and queries checked

- The source paper, Volker W. Thurey, *Angles and Polar Coordinates In Real
  Normed Spaces*, arXiv:0902.2731:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.2731
- Exact-title searches combined with `pseudonormed`, `non-convex unit ball`,
  `CSB`, `Thy angle`, `Problem 1`, and `Problem 2`.
- Exact-formula searches combining `1 + cos(4 theta)` with `Thy angle`,
  `pseudonorm`, and `CSB`.
- Volker W. Thurey, *Angles and a Classification of Normed Spaces*,
  arXiv:1207.0074 / Ann. Funct. Anal. 4 (2013), 114-137:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.0074
  The complete local arXiv source was inspected, especially its sections on
  positive-definite balancedly weighted spaces, concave corners, and open
  conjectures. It generalizes the angle family and repeats obstruction
  phenomena, but no example resolving the 2009 questions was located.
- Later works returned by citation/keyword search, including the 2017 paper
  on Thy angles in n-normed spaces and later surveys/papers citing the 2009
  construction. Their available abstracts and indexed text did not advertise
  a nonconvex positive-definite example satisfying the original Thy CSB
  inequality or (An 11).

## Outcome

No later resolution of Problems 1 and 2, no occurrence of the displayed
fourfold gauge, and no matching Bernstein certificate were located in this
bounded search. This supports treating the packet as a candidate new full
result. It is not a proof of novelty or priority, and priority is not
asserted.
