# Convex Stein domains are strong homological epimorphisms

Status: candidate_substantial_partial_likely_valid

This packet resolves the concrete unit-ball case singled out after Question
6.1 of arXiv:1908.02117 and proves a stronger theorem:

    O(C^n) -> O(U)

is a strong homological epimorphism for every convex domain U in C^n.

The proof base-changes the finite free bimodule Koszul resolution of the
diagonal and constructs an explicit continuous radial contracting homotopy
on the resulting holomorphic Koszul complex.

The full question for arbitrary Stein open embeddings and the separate
questions on singular C-infinity differentiable spaces remain open.
