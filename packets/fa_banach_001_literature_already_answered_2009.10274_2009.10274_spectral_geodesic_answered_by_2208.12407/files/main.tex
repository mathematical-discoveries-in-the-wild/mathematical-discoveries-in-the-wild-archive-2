\section*{Original signal and exact question}

Let $\Pcone$ be the cone of bounded strictly positive operators on a Hilbert
space and let $\#$ denote the Kubo--Ando geometric mean.  Sejong Kim,
\emph{Operator inequalities and gyrolines of the weighted geometric means},
arXiv:2009.10274 \cite{Kim}, defines
\[
 A\natmean_t B=(A^{-1}\#B)^tA(A^{-1}\#B)^t,
 \qquad
 d(A,B)=2\|\log(A^{-1}\#B)\|.
\]
On source-PDF page 8, Remark 2(2), the paper calls the following a
``challengeable problem'': prove, for all $s,t\in\mathbb R$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:q}
 d(A\natmean_sB,A\natmean_tB)=|s-t|d(A,B).
\end{equation}
The published article phrases the parameters over $\mathbb R$; in particular
this contains the originally extracted $s,t\in[0,1]$ question.

\section*{Decisive later answer}

Luyining Gan and Sejong Kim, \emph{Revisit on spectral geometric mean},
arXiv:2208.12407 \cite{GK}, explicitly announce the geodesic property in the
abstract and introduction.  Their Theorem 4.1 on source-PDF page 6 states
exactly \eqref{eq:q}, for every $s,t\in\mathbb R$, and proves it using the
composition law for weighted spectral means.  This is an exact
author-acknowledged answer: the supporting paper includes the author of the
question, cites the 2021 paper, and proves the identical formula in a section
devoted to the geodesic property.

\section*{Independent algebraic verification}

There is also a short direct check.  Put
\[
 C=A^{-1}\#B,\qquad X_u=A\natmean_uB=C^uAC^u.
\]
The geometric mean $P\#Q$ is the unique positive solution $Z$ of
$ZP^{-1}Z=Q$.  With $P=X_s^{-1}$ and $Q=X_t$, the positive operator
$Z=C^{t-s}$ satisfies
\[
 ZP^{-1}Z=C^{t-s}X_sC^{t-s}
 =C^{t-s}C^sAC^sC^{t-s}=C^tAC^t=X_t.
\]
Therefore
\[
 X_s^{-1}\#X_t=C^{t-s}.
\]
Functional calculus now gives
\[
 d(X_s,X_t)=2\|\log C^{t-s}\|
 =2|t-s|\|\log C\|=|t-s|d(A,B),
\]
which verifies both the later theorem and its exact fit to the source question.
