# Weighted spectral-geometric path: answered by arXiv:2208.12407

Status: `literature_already_answered_affirmative`.

Sejong Kim, arXiv:2009.10274 (published in *Mathematical Inequalities &
Applications* 24 (2021), 491--514), asks on source-PDF page 8 whether the
weighted spectral-geometric path has constant speed for the semimetric

```text
d(A,B) = 2 || log(A^{-1} # B) ||.
```

Luyining Gan and Sejong Kim, arXiv:2208.12407, prove the exact identity in
Theorem 4.1 on source-PDF page 6:

```text
d(A natural_s B, A natural_t B) = |s-t| d(A,B),  s,t in R.
```

The answer is exact, affirmative, and author-acknowledged: the later paper
includes the original author, cites the original article, and devotes Section
4 to the requested geodesic property. The packet also records a one-line
independent Riccati verification. This is a literature retrieval result, not a
new discovery of the run.
