# Stadium spectral effect algebra counterexample

Status: likely-valid full negative resolutions of Questions 1 and 5 in arXiv:1811.12407, with human review recommended.

## Result

Let

\[
K=([-1,1]\times\{0\})+D,
\]

where \(D\) is the Euclidean unit disk, and let \(E(K)\) be the effect algebra of affine functions \(K\to[0,1]\). Its support norm is

\[
\|(z_1,z_2)\|_*=|z_1|+\sqrt{z_1^2+z_2^2},
\]

which is strictly convex. This yields an explicit two-element-context spectral decomposition of every effect.

The atomic sharp effect \(p(x_1,x_2)=(1+x_2)/2\) has

\[
\widehat p=[-1,1]\times\{1\},
\]

so Question 5 has a negative answer. The endpoint \((1,1)\) is extreme but non-exposed, so it cannot be the singleton face of any atomic effect; this answers Question 1 negatively.

## Scope and novelty

The construction does not address Questions 2--4 or 6. A bounded search found no explicit publication of this stadium counterexample or these two answers. Closely related later work studies centrally symmetric state spaces and other notions of spectrality, so novelty confidence is moderate to low.
