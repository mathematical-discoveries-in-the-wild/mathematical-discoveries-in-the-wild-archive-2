# Hyperfinite semigroup dilation: structural reduction

Status: `candidate_partial_result_likely_valid`.

This packet addresses Remark 3.10 of Cédric Arhancet, *Dilations of
semigroups on von Neumann algebras and noncommutative Lp-spaces*
(arXiv:1603.04901).

Main unconditional results:

- Pointwise hyper-factorizability for positive times, together with weak*
  continuity at zero, forces the base von Neumann algebra to be
  semidiscrete; with separable predual it is hyperfinite.
- Every finite collection of nonnegative rational times admits one exact
  simultaneous hyperfinite dilation.

Upgrade theorem:

- A compatible tower of hyperfinite discrete dilations along the factorial
  meshes `1/n!` has a hyperfinite inductive limit, and its rational action
  extends to the continuous real action required by the conjecture.

The unrestricted construction of one infinite compatible tower remains
open. Eight focused attempts are recorded in the attempt note.
