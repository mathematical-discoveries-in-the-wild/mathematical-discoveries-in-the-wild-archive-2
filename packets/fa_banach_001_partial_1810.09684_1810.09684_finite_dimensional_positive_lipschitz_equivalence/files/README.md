# Finite-dimensional positive-to-isotone interpolation theorem

Source: Ralph Chill, Alberto Fiorenza, and Sebastian Król,
*Interpolation of nonlinear positive or order preserving operators on Banach
lattices*, arXiv:1810.09684 (2018), Remark 4.9(c).

Status: candidate substantial partial result, likely valid.

## Result

The open implication

```text
exact for positive linear operators
    => exact for order-preserving Lipschitz operators
```

holds for every finite-dimensional compatible Banach-lattice couple, even
when the Lipschitz map has a proper solid lattice domain.

The stronger pairwise statement says that for every two domain points
`f,h`, one can find a positive admissible linear endpoint contraction `T`
such that

```text
T(f-h) = S(f)-S(h).
```

The construction smooths `S` inside the order box generated by `f,h`, then
averages its positive derivative along a shrunken segment.

## Scope

The arbitrary infinite-dimensional implication remains open.  The packet
identifies the missing positive-extension/band-gluing step and the lack of
sum-topology continuity that prevents a routine finite-band limit.

## Human review recommendation

Accept as a substantial partial theorem.  Check the mollification argument
and finite-dimensional compactness passage; do not mark the source's general
problem solved.
