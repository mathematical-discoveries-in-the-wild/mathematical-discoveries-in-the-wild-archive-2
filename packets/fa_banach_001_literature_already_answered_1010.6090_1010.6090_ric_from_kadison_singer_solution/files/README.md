# Restricted invertibility conjecture closed by Kadison--Singer

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Section 3.2 of arXiv:1010.6090 states the restricted invertibility
conjecture (RIC) and explicitly records the known implication

`positive Kadison--Singer => RIC`.

Marcus, Spielman, and Srivastava (arXiv:1306.3969) subsequently proved two
theorems known to imply a positive solution of Kadison--Singer. Their
introduction also explicitly lists the Bourgain--Tzafriri conjecture among
the equivalent formulations. Thus the displayed RIC is a positive theorem,
not a fresh open target.

The other extraction signal, the endpoint uncertainty in Remark 3, is
answered by Theorem 2.5 later in arXiv:1010.6090 itself and is therefore a
same-paper false positive.

The adjacent stronger B-RIC for arbitrary Schauder bases is not classified
here.
