# Literature answer for arXiv:2304.07251

The 2023 source leaves open the full strong completeness of `L^p(T)` for
arbitrary Cauchy nets, except for the known cases `p=2` and `p=infinity`.

Azouzi's later paper arXiv:2512.11809 answers the question completely.
Theorem 19 equates strong completeness of `L^1(T)` and `L^p(T)` for every
`1<p<infinity`; Theorem 20 concludes that `L^p(T)` is strongly complete for
every `p` in `[1,infinity]` under the same conditional Riesz triple
hypotheses.
