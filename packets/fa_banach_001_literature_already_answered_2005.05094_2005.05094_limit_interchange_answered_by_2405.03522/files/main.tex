\fbox{\parbox{0.9\textwidth}{
\textbf{Status: literature already answered (fully, negatively).}
Problem~1 of arXiv:2005.05094 asks whether one may interchange the
$T\to\infty$ and $\sigma\to0^+$ limits in the mean counting function for
every Dirichlet series in the relevant Nevanlinna class.  Theorems~1.6 and
1.7 of arXiv:2405.03522 give two counterexamples: equality can fail even when
the interchanged limit exists, and the interchanged limit itself can fail to
exist.
}}
\end{center}

\section{The source question}

O.~F.~Brevig and K.-M.~Perfekt, \emph{A mean counting function for Dirichlet
series and compact composition operators}, arXiv:2005.05094, define for a
Dirichlet series $f$ the mean counting function by first restricting the
zeros to $\operatorname{Re}s>\sigma$, averaging in the vertical direction,
and then taking $\sigma\to0^+$.  Section~8.1 asks whether the order of these
limits can always be reversed.  In the notation of the paper:

\begin{question}
Let $f\in\mathcal N_u$.  Is it true that
\[
 M_f(w)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{\pi}{T}
 \sum_{\substack{s\in f^{-1}(\{w\})\\ |\operatorname{Im}s|<T\\
                         0<\operatorname{Re}s<\infty}}
 \operatorname{Re}s
\]
for every $w\ne f(+\infty)$?
\end{question}

Writing the expression before the $T$-limit as $N_f(w,T)$, the question is
whether
\[
 \lim_{T\to\infty}N_f(w,T)=M_f(w)
\]
always holds.

\section{The later resolution}

O.~F.~Brevig, A.~Kouroupis, and K.-M.~Perfekt, \emph{Almost periodicity and
boundary values of Dirichlet series}, arXiv:2405.03522, explicitly identifies
the question above as ``[12, Problem 1]'' and proves:

\begin{laterthm}
For every $0<\varepsilon<1$ there is a Dirichlet series
$f:\mathbb C_0\to\mathbb D$, with $f(+\infty)=0$, such that
\[
 \lim_{T\to\infty}N_f(\xi,T)=\log\frac1{|\xi|}
\]
for quasi-every $\xi\in\mathbb D\setminus\{0\}$, whereas
\[
 M_f(\xi)\leq \log\frac1{|\xi|}
 -(1-\varepsilon)\frac{1-|\xi|^2}{2}
 \qquad(\xi\ne0).
\]
Thus the proposed equality fails wherever the first display holds.

Moreover, for every $0<\varepsilon<1$ there is a Dirichlet series
$f:\mathbb C_0\to\mathbb D$ for which
$\lim_{T\to\infty}N_f(\xi,T)$ fails to exist for quasi-every $\xi$ satisfying
$\varepsilon<|\xi|<1$.
\end{laterthm}

These are Theorems~1.6 and 1.7 of the later paper.  Immediately after their
statements, the authors write that those two theorems resolve the earlier
Problem~1.

\section{Why this answers the exact question}

The later examples are bounded Dirichlet series mapping the right half-plane
to the unit disc, hence lie within the source paper's class $\mathcal N_u$.
The quantity $N_f(\xi,T)$ is exactly the uncut finite-height sum appearing in
the source problem.  Theorem~1.6 refutes the universal equality while retaining
existence of the $T$-limit; Theorem~1.7 gives the stronger failure of existence.
Therefore the answer to Problem~1 is unambiguously \emph{no}.

target also produced a separate new partial-result packet for the source
paper's weighted boundary-embedding problem.
