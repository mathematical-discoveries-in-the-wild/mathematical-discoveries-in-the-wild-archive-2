# Literature answer: 2005.05094 Problem 1 via 2405.03522

## Status

`literature_already_answered` — full negative answer.

Brevig and Perfekt ask whether the two limits defining their mean counting
function may be interchanged. Brevig, Kouroupis, and Perfekt later construct
both a Dirichlet series for which the interchanged limit exists but differs
strictly from the mean counting function and another for which the
interchanged limit fails to exist quasi-everywhere on a prescribed annulus.
Their paper explicitly states that Theorems 1.6 and 1.7 resolve the earlier
Problem 1.
