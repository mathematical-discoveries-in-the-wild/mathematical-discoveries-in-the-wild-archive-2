\section*{Source question}

In \emph{Dimension-free discretizations of the uniform norm by small
product sets}, Becker--Klein--Slote--Volberg--Zhang ask (Question 2,
PDF page 9):
\begin{quote}
What is the best constant for the cyclic-group Bohnenblust--Hille
inequality, $\mathrm{BH}_{\Omega_K}^{\le d}$? As a starting point, is
$\mathrm{BH}_{\Omega_K}^{\le d}$ subexponential in $d$?
\end{quote}
Their constant concerns functions on $\Omega_K^N$ whose Fourier spectrum
has total degree at most $d$.

\section*{Answer in later literature}

Pellegrino--Raposo answer this affirmatively in arXiv:2608.05366v1.
On PDF page 5 they prove Theorem A for the larger class
of functions whose Fourier characters involve at most $d$ active
coordinates.  If $\BHint_{d,q}$ is its optimal constant, then for every
fixed $q\ge2$,
\[
 \BHint_{d,q}\le
 \exp\left(c_q\sqrt{d\log d}
 +O_q\left(\sqrt{\frac d{\log d}}\log\log d\right)\right).
\]

For a Fourier index $\alpha\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}^N$, its interaction
order satisfies
\[
 |\{j:\alpha_j\ne0\}|\le |\alpha|.
\]
Thus the total-degree-$d$ class in the source is contained in the
interaction-order-$d$ class.  Consequently
\[
 \BHdeg_{d,q}\le\BHint_{d,q}.
\]
This gives Corollary 1.1 of the later paper, explicitly titled
``Answer to the question of Becker et al.'':
\[
 \BHdeg_{d,q}\le
 \exp\left(c_q\sqrt{d\log d}
 +O_q\left(\sqrt{\frac d{\log d}}\log\log d\right)\right)
 =\exp(o(d)).
\]
Under the later paper's notation,
$\BHdeg_{d,q}=\mathrm{BH}_{\Omega_q}^{\le d}$ from the source.  Hence
the requested subexponentiality holds for every fixed finite cyclic
group, including the intermediate cases $q\ge3$.

\section*{Scope}

This is a full affirmative answer to the ``starting point'' in source
Question 2.  It does not determine the optimal asymptotic constant, nor
does it answer the source's separate Question 1 about optimal
$K$-dependence of the uniform-norm discretization constant.
