## Identification

Mao explicitly knew he was addressing Chen's question. His Theorem 2.2(ii)
removes the source table's epsilon gap for the ergodic birth-death Nash
criterion, and Theorem 3.1(ii) gives the corresponding half-line diffusion
criterion. Mao describes the answer as complete for birth-death processes
and partial in the paper's broader Markov-process scope.

Chen's later papers answer a weakened reading of Open Question 4: they give
Banach/Orlicz variational upper formulas, exact finiteness criteria, and
explicit bounds for Nash and logarithmic-Sobolev constants. They do not state
the exact sup-inf/inf-sup equality asked for in Theorem 4.1(2) form.
