# Product-form counterexample answers Q3 and Q5

Status: literature-implied negative answers.

The questions are Q3 and Q5 in Section 10 (PDF page 38) of
arXiv:1607.08024.  Example 8.4 (PDF page 33) of arXiv:2209.05616 gives the
counterexample `(N,D)=(24,{0,1,16,17})`.  The accompanying note explains why
the same example meets the extra no-overlap hypothesis in Q5.
