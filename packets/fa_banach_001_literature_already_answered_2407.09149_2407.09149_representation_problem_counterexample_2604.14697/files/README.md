# Banach-lattice-algebra representation problem: negative answer

Result type: `literature_already_answered`

Status: exact later-paper answer; not a new mathematical result of this run.

## Original question

- David Muñoz-Lahoz, “Band projections and order idempotents in Banach
  lattice algebras,” arXiv:2407.09149, Question 1.1 on PDF page 2.
- It asks whether every Banach lattice algebra is isometrically isomorphic to
  a closed subalgebra and sublattice of some space of regular operators.

## Decisive later answer

- David Muñoz-Lahoz, “Wickstead's conjecture on positive projections and
  non-representable Banach lattice algebras,” arXiv:2604.14697v3 (10 August
  2026).
- The abstract announces two-dimensional non-representable Banach lattice
  algebras. The introduction on page 2 explicitly says this answers
  Wickstead's Question 5.3 in the negative.
- Proposition 4.3 gives the non-representability criterion. Example 4.4 gives
  a family on R²; for instance the cone parameter β = -2/3 yields a unital
  Banach lattice algebra with no faithful representation on regular operators
  over any Dedekind complete vector lattice, even if the representation need
  not be unital.
