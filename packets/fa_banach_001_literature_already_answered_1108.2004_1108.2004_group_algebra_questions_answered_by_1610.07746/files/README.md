# Group-algebra questions answered in later literature

Original source: S. Beiser and S. Waldmann, *Fréchet algebraic deformation
quantization of the Poincaré disk*, arXiv:1108.2004v3.

Later answer: M. Cahen, S. Gutt, and S. Waldmann, *Nuclear Group Algebras
for Finitely Generated Groups*, arXiv:1610.07746.

Status: literature already answered; verified against the primary later
paper.

## Result

Proposition 5.2 of the later paper proves that the recursive group-algebra
completion from the original source is exactly a weighted `ell^1`
factorial-decay algebra, with equality of locally convex topologies.  In the
original rescaled basis, this is precisely the requested subfactorial-growth
description.

The later paper also states that the fixed-parameter algebra generally
depends on the word length, while a projective limit over all decay
parameters is independent of the finite generating set.  Its exact
coproduct norm identity leads to a Fréchet-Hopf star algebra on that
projective limit.
