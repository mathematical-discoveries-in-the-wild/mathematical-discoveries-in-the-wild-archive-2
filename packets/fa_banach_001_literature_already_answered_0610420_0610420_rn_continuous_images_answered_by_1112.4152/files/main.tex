\section*{Source problem}
On source PDF page 4, Haydon writes \cite{Haydon}:
\begin{quote}
``The situation is closely related to the open problem of whether every continuous image of a Radon--Nikod\'ym compact is again a Radon--Nikod\'ym compact.''
\end{quote}
A compact space is Radon--Nikod\'ym (RN) when it is fragmented by a lower semicontinuous metric; equivalently, it is homeomorphic to a weak-star compact subset of the dual of a Banach space with the Radon--Nikod\'ym property.

\section*{Explicit later answer}
The abstract of Avil\'es and Koszmider says that they construct such a counterexample, ``solving the problem posed in the 80ties by Isaac Namioka'' \cite{AK}.  Their introduction restates the precise question
\begin{quote}
``Is the class of RN compact spaces closed under continuous images?''
\end{quote}
and Theorem 1.1, on supporting PDF page 2, proves:
\begin{quote}
There exists a continuous surjection $\pi:L_0\to L_1$ such that $L_0$ is a zero-dimensional RN compact space but $L_1$ is not RN compact.
\end{quote}
This is a full negative answer, with the additional strength that the domain is zero-dimensional.

\section*{Provenance and scope}
The supporting authors do not cite Haydon's paper, but they explicitly state that they are solving the same named general problem and trace it to Namioka.  This is therefore an explicit literature answer rather than an agent-generated counterexample.

The answer does \emph{not} settle the separate question in the next paragraph of Haydon's paper: whether $C(K)$ is LUR-renormable for every descriptive compact $K$, equivalently whether a Banach space whose dual norm is weak-star LUR must admit an equivalent LUR norm.  That broader renorming question remains outside this packet.
