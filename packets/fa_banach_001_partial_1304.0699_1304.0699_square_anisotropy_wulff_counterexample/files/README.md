# Square-anisotropy Wulff counterexample

Candidate partial result for the open minimizer-classification problem in
arXiv:1304.0699.

For every `0<s<1`, the square `Q=[-1,1]^2` is not a fixed-area minimizer of
`P_s(.,Q)`: its anisotropic nonlocal curvature strictly increases along a side
from its midpoint toward a corner. Affine covariance extends this to every
origin-symmetric parallelogram. An exact covariogram/layer-cake calculation
also proves that the square is nevertheless the unique minimizer among all
fixed-area axis-aligned rectangles.
