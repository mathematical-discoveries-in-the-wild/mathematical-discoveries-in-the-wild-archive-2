## Outcome

The published paper retains the statement as Conjecture 5.8.  Searches found
the source and later papers citing it, but no proof, disproof, or matching
boundary-trace counterexample.  The interval argument appears absent from the
bounded search.

The result should still receive independent bibliographic review.  The novelty
claim is deliberately limited to the sources and indexes searched.
