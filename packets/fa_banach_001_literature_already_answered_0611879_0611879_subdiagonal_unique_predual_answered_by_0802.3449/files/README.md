## Identification

Closing Remark 2 on PDF page 21 of arXiv:math/0611879 asks whether
subdiagonal algebras have unique preduals.

Ueda answers this exactly. The introduction of arXiv:0802.3449 explicitly
calls its result an affirmative answer to Godefroy's question as stated in the
Blecher-Labuschagne survey. Theorem 3.1, on PDF page 4, proves that for every
finite maximal subdiagonal algebra `A=H^infinity(M,tau)`, the canonical quotient
`M_*/A_perp` is the unique predual of `A`.

## Scope

This closes only the survey's unique-predual question in its finite tracial
subdiagonal setting. It does not answer the neighboring questions about type
III extensions, unique completely contractive extensions characterizing
maximal subdiagonality, noncommutative Helson-Szego theory, or isometries of
noncommutative Hardy spaces.
