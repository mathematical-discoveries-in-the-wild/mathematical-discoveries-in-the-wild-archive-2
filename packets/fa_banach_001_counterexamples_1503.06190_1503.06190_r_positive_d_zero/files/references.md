# References and novelty audit

## Primary source

- A. G. Aksoy and G. Lewicki, *Bernstein's Lethargy Theorem in Fréchet
  Spaces*, arXiv:1503.06190 (2015). Definitions of `rho_n`, `d_{n,V}`, and
  `d_V` appear before Lemma 2.3; `R(V)`, the known implication, and the open
  question appear in Lemma 2.22 and Remark 2.23 on source PDF page 16.

## Bounded novelty search

On 11 August 2026, the run's result/attempt indexes were searched for the
arXiv identifier and the invariants `d_V` and `R(V)`. Exact web searches were
also made for:

- `"d_V > 0" "R(V) > 0" Bernstein lethargy`
- `"d_{mathcal V}" "R(mathcal V)" Bernstein`
- `"R(V)>0" "d_V" Fréchet spaces`
- `"Remark 2.23" Bernstein lethargy Frechet`

These returned the source paper, its journal version, and unrelated material,
but no later proof or counterexample to Remark 2.23 was located. This is a
bounded search, not a claim of priority. A subject expert should run a broader
MathSciNet/zbMATH/citation search before dissemination.
