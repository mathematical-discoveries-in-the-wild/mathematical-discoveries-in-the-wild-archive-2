# Geometric-scattering diffeomorphism stability: later answer

The 2018 workshop paper arXiv:1812.06968 postpones a Lipschitz-type
diffeomorphism-stability theorem for its full geometric scattering network.
The authors' expanded paper arXiv:1905.10448 proves that theorem for
bandlimited signals, including a non-windowed version, and also obtains an
unbandlimited finite-width result under additional geometric assumptions.

The answer is qualified: the later paper does not remove bandlimiting for a
general infinite-width network and does not settle comparison across two
merely diffeomorphic manifolds.
