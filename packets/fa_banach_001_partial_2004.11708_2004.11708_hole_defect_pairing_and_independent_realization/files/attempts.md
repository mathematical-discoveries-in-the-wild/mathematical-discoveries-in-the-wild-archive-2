# Upgrade attempts

1. **Resolvent invariance reformulation.** Observed that for `lambda in rho(T)`, failure of surjectivity of the restriction is equivalent to failure of invariance of `M` under `(T-lambda)^{-1}`. This recovers the all-or-none phenomenon but is too tautological to promote alone.

2. **Canonical exact-sequence upgrade.** Passed simultaneously to the restriction and quotient. Constructed the explicit mutually inverse maps
   `ker(T_quotient-lambda) -> M/(T|M-lambda)M` and back using `T-lambda` and its inverse. This gives an exact pointwise defect pairing without a complemented-subspace assumption.

3. **Componentwise multiplicity upgrade.** Used that every restricted pencil `T|M-lambda` is bounded below for `lambda in rho(T)`, hence upper semi-Fredholm. Local constancy of the extended semi-Fredholm index makes the finite defect, or infinitude, constant on a resolvent component. This strengthens spectral all-or-none to multiplicity all-or-none.

4. **Independent finite pattern realization.** Combined bilateral shifts on disjoint spectral circles with their unilateral invariant halves. A direct-sum model realizes each subset of holes independently.

5. **One fixed operator and arbitrary multiplicities.** Replaced finitely many shift blocks by countably many copies per circle. A full copy keeps each boundary circle in every restriction, while any chosen number of unilateral halves prescribes the defect `d_j in N_0 union {infinity}`. The ambient operator is fixed while the invariant subspace varies.

6. **Nonnormal upgrade.** Applied a commuting `2 x 2` Jordan extension `[[B,I],[0,B]]`. It preserves the ambient spectrum and the selected restrictions but is manifestly nonnormal, moving the realization outside the normal case already treated by Conway–Olin.

7. **Arbitrary-boundary upgrade attempt.** Considered replacing circles by Jordan boundaries and unilateral shifts by Hardy/Smirnov multiplication models. This likely works under regularity hypotheses, but it would require boundary-space machinery and careful spectral verification; it was not needed for the sharp finite independence result and was not promoted.

8. **Literature-overlap audit.** The source and older Dowson/Scroggs results contain all-or-none and residual-spectrum conclusions. Barnes (2007) develops restriction/quotient Fredholm relations and an invertible shift/backward-shift triangular example. Conway–Olin treat the normal-Hilbert classification. Bounded exact-phrase and subject searches through 2026-08-11 did not locate the precise canonical pairing together with a single fixed nonnormal simultaneous multiplicity realization. Novelty is therefore stated cautiously as a candidate partial synthesis.
