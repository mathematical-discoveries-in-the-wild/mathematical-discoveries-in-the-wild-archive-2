# Column-row question answered by Hartz

Status: `literature_already_answered` (full answer, optimal constant).

Source: Raphaël Clouâtre and Michael Hartz, *Multipliers and operator space
structure of weak product spaces*, arXiv:1909.12883v2 (2022).

Supporting answer: Michael Hartz, *Every complete Pick space satisfies the
column-row property*, arXiv:2005.09614v1 (2020).

On page 4 of the source paper, after defining the column-row property, the
authors ask whether every normalized complete Nevanlinna--Pick space has this
property with some finite constant.  Hartz's Theorem 1.2 (page 3) proves that
every normalized complete Pick space has the property with constant 1.  This
is the optimal possible constant and hence fully answers the question.

The supporting paper explicitly knows the connection: it cites the source
paper and, in Theorem 1.4 (page 4), combines its main theorem with the source's
Corollary 1.3 to prove the global equality
\(\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H\odot\mathcal H)=
\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H)\), including equality of norms.  Thus it also
closes source Question 1.1 throughout the normalized complete Pick class.

No part of the stated finite-constant question remains open in that class.
The much broader classification of reproducing-kernel Hilbert spaces or
operator spaces having a column-row property is a different question.
