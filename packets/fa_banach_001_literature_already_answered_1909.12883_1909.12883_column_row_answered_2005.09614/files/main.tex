\begin{center}
  {\Large\bfseries Literature Status: the Column--Row Question for Complete Pick Spaces}\par
  \vspace{0.45em}
  {Run \texttt{fa\_banach\_001}, lane 3 --- 9 August 2026}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.35em}

\textbf{Status.} \texttt{literature\_already\_answered} (complete answer,
with optimal constant).

\textbf{Source question.} In Clou\^atre--Hartz,
arXiv:1909.12883v2, page 4, immediately after the definition of the
column--row property, the authors state:
\begin{quote}
It is an open question whether every normalized complete
Nevanlinna--Pick space satisfies the column--row property with a finite
constant.
\end{quote}
The same paper's Question 1.1 (page 2) asks whether
\[
  \operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H\odot\mathcal H)
  =\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H).
\]
Its Corollary 1.3 shows that the column--row property implies this equality.

\textbf{Answer.} Hartz, arXiv:2005.09614v1, Theorem 1.2 (page 3), proves:
\begin{quote}
Every normalized complete Pick space satisfies the column--row property
with constant $1$.
\end{quote}
This is stronger than the requested existence of a finite constant and is
optimal, since the definition requires the constant to be at least one.
Hartz's Theorem 1.4 (page 4) then explicitly combines Theorem 1.2 with
Clou\^atre--Hartz, Corollary 1.3, to obtain
\[
  \operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H\odot\mathcal H)
  =\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H),
  \qquad
  \|\varphi\|_{\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H\odot\mathcal H)}
  =\|\varphi\|_{\operatorname{Mult}(\mathcal H)}.
\]
Thus both the finite-constant question and Question 1.1 are completely answered
for normalized complete Pick spaces.

\textbf{Provenance and scope.} This is an explicit literature answer, not a
new result of the run.  The supporting paper cites the source paper and names
its Corollary 1.3 in deriving Theorem 1.4.  No portion of the normalized
complete Pick case remains open.  Questions classifying more general
reproducing-kernel Hilbert spaces or arbitrary operator spaces with the
column--row property lie outside the source question.
