# Canonical core, hull, and duality for non-even Orlicz cones

Status: **candidate major partial, likely valid; human review requested**.

Source: Thomas Ruf, *Non-Separably Valued Orlicz Spaces I*, arXiv:2204.12282v2 (2023), open and follow-up questions on PDF page 3.

## Result

For the Orlicz cone `C={u:p_phi(u)<infinity}` generated by a non-even convex Orlicz integrand:

- its unique maximal vector subspace is exactly the ordinary even Orlicz space generated by
  `phi_bi(omega,x)=max(phi(omega,x),phi(omega,-x))`;
- the two natural norms on this core agree within the optimal universal factor `2`;
- its algebraic hull `C-C` is exactly the Luxemburg domain of the selection-free function-space infimal convolution
  `J(u)=inf_v[I_phi(v)+I_phi(v-u)]`;
- the Grothendieck norm `r(u)=inf_{u=v-w}[p_phi(v)+p_phi(w)]` satisfies
  `p_J<=r<=2p_J`, again with optimal factor `2`;
- every bounded additive positively homogeneous functional on the cone extends uniquely and isometrically to the Grothendieck hull;
- `J*(ell)=I_phi*(ell)+I_phi*(-ell)`, and an attained optimal decomposition gives an exact forward/backward subdifferential split;
- for finite-dimensional range and a normal integrand on a complete sigma-finite measure space, the hull is the ordinary Banach Orlicz space generated by the pointwise infimal convolution
  `psi(omega,x)=min_y[phi(omega,y)+phi(omega,y-x)]`.

The broad program in the source—full nonseparable structural theory and a systematic calculus directly on locally convex cones—remains open.
