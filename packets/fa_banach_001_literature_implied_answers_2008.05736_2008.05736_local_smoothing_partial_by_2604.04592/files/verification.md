## Supporting preprint

- arXiv:2604.04592, PDF page 2: the general `W^{2,1}` approximation
  theorem is stated to remain unknown.
- PDF page 14, Assumption 5.1: finite conforming rectangular partition,
  piecewise quadratic and `C^1`-compatible map, homeomorphism, uniform
  positive Jacobian, and global lower bi-Lipschitz bound.
- PDF pages 17--20, Theorem 5.5: `W^{2,1}` and uniform-gradient
  approximation by injective `C^1` maps with positive Jacobian, smooth
  away from tiny neighborhoods of finitely many edge endpoints.
- Section 6, Assumption 6.1 and Theorem 6.2: global smooth approximation
  is conditional on an additional localized completion assertion.

## Scope conclusion

The later preprint resolves a structured local analytic subproblem. It
does not supply the global structured approximation of an arbitrary source
homeomorphism, and it does not prove its own completion assumption. The
correct status is therefore partial literature progress, not a full answer.
