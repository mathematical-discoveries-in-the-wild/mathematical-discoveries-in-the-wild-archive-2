# Bounded novelty search

Date: 2026-08-11

Targets: Questions 6.1 and 6.2 of arXiv:2011.10638, asking whether every
summable ideal is an isomorphic direct sum of ideals generated by sequences
with consecutive ratios bounded below, and whether the exceptional
coordinatewise representability set `mathscr A` is analytic.

Searches performed:

- all lightweight indexes for `fa_banach_001`, using the arXiv id and the
  phrases `convergent subseries`, `summable ideal`, and `analytic subset`;
- the exact source question and its surrounding definitions;
- an arXiv API search for the exact phrase `summable ideal` (11 records were
  returned; the post-2020 titles concerned densities and Katetov order, not
  this representability set);
- the works indexed by OpenAlex as citing the published source (three hits,
  two genuine but unrelated papers and one spurious sensor-paper match).

No later paper resolving either question was located. This is evidence only,
not a claim of priority or an exhaustive literature review.
