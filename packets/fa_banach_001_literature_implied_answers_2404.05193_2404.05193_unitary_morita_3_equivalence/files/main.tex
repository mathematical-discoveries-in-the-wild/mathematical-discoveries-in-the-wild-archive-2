\section*{Original conjecture}

Giovanni Ferrer's \emph{Foundations for operator algebraic tricategories},
arXiv:2404.05193 \cite{Ferrer}, defines the W$^*$-3-category
$\mathsf{UmFC}$ of unitary multifusion categories, unitary bimodule
categories, unitary bimodule functors, and bimodule natural transformations.
Immediately after Example 3.15, on PDF page 26, it states:
\begin{quote}
``We conjecture there exists an equivalence
$\mathsf{Mod}^{\dagger}\colon \mathsf{UmFC}\cong\mathsf{FSSU2C}$, where the
latter is the W$^*$-3-category of finitely semisimple unitary 2-categories.''
\end{quote}
The four other extraction hits of the form ``we ask that'' are coherence
requirements inside definitions, not open research questions.

\section*{Supporting theorem}

Quan Chen, Giovanni Ferrer, Brett Hungar, David Penneys, and Sean Sanford,
\emph{Manifestly unitary higher Hilbert spaces}, arXiv:2410.05120
\cite{CFHPS}, cites arXiv:2404.05193 when using its C$^*$-3-category
foundations.  Its Theorem C, stated on PDF page 4 and proved in Section 5,
asserts
\[
 \mathsf{Mod}^{\dagger}\colon
 \mathsf{H}^{*}\mathsf{mFC}\ \simeq\ 3\mathsf{Hilb}
\]
as a unitary equivalence of C$^*$-3-categories, preserving unitary adjoints
and spherical weights.

This is an enhanced form of the source conjecture.  The supporting paper also
proves the facts needed to forget the enhancements:
\begin{itemize}
\item every unitary multifusion category admits the required unitary dual
functor and spherical weight (discussion around its Example 2.9);
\item every unitary module category admits a faithful module trace (Corollary
3.13), and finite semisimple bimodule categories are uniquely unitarizable
(Corollary 5.8, PDF page 53);
\item the forgetful 3-functor
$\mathsf{H}^{*}\mathsf{mFC}\to\mathsf{mFC}$ is fully faithful (Corollary 5.13,
PDF page 54);
\item unitarizable finite semisimple 2-categories are uniquely unitarizable
(the corollary on PDF page 53), and the forgetful functor
$3\mathsf{Hilb}\to3\mathsf{Vect}$ is fully faithful (Corollary D).
\end{itemize}

Existence supplies lifts of the objects and 1-morphisms in the original
unitary 3-categories; full faithfulness and uniqueness make the result
independent, up to unitary equivalence, of the chosen enhancement.  Forgetting
the unitary-adjoint, weight, and trace decorations in Theorem C therefore gives
exactly
\[
 \mathsf{Mod}^{\dagger}\colon
 \mathsf{UmFC}\ \simeq\ \mathsf{FSSU2C},
\]
which is the conjecture in arXiv:2404.05193.

\section*{Scope and provenance}

The implication uses the later paper's enhanced theorem together with its own
existence and forgetful corollaries.  Because the supporting paper does not
restate the undecorated conjecture verbatim, this packet is classified as a
literature-implied answer.  It is not merely the older nonunitary
equivalence between multifusion categories and finite semisimple
2-categories.  The supporting paper appeared later, has Ferrer as a coauthor,
and explicitly cites arXiv:2404.05193.  Exact-title, notation, author, and
close-variant searches through 13 August 2026 found this as the decisive later
answer.  No part of this packet is claimed as a new proof.
