# Literature-implied answer: non-Hilbert complex Radon planes exist

Status: `literature_implied_answer`

## Source question

Ryotaro Tanaka, *On Birkhoff-James orthogonality preservers between real
non-isometric Banach spaces*, arXiv:2108.00655, Remarks, PDF page 11, says
that the existence of a complex Radon plane was not known to the author.  In
context the intended question is the existence of a non-Hilbert example.

## Supporting answer

Alexander Guterman, Bojan Kuzma, Sushil Singla, and Svetlana Zhilina,
*Birkhoff-James classification of norm's properties*, arXiv:2402.13416,
Example 3.17 on PDF pages 23-24, explicitly constructs a nonsmooth complex
Radon plane.  Nonsmoothness makes the example non-Hilbert.  The paper also
credits still earlier complex examples to Oman.

The later paper cites Tanaka's source article elsewhere, but it does not
explicitly say that Example 3.17 answers Tanaka's sentence.  The provenance is
therefore `literature_implied_answer`, not `literature_already_answered`.

## Scope

Only the complex-Radon-plane existence branch is settled here.  The source's
other complex questions about automatic continuity of nonlinear
Birkhoff-James preservers, and its general real/complex classification
questions, are not resolved by this packet.
