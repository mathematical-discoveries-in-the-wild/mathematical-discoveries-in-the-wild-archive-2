## Area-formula audit

For Lipschitz `Phi=(F,G)` on each surface chart, the metric/approximate
two-Jacobian is `abs(det D(F,G))`. The a.e. relation `dF wedge dG=0` makes it
zero. The full area formula with multiplicity gives
`integral N(Phi,E,y) dy = integral J_2 Phi dx = 0`, so each chart image and
therefore the finite union `Phi(M)` have planar measure zero. No injectivity
or Lusin-N shortcut is used.

## Dimension audit

The joint image is compact and planar-null, hence has empty interior. The
classical Euclidean covering-dimension theorem (Engelking, Theorem 1.8.10)
says that a subset of `R^2` has covering dimension two iff it contains an
interior point. Thus the image has covering dimension at most one. The proof
does not make the false inference that measure zero implies Hausdorff
dimension below two.

## Quasi-state audit

it linear on the at-most-one-dimensional compact metric image. Applying this
to the two coordinate functions gives the desired identity exactly.

answers the finite-smoothness request.
