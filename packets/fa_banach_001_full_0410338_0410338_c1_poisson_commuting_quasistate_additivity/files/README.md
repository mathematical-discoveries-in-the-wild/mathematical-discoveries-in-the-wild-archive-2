# Finite-smoothness quasi-state additivity on surfaces

Candidate full answer to Problem 8.2 of arXiv:math/0410338v2.

For any quasi-state on a closed surface, additivity holds for Lipschitz
functions `F,G` whenever `dF wedge dG = 0` almost everywhere. In particular,
every `C1` Poisson-commuting pair satisfies the identity requested by the
source problem.

The joint map `(F,G)` is Lipschitz with zero Jacobian. The area formula makes
its compact planar image null, so its covering dimension is at most one.
Pulling the quasi-state to that image and applying the same
Wheeler--Shakhmatov linearity theorem used by the source completes the proof.
