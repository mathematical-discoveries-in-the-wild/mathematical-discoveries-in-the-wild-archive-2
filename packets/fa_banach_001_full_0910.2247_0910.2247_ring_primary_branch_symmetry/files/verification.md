## Proof audit

1. With epsilon_0=-1 and epsilon_1=1, the stationary residual has the signs
   displayed in equation (1) of the packet.
2. On the v_3-axis the potential is odd. Oddness of S_0 makes its inner
   products with 1 and cosine vanish.
3. In the plane v_3=0 the potential and its sigmoid are even, so the sine
   inner product vanishes.
4. The source kernel at lambda_1 is the axis direction; at lambda_3 it is an
   even-plane direction. Applying simple-eigenvalue branch uniqueness to the
   full and restricted equations identifies the local curves.
5. Primary continuation without branch switching preserves the restricted
   curve. This matches the source’s numerical P_i convention.
6. A nontrivial axis sequence tending to zero gives
   1=lambda_* sigma_1/4 after division and dominated convergence, hence
   lambda_*=lambda_1.
7. A nontrivial even-plane sequence tending to zero yields a kernel vector of
   the restricted linearization. Its only spectral candidates are
   4/sigma_2 and 4/sigma_3. The source has sigma_2<0, so a positive limit is
   lambda_3.
8. The axis and plane intersect only at zero, while lambda_1 and lambda_3 are
   strictly ordered for alpha unequal to 2 close to 2. Therefore the two
   primary branches are disjoint.
