# Novelty and literature-status check

Status: bounded search; exhaustive priority is not claimed.

## Sources checked

- The run's `registry_index.tsv`, `solutions/index.tsv`, `attempts/index.tsv`,
  and `proof_gaps/index.tsv` were searched for arXiv `1401.2123`, the exact
  title, “summability degree,” “homotopy invariant,” “mapping cylinder,” and
  Cuntz--Krieger terminology.  No prior result on this question was found.
- The source arXiv TeX and final Documenta Mathematica PDF were inspected,
  especially Definition 4 and Lemma 6 on PDF page 6 and Theorem 7 on page 7.
- Exact-phrase and close-variant web searches were made for a proof or
  counterexample to homotopy invariance, and for mapping-cylinder treatments
  of summability degree.
- M. Dadarlat, “The homotopy groups of the automorphism group of Kirchberg
  algebras,” J. Noncommut. Geom. 1 (2007), 113--139, was checked in the
  primary journal PDF.  Theorem 2.3(i) is the decisive realization theorem.
- D. M. Gerontogiannis's 2021 Glasgow thesis still describes the analogous
  homotopy-invariance problem as interesting/open; it did not supply the
  mapping-cylinder counterexample.

## Finding

No checked source contained the construction in this packet.  Searches for
the exact question primarily returned the original paper and later repetitions
of the question.  Searches combining “mapping cylinder,” “summability degree,”
and “Fredholm module” did not find a prior counterexample.

The proof uses established ingredients in a short new combination: the two
extreme examples from the source paper, UCT, Kirchberg's homomorphism
realization theorem, and the elementary behavior of smooth subalgebras under
surjective pullback.  Novelty confidence is moderate to high after this
bounded search, but no exhaustive priority claim is made.
