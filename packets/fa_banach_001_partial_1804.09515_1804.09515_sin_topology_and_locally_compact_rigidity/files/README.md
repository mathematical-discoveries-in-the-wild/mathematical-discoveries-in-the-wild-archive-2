# SIN-topology and locally compact rigidity for `PU(M)`

Status: candidate partial result, likely valid, subject to expert review.

Source: Philip A. Dowerk, *Strong uncountable cofinality for unitary groups
of von Neumann algebras*, arXiv:1804.09515, Section 5, pages 11–12.

## Result

- For every separable II1 factor, the strong operator topology is the unique
  nontrivial separable SIN topology on `PU(M)`.
- For every separable type III factor, `PU(M)` has no nontrivial separable SIN
  topology.
- In both cases the abstract group `PU(M)` has no Hausdorff precompact group
  topology and no nontrivial homomorphism to a compact group.
- Combining this with the source paper's strong uncountable cofinality, every
  abstract homomorphism from `PU(M)` to a second-countable locally compact
  group is trivial.
- Consequently the source metric-completeness question has a positive answer
  whenever the induced topology is SIN.

The general non-SIN uniqueness/completeness question remains open. Fullness is
not needed for the partial theorem.
