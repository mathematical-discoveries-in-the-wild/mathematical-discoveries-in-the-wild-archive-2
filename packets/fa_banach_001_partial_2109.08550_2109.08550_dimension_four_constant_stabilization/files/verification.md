## Analytic proof checks

1. Ball automorphisms preserve strict commuting row contractions by the
   displayed defect identity; unitary variable rotations do so trivially.
2. The inverse ball automorphism recovers the original tuple, so the
   generated algebra and its joint primary block sizes are unchanged.
3. On a generalized eigenspace of size `r`, Schur's algebra bound gives at
   most `floor(r^2/4)` independent nilpotent coordinate parts.
4. With at least two spectral points, the four partitions of `4` give total
   coordinate-span dimension at most three: `3+1`, `2+2`, `2+1+1`, and
   `1+1+1+1` all attain the upper count `3`.
5. With one spectral point, the coordinate span consists of commuting
   nilpotents and has dimension at most four.
6. In the four-dimensional case, the unital algebra generated by the span
   has Schur-maximal dimension five.  Hence the span is closed under products.
7. After strict upper triangularization, `E_14` must belong to the nilpotent
   algebra.  If an element with nonzero square has middle coordinate `d!=0`,
   its strict-upper centralizer has dimension at most three.  If `d=0`, the
   projected algebra would be a three-dimensional isotropic subspace of a
   four-dimensional symplectic space.  Both contradict dimension four.
8. Consequently the maximal nilpotent algebra is square-zero.
9. For a square-zero row contraction, functional calculus retains only the
   value and gradient at zero.  Schwarz-Pick gives
   `||grad q(0)||_2<=1-|q(0)|^2`; the displayed disk Schur function matches
   the first jet, and classical von Neumann gives constant one.
10. In the three-variable branch, the transformed rational function is
    holomorphic beyond the closed ball, so uniform polynomial approximation
    legitimately extends the definition of `C_{3,4}` to it.
11. Zero-padding gives the reverse inequality `C_{d,4}>=C_{3,4}`.
12. Radial approximation `rho T -> T` handles non-strict row contractions.
