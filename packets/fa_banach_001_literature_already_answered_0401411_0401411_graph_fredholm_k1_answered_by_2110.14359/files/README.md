# arXiv:math/0401411 — graph-topology Fredholm problem answered

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Matthias Lesch asks for the fundamental group of the graph-topology space of
unbounded self-adjoint Fredholm operators and, more strongly, whether this
space classifies odd K-theory.

Marina Prokhorova, *Spaces of unbounded Fredholm operators. I. Homotopy
equivalences* (arXiv:2110.14359v3), proves in Theorem C that the relevant
graph-topology space is homotopy equivalent to the classical bounded and
unitary models for `K^1`. Consequently it is a `K^1`-classifying space and its
fundamental group is `Z`. Combined with the source paper's surjectivity result,
spectral flow induces an isomorphism on the fundamental group (up to the
choice of sign convention).

Prokhorova also records that Joachim had proved the K-theoretic classification
in 2003. The packet uses Prokhorova's explicit natural homotopy-equivalence
theorem as the directly checked supporting result.
