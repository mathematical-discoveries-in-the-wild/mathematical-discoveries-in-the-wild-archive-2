# arXiv:2010.06938 — both final mean-ergodicity questions answered by arXiv:2206.00341

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

On PDF page 11, Questions 3.15 and 3.16 of Hamzeh Keshavarzi, *Mean ergodic composition operators on* `H^∞(B_n)` (arXiv:2010.06938; Positivity 26 (2022), article 30), ask:

1. whether the converse of the paper's sufficient Bergman-metric condition for uniform mean ergodicity holds; and
2. whether a holomorphic self-map of the ball with no interior fixed point always induces a composition operator that is not mean ergodic.

Theorems 1.3 and 1.4 of Hamzeh Keshavarzi and Karim Hedayatian, *Ergodic behaviors of composition operators acting on space of bounded holomorphic functions* (arXiv:2206.00341v1), answer both questions. Theorem 1.3 characterizes mean and uniform mean ergodicity in the interior-fixed-point case and, through its proof, yields exactly the converse Bergman-metric implication. Theorem 1.4 proves non-mean-ergodicity whenever the symbol has no interior fixed point and explicitly says that it answers the source paper's Question 3.16.
