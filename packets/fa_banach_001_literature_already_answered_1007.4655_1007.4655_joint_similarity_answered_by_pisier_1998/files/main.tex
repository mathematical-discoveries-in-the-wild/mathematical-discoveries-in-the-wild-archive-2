\begin{center}
{\LARGE\bfseries The commuting-pair joint-similarity question}\par
\vspace{0.25em}
{\large A negative answer predating arXiv:1007.4655}\par
\vspace{0.65em}
Literature-status identification, lane 12 \quad $\cdot$ \quad 13 August 2026
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4em}

\paragraph{Status.}
\texttt{literature\_already\_answered}.  One of the two questions flagged in
Loring--Shulman was already answered negatively by Pisier in 1998.  The
separate Olsen compact-perturbation questions are not claimed to be resolved
here.

\section*{Source question}

Loring and Shulman, \emph{A Generalized Spectral Radius Formula and Olsen's
Question}, arXiv:1007.4655v2, state in their Introduction (printed p.~2) the
question whether commuting Hilbert-space operators $A,B$, each individually
similar to a contraction, must be simultaneously similar to contractions
\cite{LS}.  Their Theorem~8 proves the result when one member is similar to a
strict contraction.

\section*{Decisive literature answer}

Pisier's paper \emph{Joint similarity problems and the generation of operator
algebras with bounded length}, published in 1998, states in its abstract that
there is a commuting pair $(T_1,T_2)$ such that each $T_i$ is similar to a
contraction while the pair is not similar to a pair of contractions
\cite{Pisier}.

Clouatre and Mbacke explicitly identify the same result in the abstract and
Introduction of arXiv:1806.09027 (printed pp.~1--2) \cite{CM}.  They cite it as
Pisier's Theorem~1 and record the stronger obstruction that $T_1T_2$ is not
even polynomially bounded.

Indeed, if one invertible $Y$ made both $YT_1Y^{-1}$ and $YT_2Y^{-1}$
contractions, then
\[
 YT_1T_2Y^{-1}=(YT_1Y^{-1})(YT_2Y^{-1})
\]
would be a contraction.  Hence $T_1T_2$ would be similar to a contraction and
therefore polynomially bounded, contradicting Pisier's example.  Thus the
answer to the source question is \emph{no}.

\section*{Chronology, provenance, and scope}

The answer predates arXiv:1007.4655 by twelve years.  This is therefore a
literature-status correction, not a new counterexample.  Clouatre--Mbacke's
paper knows and explicitly states the joint-similarity obstruction, cites the
primary 1998 theorem, and contrasts it with their positive finite-dimensional
result.

The original paper also asks Olsen's two exact compact-perturbation questions:
for a fixed polynomial, and simultaneously for all polynomials.  Neither is
answered by Pisier's construction, so they are outside this packet.  Exact
phrase searches for Olsen's question and inspection of the adjacent lifting
papers arXiv:0907.2618 and arXiv:1111.6124 found no general resolution in the
bounded search.
