\section*{Source question}

Marra and Reggio's \emph{Stone duality above dimension zero} asks in
Remark 1.5 on page 3 of the source PDF:
\begin{quote}
``To the best of our knowledge it is an open problem whether
$\CompHaus^{\mathrm{op}}$ is axiomatisable by a first-order theory.''
\end{quote}
Here $\CompHaus$ is the category of compact Hausdorff spaces and continuous
maps~\cite{MarraReggio}.

\section*{Identification of the answer}

Lieberman, Rosick\'y, and Vasey explicitly state in the introduction of their
later paper that their non-elementarity theorem answers
Marra--Reggio's Question 1.5~\cite[p.~2]{LRV}.  Their Theorem 22 proves that
the category of commutative unital $C^*$-algebras with unit-preserving
$*$-homomorphisms is not an abstract elementary category.  Gelfand duality
identifies that category with $\CompHaus^{\mathrm{op}}$, so their Corollary 24
states, on page 8 of the supporting PDF,
\[
 \CompHaus^{\mathrm{op}}
 \quad\text{is not an abstract elementary category.}
\]

A category of models of a finitary first-order theory, with all structure
homomorphisms, is an abstract elementary category in the supporting paper's
framework.  Corollary 24 therefore gives the requested negative answer:
$\CompHaus^{\mathrm{op}}$ is not axiomatisable by a first-order theory.  In
fact, the failure of abstract elementarity is strictly stronger.

\section*{Provenance and scope}

This is a literature identification, not a new mathematical result of the
run.  The supporting authors explicitly knew they were answering the source
question: their introduction cites Marra--Reggio, Question 1.5.  Exact-title,
exact-question, author, and categorical-term searches located the later paper
and a 2020 independent citation confirming the implication.

The answer concerns the first-order question in Remark 1.5.  It does not
negate Marra--Reggio's main positive theorem, which supplies a finite
equational axiomatisation using infinitary operations.
