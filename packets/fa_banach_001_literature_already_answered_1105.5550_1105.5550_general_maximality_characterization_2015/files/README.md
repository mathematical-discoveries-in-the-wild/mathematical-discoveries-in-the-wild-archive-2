- Exact target: published Remark 7 on PDF page 10 (submitted-source Remark

Later source:

- Nicolas Hadjisavvas, Flávia M. O. Jacinto, and Juan E. Martínez-Legaz,
  *Some Conditions for Maximal Monotonicity of Bifunctions*, Set-Valued and
  Variational Analysis 25 (2017), 323–332; published online in 2015,
  DOI 10.1007/s11228-015-0343-6.
- Exact answer: Theorem 2 on publisher-PDF page 7 (journal page 329).

The later theorem gives necessary and sufficient conditions for a monotone
bifunction with convex lower-semicontinuous second-variable slices to be
maximal monotone.  Applied to the standard extended-real extension of
`F+G` from `C times C` to `X times X`, its conditions (2)–(4) are exact
answers to what must be added.  This is a mathematical inference from the
later theorem; the later authors do not label it as an answer to the
source's Remark 7.
