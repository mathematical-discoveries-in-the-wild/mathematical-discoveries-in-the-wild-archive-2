# Supporting evidence

Supporting paper: Gestur Ólafsson and Angela Pasquale, *Ramanujan's Master
theorem for the hypergeometric Fourier transform on root systems*,
arXiv:1211.0024, submitted 31 October 2012.

- Official abstract: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.0024>
- Official PDF: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0024>
- Abstract: announces an analogue for the hypergeometric Fourier transform on
  root systems and says it generalizes the authors' symmetric-space theorem to
  arbitrary positive multiplicity functions.
- Introduction, PDF pages 1--2: identifies the root-system theorem as a
  generalization of the authors' semisimple Riemannian symmetric-space result.
- Theorem 5.1, PDF pages 18--19: `Ramanujan's Master Theorem for root systems`.
- Section 6, beginning on PDF page 19: proves Theorem 5.1 and explicitly says
  the proof extends the necessary estimates from the earlier symmetric-space
  theorem to positive multiplicity functions.
