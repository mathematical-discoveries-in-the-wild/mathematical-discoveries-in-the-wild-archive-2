# Root-system Ramanujan theorem answered by arXiv:1211.0024

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Source: Gestur Ólafsson and Angela Pasquale, *Ramanujan's Master Theorem for
Riemannian symmetric spaces*, arXiv:1103.5126.

Supporting answer: Gestur Ólafsson and Angela Pasquale, *Ramanujan's Master
theorem for the hypergeometric Fourier transform on root systems*,
arXiv:1211.0024.

## Identification

In Section 8 (`Further remarks and open problems`), the source asks for a
generalization of its theorem to Heckman--Opdam hypergeometric functions
associated with root systems.  It says even multiplicities should be the
easiest case.

The later paper explicitly solves that problem.  Its abstract states that it
proves a Ramanujan Master theorem for the hypergeometric Fourier transform on
root systems and extends the earlier symmetric-space result to **arbitrary
positive multiplicity functions**.  The precise result is Theorem 5.1 on PDF
pages 18--19, followed by its proof in Section 6.  The introduction and proof
explicitly identify arXiv:1103.5126 as the earlier result being generalized.

This is therefore an author-known direct answer, stronger than the source's
suggested even-multiplicity subcase.

## Scope

This packet clears only the root-system extension.  It does not answer the
source's separate broad requests for higher-rank analogues of Bertram's kernel
formulas or a Paley--Wiener characterization of all admissible functions.
