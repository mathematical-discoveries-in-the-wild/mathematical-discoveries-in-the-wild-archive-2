\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.91\textwidth}{\textbf{Status: literature\_implied\_answer (full counterexample as written).}
Conjecture~5.1 of Pfander--Salanevich cannot hold under a cardinality
assumption alone.  For
\(\Lambda_M=\{0\}\times\mathbb Z_M\), the Gabor system generated by a
uniform random unit vector is almost surely a frame and
\(|\Lambda_M|=M=O(M\log^\alpha M)\) for every \(\alpha\geq0\), but for every
fixed retained proportion \(p\in(0,1)\),
\[
 \Delta_M(p)\leq M\min_j|g(j)|^2\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
In fact, for \(0<c<1\),
\(\Pr\{M\min_j|g(j)|^2\geq c\}=(1-c)^{M-1}\).}}
\end{center}

\section*{The conjecture}

For a Gabor frame $(g,\Lambda)$ in $\mathbb C^M$, let
\[
 \Delta(p)=\min_{\substack{\Lambda''\subset\Lambda\\
                  |\Lambda''|\geq p|\Lambda|}}
     \sigma_{\min}^2(\Phi_{\Lambda''}^*),
 \qquad 0<p<1.
\]
Conjecture~5.1 of Pfander--Salanevich asks for an exponent $\alpha\geq0$
such that, whenever $g$ is uniform on the complex unit sphere and
$|\Lambda|=O(M\log^\alpha M)$, one has
\[
 \Delta(p)\geq c(p,\varepsilon)\frac{|\Lambda|}{M}
\]
with probability at least $1-\varepsilon$
\cite[Section~5, Conjecture~5.1]{source}.  As stated, the hypothesis gives
only an upper cardinality bound and imposes no spreading condition on
$\Lambda$.

\section*{Idea of the counterexample}

Take all modulations but only one translation.  The resulting synthesis
matrix is a diagonal matrix formed from $g$ times the discrete Fourier
matrix.  Hence its least frame eigenvalue is controlled by the smallest
coordinate of $g$.  A uniform vector on the complex sphere has exponentially
distributed coordinate masses after normalization, and its smallest mass is
of order $M^{-2}$, not $M^{-1}$.  Since the full frame itself is one of the
subframes allowed in the definition of $\Delta(p)$, no adversarial erasure is
even needed.

\section*{Exact counterexample}

Let
\[
 \Lambda_M=\{0\}\times\mathbb Z_M,
 \qquad
 \Phi_M=\{M_\ell g:\ell\in\mathbb Z_M\},
\]
where $(M_\ell g)(m)=e^{2\pi i\ell m/M}g(m)$.  If $F_M$ denotes the
unnormalized Fourier matrix, then the synthesis matrix is
$\operatorname{diag}(g)F_M$.  Since $F_MF_M^*=MI$,
\[
 \Phi_M\Phi_M^*=M\operatorname{diag}(|g(0)|^2,\ldots,|g(M-1)|^2),
\]
and consequently
\[
 \sigma_{\min}^2(\Phi_M^*)=M\min_j|g(j)|^2.                 \tag{1}
\]
All coordinates of $g$ are nonzero almost surely, so this is almost surely a
Gabor frame.  This computation is also the $F=\{0\}$ case of the exact frame
bound formula in Salanevich--Strachan, Proposition~4.1
\cite[Proposition~4.1]{bounds}.

For a uniform vector on the complex unit sphere, the vector of squared
coordinate moduli is uniform on the simplex, i.e.
\[
 (|g(0)|^2,\ldots,|g(M-1)|^2)\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1).
\]
Translating and rescaling the simplex gives, for $0\leq t\leq1/M$,
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{\min_j|g(j)|^2\geq t\right\}=(1-Mt)^{M-1}.
\]
Putting $t=c/M$ yields the exact identity
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{M\min_j|g(j)|^2\geq c\right\}=(1-c)^{M-1}\longrightarrow0
 \qquad(0<c<1).                                            \tag{2}
\]

For every $p\in(0,1)$, the choice $\Lambda''=\Lambda_M$ is admissible in the
minimum defining $\Delta_M(p)$.  Equations (1)--(2) therefore imply
\[
 \Delta_M(p)\leq\sigma_{\min}^2(\Phi_M^*)\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
On the other hand, $|\Lambda_M|/M=1$.  Thus no positive constant depending
only on $p$ and $\varepsilon$ can give the conjectured lower bound with
probability $1-\varepsilon$ for all sufficiently large $M$.

\section*{The obstruction persists for every fixed number of translations}

The same issue is not peculiar to a basis.  Fix $K\geq1$, take
$F=\{0,1,\ldots,K-1\}$, and put $\Lambda=F\times\mathbb Z_M$.  Proposition
4.1 gives the exact lower frame bound
\[
 A_M=M\min_m\sum_{k\in F}|g(m-k)|^2.                       \tag{3}
\]
Write $g_j=Z_j/(\sum_s|Z_s|^2)^{1/2}$ with $|Z_j|^2$ independent
$\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ variables.  Among the cyclic $K$-blocks in (3) are
$\lfloor M/K\rfloor$ disjoint blocks, whose unnormalized sums are independent
$\operatorname{Gamma}(K,1)$ variables.  Their minimum converges to zero in
probability, while $M^{-1}\sum_s|Z_s|^2\to1$.  Hence $A_M\to0$ in probability
for every fixed $K$.  Again $\Delta_M(p)\leq A_M$, whereas the conjectured
scale is $|\Lambda|/M=K$.

This also explains the necessary logarithmic threshold in the later paper:
for a uniform spherical window and $\Lambda=F\times\mathbb Z_M$, its
dimension-uniform lower-frame estimate assumes $|F|\geq C\log M$
\cite[Example~4.3(iii)]{bounds}.

\section*{Scope and repair}

The counterexample addresses Conjecture~5.1 exactly as quantified.  It does
not rule out a repaired theorem requiring both a lower cardinality bound and
geometric spreading or randomness of $\Lambda$; nor does it settle the
paper's separate uniform phase-retrieval robustness conjecture.  At minimum,
$|\Lambda|=O(M\log^\alpha M)$ must be replaced by a two-sided oversampling
condition together with structural assumptions that exclude fixed-translation
sets.  The supporting paper explicitly emphasizes that frame bounds depend
on the structure, not merely the cardinality, of $\Lambda$
\cite[Sections~1.2 and~4]{bounds}.
