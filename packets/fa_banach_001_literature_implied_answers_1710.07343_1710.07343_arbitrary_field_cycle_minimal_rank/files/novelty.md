# Bounded literature and novelty check

Checked on 2026-08-11.

## Local indexes

## Sources checked

- Grossmann--Woerdeman, arXiv:1710.07343 and the 2021 journal metadata.
- Benjamin Grossmann's 2019 Drexel thesis, especially pp. 64--65 and 69--70.
- Cohen--Pereira, *The cyclic rank completion problem with regular blocks*,
  journal abstract and DOI metadata. The full article was closed access in the
  checked sources.
- Cohen--Pereira's later general-block cycle paper metadata.
- Citation/search queries for the exact title, arXiv id, arbitrary-field
  wording, eigenvalue hypothesis, and small-rank-step formulation.
- Shitov's later work on fractional minimal rank and chordal bipartite graphs.

## Finding

Grossmann's thesis gives the decisive bridge: it attributes to Cohen--Pereira
an arbitrary-field completion upper bound `(nk-d)/(n-1)` for the same regular
block cycle. At `k=n-1,d=0`, this is the rank `n` asked for in the source.
Accordingly, the minimal-rank value is not classified as a new result.

The packet contains an independently derived constructive proof and a modest
strengthening to every nonidentity invertible holonomy. No claim is made that
this proof or strengthening is absent from Cohen--Pereira, whose exact theorem
should be checked by a human reviewer.
