# BV embeds strictly into the one-sided Riemann--Liouville space

Status: **literature already answered**.

Bergounioux, Leaci, Nardi, and Tomarelli ask in arXiv:1603.05033 whether
every one-dimensional function of bounded variation belongs to
`W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)`, for every `0<s<1`. Since their paper already exhibits
elements of every such Riemann--Liouville space that are not in `BV(I)`, the
question is whether the inclusion is strict.

Carbotti and Comi explicitly answer the question in arXiv:2003.09515.
Theorem 3.4 proves the continuous embedding
`BV(I) -> W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)`, gives the derivative representation, and proves
weak-* convergence of the fractional derivatives as `s` tends to one.
Proposition 3.9 proves strictness.
