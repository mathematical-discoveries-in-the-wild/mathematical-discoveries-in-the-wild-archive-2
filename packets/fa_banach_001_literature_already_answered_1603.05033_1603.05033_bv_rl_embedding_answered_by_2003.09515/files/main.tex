\section*{Source question}
Let $I=(a,b)$ be a bounded interval and $0<s<1$.  Bergounioux, Leaci,
Nardi, and Tomarelli define
\[
 W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)
 =\{u\in L^1(I): I_{a+}^{1-s}u\in W^{1,1}(I)\}.
\]
Their Theorem~4.1 proves $SBV(I)\subset W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)$, but the
conclusion states that it was not known whether
\[
 BV(I)\subsetneq W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)
 \qquad(0<s<1).
\]
This appears on printed p.~22, Section~5, of the source paper
\cite[p.~22, Section~5]{BLNT}.

\section*{Exact later answer}
Carbotti and Comi explicitly state that they answer questions posed in the
source paper and extend its Theorem~4.1 from $SBV$ to $BV$.

\begin{answer}[Carbotti--Comi, Theorem 3.4 and Proposition 3.9]
For every $u\in BV(I)$ and $0<s<1$,
\[
 u\in W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I),\qquad
 D^s_{a+}u(x)=I^{1-s}_{a+}[Du](x)
 +\frac{u(a+)}{\Gamma(1-s)(x-a)^s}.
\]
The embedding $BV(I)\hookrightarrow W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)$ is continuous.
Moreover, $W^{s,1}_{RL,a+}(I)$ strictly contains $BV(I)$.
\end{answer}

The embedding and representation are Theorem~3.4, beginning on printed
p.~23; the theorem also proves
\[
 D^s_{a+}u\,\mathcal L^1 \stackrel{*}{\rightharpoonup}
 Du+u(a+)\delta_a
 \quad\text{in }\mathcal M([a,b])\quad(s\uparrow1).
\]
Strictness is Proposition~3.9 on printed pp.~28--29
\cite[Theorem~3.4 and Proposition~3.9]{CC}.  Thus no part of the source's
inclusion question remains open.
