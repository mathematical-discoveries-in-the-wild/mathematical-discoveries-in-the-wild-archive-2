\section*{Status}

\textbf{Literature already answered.} This note records a later-literature
answer to a question suggested by Milman~\cite{Milman}; it is not a new proof
from this run.

\section*{Source Question}

For $n\geq 2$, $|x|<1$, and $\alpha\geq-n$, let $\mu_x^{n,\alpha}$ be the
probability measure on $S^n$ whose density relative to spherical measure is
proportional to $|y-x|^{-(n+\alpha)}$. In Section~4.2, page~9 of the source
PDF, Milman writes that interpolation between $\alpha=-n$ and $\alpha=0$
suggests that the spectral gap remains of order $n$, uniformly for
$\alpha\in[-n,0]$ and $|x|<1$.

\section*{Supporting Answer and Parameter Match}

Ma and Wang~\cite{MaWang} study $\mu_x^{m,\beta}$ on $S^{m-1}$, with density
proportional to $|y-x|^{-(m+\beta)}$. Their Theorem~1.1 gives, in the branches
relevant here,
\[
 \lambda_1(\mu_x^{m,\beta})\geq
 \begin{cases}
  m-2, & \beta\leq 2-m,\\[2mm]
  \dfrac{m-\beta}{2}-1, & 2-m<\beta<-1,\\[2mm]
  \dfrac{(\beta+3)(m-1)}{4}, & \beta=-1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Set
\[
 m=n+1,\qquad \beta=\alpha-1.
\]
Then $m+\beta=n+\alpha$, so the two papers define the same measure. The
displayed estimate becomes
\[
 \lambda_1(\mu_x^{n,\alpha})\geq
 \begin{cases}
  n-1, & -n\leq\alpha\leq 2-n,\\[1mm]
  \dfrac{n-\alpha}{2}, & 2-n<\alpha<0,\\[2mm]
  \dfrac n2, & \alpha=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Hence
\[
 \inf_{|x|<1}\lambda_1(\mu_x^{n,\alpha})\geq \frac n2
 \qquad(-n\leq\alpha\leq0),
\]
which is exactly the uniform order-$n$ conclusion proposed in the source.

\section*{Provenance and Scope}

The supporting authors explicitly identify Milman's work: their abstract says
that their general-$\beta$ bounds improve Milman's 2015 estimates, and the
publisher text discusses Milman's generalized harmonic measures. Thus this is
an explicit later answer, not merely an agent-identified consequence.

The range $\alpha\in[-n,0]$ is completely covered. Milman's separate question
about $\alpha<-n$ remains outside this identification.

The supporting article has no arXiv version located in the bounded
title/author/DOI search, and its publisher PDF endpoint denied access. The
source arXiv PDF is included locally; the supporting paper is identified by
its stable DOI and exact theorem label.
