## Mathematical audit

1. The ten displayed Pauli tensor matrices are a Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal
   basis of the ten-dimensional real space `Sym_4(R)`.
2. `K=span{Z tensor I, X tensor I, Y tensor Y}` is a symmetric Clifford
   space. Pairwise anticommutation gives
   `(aA1+bA2+cA3)^2=(a^2+b^2+c^2)I`, so every nonzero member has rank four.
3. Its orthogonal complement `S` has basis `I,Q1,...,Q6`. The seven displayed
   rank-two projections recover that basis by elementary linear combinations,
   so their span is exactly `S`.
4. Equal projection-norm measurements put `xx^T-yy^T` in `K`. Its rank is at
   most two, while a nonzero member of `K` has rank four; hence it is zero and
   `x=+/-y`.
5. If `zz^T` lies in `S`, its three inner products with the Clifford
   generators vanish. The Hopf identity says the sum of their squares is
   `||z||^4`, hence `z=0`. Thus `S` contains no nonzero rank-one matrix.
6. If `S` were spanned by seven rank-one projections, each generator would
   be a nonzero rank-one member of `S`, contradiction.

No circular step or dimension mismatch was found.
