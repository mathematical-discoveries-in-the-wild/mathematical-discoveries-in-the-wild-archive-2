\section{Source question}

In Example 5.9 of \emph{Weak-* and completely isometric structure of
noncommutative function algebras}, Sampat and Shalit ask whether the nc column
unit ball
\[
 \Cball_d=\left\{X\in\M^d:
 \left\|\sum_{j=1}^dX_j^*X_j\right\|<1\right\}
\]
is right regular \cite{SampatShalit2025}.  The question appears on PDF page 23.

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/open_problem_crop.png}
  \caption{The source question, arXiv:2411.09372v2, Example 5.9 on PDF
  page 23.}
\end{figure}

For an nc operator ball $\mathbb D_Q$, right regularity requires, among other
things, a constant $M$ such that for every $N$ and every function $f$ in the
ideal generated by the monomials of length $N$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:regularity}
 \left(\sup_{X\in\mathbb D_Q}
 \left\|\operatorname{row}(X^w)_{|w|=N}\right\|\right)
 \left(\sup_{X\in\mathbb D_Q}
 \left\|\operatorname{col}(\Delta^w f(0,\ldots,0,X))_{|w|=N}\right\|\right)
 \le M\|f\|.
\end{equation}
The crucial point is that $M$ is independent of both $N$ and $f$.

\section{Proof intuition}

The geometric factor in \eqref{eq:regularity} is already too large.  Although
each coordinate of a column contraction is contractive, the row containing
all $d^N$ words of length $N$ can have norm $d^{N/2}$.  Finite truncated
backward shifts attain this value in the limit: every word sends a distinct
length-$N$ basis vector to the same vacuum vector.

The monomial $f_N(Z)=Z_1^N$ prevents the second factor from compensating for
this growth.  It has norm one, and its right Taylor--Taylor coefficient column
at order $N$ contains a single nonzero entry, the constant function one.
Thus the left side of \eqref{eq:regularity} is exactly $d^{N/2}$.

\section{Exact word-row growth}

For $N\ge1$, put
\[
 A_{N,d}=\sup_{X\in\Cball_d}
 \left\|\operatorname{row}(X^w)_{|w|=N}\right\|.
\]

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:growth}
For every $d,N\ge1$, one has $A_{N,d}=d^{N/2}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $X\in\Cball_d$, then $X_j^*X_j\le\sum_kX_k^*X_k<I$, so every $X_j$ is a
contraction.  Hence $\|X^w\|\le1$ for every word $w$, and
\[
 \left\|\operatorname{row}(X^w)_{|w|=N}\right\|^2
 =\left\|\sum_{|w|=N}X^wX^{w*}\right\|
 \le d^N.
\]
Thus $A_{N,d}\le d^{N/2}$.

For the reverse inequality, let $\mathcal F_{d,N}$ be the finite-dimensional
Hilbert space with orthonormal basis $(e_v)_{|v|\le N}$ indexed by words in
$\{1,\ldots,d\}$, including the empty word $\vac$.  Define truncated left
creation operators by
\[
 L_je_v=\begin{cases}
 e_{jv},&|v|<N,\\
 0,&|v|=N.
 \end{cases}
\]
The ranges of the $L_j$ are mutually orthogonal and sum to the non-vacuum
subspace.  Given $0<r<1$, set $X_j=rL_j^*$.  Then
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^dX_j^*X_j
 =r^2\sum_{j=1}^dL_jL_j^*
 =r^2(I-P_{\vac})<I,
\]
so $X\in\Cball_d$ at the finite matrix level
$1+d+\cdots+d^N$.

For each word $w=(w_1,\ldots,w_N)$, the vector $X^{w*}e_{\vac}$ is
$r^Ne_{w_N\cdots w_1}$, and applying $X^w$ sends it back to
$r^{2N}e_{\vac}$.  Therefore
\[
 \sum_{|w|=N}X^wX^{w*}e_{\vac}
 =d^Nr^{2N}e_{\vac},
\]
and the word-row norm is at least $r^Nd^{N/2}$.  Letting $r\uparrow1$ proves
$A_{N,d}\ge d^{N/2}$.
\end{proof}

\section{Counterexample to right regularity}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
The nc column ball $\Cball_d$ is right regular if and only if $d=1$.  In
particular, it is not right regular for every $d\ge2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix $d\ge2$ and, for every $N\ge1$, consider
\[
 f_N(Z)=Z_1^N.
\]
This monomial lies in the ideal generated by the words of length $N$.  Since
the first coordinate of every $X\in\Cball_d$ is a contraction,
$\|f_N\|\le1$.  Conversely, scalar points $(r,0,\ldots,0)$ with $r\uparrow1$
show that $\|f_N\|=1$.

The order-$N$ right Taylor--Taylor expansion of $f_N$ is simply
\[
 f_N(Z)=\sum_{|w|=N}Z^w\Delta^wf_N(0,\ldots,0,Z)=Z_1^N\cdot1.
\]
Thus the coefficient column has one entry equal to the constant function one
and every other entry zero.  Its supremum norm on $\Cball_d$ is exactly one.
By Lemma \ref{lem:growth}, the left side of \eqref{eq:regularity} for $f_N$
is therefore
\[
 d^{N/2}\cdot1=d^{N/2}.
\]
No constant $M$ independent of $N$ can satisfy \eqref{eq:regularity}.  Hence
$\Cball_d$ is not right regular.

When $d=1$, the column ball is the one-variable row ball, which is right
regular by the row-ball example immediately following Definition 5.5 in the
source paper \cite{SampatShalit2025}.
\end{proof}

\section{Verification}

The proof uses finite matrices only.  A numerical checker included with this
packet constructs the truncated shifts for $d=2,3$ and $N=1,\ldots,4$ at
$r=0.83$.  It verifies
\[
 \left\|\sum_jX_j^*X_j\right\|=r^2,
 \qquad
 \left\|\operatorname{row}(X^w)_{|w|=N}\right\|=r^Nd^{N/2}.
\]
All cases pass.  These finite checks illustrate but do not replace the exact
argument above.

\section{Scope and novelty status}

The theorem fully answers Example 5.9 and gives the exact word-row growth.
It does not characterize right regularity for general nc operator balls.

A bounded search on 11 August 2026 checked the four run indexes, the current
arXiv v2 and journal metadata, the exact phrases ``column unit ball'' and
``right regular'', and combinations with ``nc operator ball'', ``truncated
shift'', and the growth $d^{N/2}$.  No separate answer was found.  This was
not an exhaustive citation search, so novelty remains unconfirmed.

\section{Human-review recommendation}

High priority.  The key audit is that the regularity constant in Definition
5.5 is uniform in $N$.  Under that stated quantifier, Lemma
\ref{lem:growth} and the monomial $Z_1^N$ give an immediate obstruction.
