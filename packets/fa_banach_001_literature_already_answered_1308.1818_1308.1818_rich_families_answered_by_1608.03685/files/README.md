# 1308.1818 — suitable models and rich families are equivalent

Status: literature_already_answered (full positive answers to source Questions 2 and 3).

Model: GPT5.6.

Source: Marek Cúth and Ondřej F. K. Kalenda, *Rich families and elementary submodels*, arXiv:1308.1818v3, source PDF pages 7 and 10.

Supporting answer: Marek Cúth, *Separable determination in Banach spaces*, arXiv:1608.03685v2, abstract and Theorems 4 and 15 on supporting PDF pages 1, 2, and 5.

## Identification

Source Question 2 asks whether suitable models generate nice rich families in every Banach space, including `ell_infinity^c(Gamma)`. Source Question 3 asks whether separable determination by suitable models is equivalent to separable determination by rich families without the earlier structural restrictions.

The supporting paper explicitly says it answers both questions. Its Theorem 15 proves the stronger statement that suitable models generate nice rich families in every topological space homeomorphic to a Banach space. Its Theorem 4 proves the two refinement implications between rich and suitable-model-large families in every Banach space. Hence Questions 2 and 3 are answered positively in full.

The source's narrower Question 1—whether the particular spanning-system sufficient condition in its Lemma 2.6 is necessary—is not implied by the later theorem and is not classified here.
