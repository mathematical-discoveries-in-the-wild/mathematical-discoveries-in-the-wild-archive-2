# Finite-support hypothesis in the noncommutative sequence dichotomy

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Randrianantoanina's arXiv:math/0004146 asks whether the common finite-trace
left-support hypothesis can be removed from its Rademacher/disjoint-sequence
dichotomy. The 2003 journal revision retains the question as Remarks 3.5.

Huang and Sukochev answer it affirmatively in *Bounded sequences in
noncommutative bimodules of tau-measurable operators*, Proceedings of the
AMS 154 (2026), 793--806, DOI `10.1090/proc/17465`. Their theorem gives the
same alternative for every bounded sequence in an order-continuous
noncommutative quasi-Banach bimodule, with no finite-support condition. Their
published abstract explicitly identifies Randrianantoanina's question as an
application.
