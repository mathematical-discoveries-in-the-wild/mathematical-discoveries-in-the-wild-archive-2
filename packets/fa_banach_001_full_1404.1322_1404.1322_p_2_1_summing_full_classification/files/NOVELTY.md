provisional pending specialist review.

## Bounded search performed

The run's cheap indexes were searched for arXiv id `1404.1322`, the title,
`absolutely (p;2,1)-summing`, and the core `ell_p`/`ell_q` equation.  No prior
solution or attempt packet was found.

Web/arXiv searches were then made for:

- the exact paper title together with `equation (4)` and `open problem`;
- the exact phrase `absolutely (p;2,1)-summing` with `ell_p` and `ell_q`;
- later papers citing or repeating the displayed equality; and
- combinations of the authors' names with the summability exponents.

The searches recovered the source preprint and published paper, which still
state that equation (4) is unknown, plus general literature on multilinear
summability.  They did not recover a later paper explicitly proving or
disproving the equality, nor the endpoint classification in this packet.

## Limitation

This was a bounded keyword/citation-style search, not an exhaustive
bibliographic proof of novelty.  The Rademacher-block counterexample may be
implicit in operator-ideal folklore or in literature using different
terminology.  A specialist should therefore check MathSciNet/Zentralblatt and
the post-2014 literature before any public novelty claim.
