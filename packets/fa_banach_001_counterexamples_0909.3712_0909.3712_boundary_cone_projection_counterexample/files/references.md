# References and search record

## Primary sources

- Philipp Grohs, *Continuous Shearlet Frames and Resolution of the Wavefront
  Set*, arXiv:0909.3712; *Monatshefte für Mathematik* 164 (2011), 393–426.
  Local copy: `source_paper.pdf`. Definition 2.1 defines directed
  `N`-regularity; Lemma 5.1 proves cone-projection equivalence for `s_0<v`;
  Remark 5.2 asks about `s_0=v`.
- Philipp Grohs, *Continuous Shearlet Tight Frames*, arXiv:1001.1516;
  *Journal of Fourier Analysis and Applications* 17 (2011), 506–518. This
  follow-up resolves the separate structured-dual/tight-frame direction and
  is not a resolution of Remark 5.2.
