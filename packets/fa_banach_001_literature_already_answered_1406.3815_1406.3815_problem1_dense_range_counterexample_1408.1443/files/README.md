# Problem 1 of arXiv:1406.3815 — negative literature answer

Status: `literature_already_answered (Problem 1 only)`

Original source:

- Amir Bahman Nasseri, *The Spectrum of Operators on C(K) with the
  Grothendieck Property and Characterization of J-Class Operators which are
  Adjoints*, arXiv:1406.3815.
- Exact location: Section 4, Problem 1, arXiv PDF page 18.

Answering source:

- William B. Johnson, Amir Bahman Nasseri, Gideon Schechtman, and Tomasz
  Tkocz, *Injective Tauberian Operators on L1 and Operators with Dense Range
  on ell-infinity*, arXiv:1408.1443; Canadian Math. Bulletin 58 (2015),
  276–280.
- Exact location: Corollary 1, arXiv PDF page 5.

Problem 1 asks whether every dense-range operator on a Grothendieck `C(K)`
space must be surjective.  Corollary 1 of the answering paper constructs an
injective, dense-range, nonsurjective operator on `ell_infinity`.  Since
`ell_infinity = C(beta N)` and is a Grothendieck space, this is an exact
negative answer.

The answering paper cites arXiv:1406.3815 and uses its spectral theorem to
observe that zero must be an interior spectral point of any such operator.
Problems 2 and 3 of the original paper are outside this identification and
remain unresolved by this packet.
