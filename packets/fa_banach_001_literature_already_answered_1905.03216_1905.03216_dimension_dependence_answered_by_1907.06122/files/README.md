# Dimension dependence in Hermite--Hadamard inequalities

Classification: literature already answered.

The introduction of arXiv:1905.03216 asks whether the constants in its
normalized inequality (1) remain bounded with dimension and whether the
constants in its scale-invariant Theorem 1 might decay.  The direct follow-up
arXiv:1907.06122 answers both qualitative questions negatively.

- It proves that the normalized optimal constants satisfy `c_n >= n-1`.
- It identifies the best constant on a fixed domain with the maximum inward
  normal derivative of the torsion function.  The ellipsoid family already
  discussed in the source, after correcting a harmless right-hand-side
  normalization, gives a positive dimension-independent lower bound for the
  scale-invariant constants.

The literal queue phrase “we do not know” only concerns the location of
surviving Brownian particles inside the proof and is not an open question.
