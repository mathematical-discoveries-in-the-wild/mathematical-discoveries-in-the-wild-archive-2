# Every quasicentralizer is associative

This packet gives a full affirmative answer to the first question in Remark
5.5 of Kaneda--Paulsen, arXiv:math/0312501 (PDF page 18). It therefore refutes
the authors' conjectured negative answer to that question.

## Result

For every operator space `X`, every quasicentralizer
`m(x,y)=gamma(x)y=x psi(y)` is associative. The reason is that canonical left
and right multiplier actions commute in the injective-envelope linking
C*-algebra.

The second question in the same remark, whether every such product belongs to
`QMB(X)`, is not claimed solved. Eight focused upgrade attempts and exact
finite-dimensional searches found no counterexample or general proof.
