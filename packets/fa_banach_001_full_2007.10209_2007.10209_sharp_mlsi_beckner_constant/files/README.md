# Sharp mLSI-to-Beckner constant

Candidate full solution to the open question in arXiv:2007.10209.

The packet proves that the best universal factor is `K_p^opt = 1/2` for every
`1 < p <= 2`. The lower bound follows by expressing the power entropy as a
positive Stieltjes mixture of shifted logarithmic entropies and integrating
mLSI. A symmetric two-point Dirichlet form attains the factor.
