# Schwartz Gabor frames over rational lattices

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Enstad--Thiel--Vilalta, arXiv:2408.03423, ask whether every phase-space
lattice of covolume below one admits a Schwartz-window Gabor frame. Their
remaining case is rational lattices in dimension at least two, and their main
theorem supplies a stronger sufficient arithmetic inequality.

Caragea--Kolountzakis--Pfander, arXiv:2608.06679 (posted 2026-08-07), prove
that this stronger inequality is also necessary. They classify all lattices
admitting Schwartz, or equivalently Feichtinger-algebra, Gabor-frame windows.
In particular,
`diag(1,1,1,1/2) Z^4` has covolume `1/2` but admits no such window, giving a
full negative answer to the broad source question.
