\section{The arXiv-version question}

Let $\mathcal M$ be a Cowen--Douglas atom and let $\mathcal H\in B_m(\D)$ be
a pure $\mathcal M$-contractive Hilbert module whose eigenvector bundle has
the form
\[
 E_{\mathcal H}\cong E_{\mathcal M}\otimes V,
\]
where $V$ is an anti-holomorphic rank-$m$ coefficient bundle in a Hilbert
space $\mathcal E$.  Write $\overline\Pi_V(w)$ for the orthogonal projection
onto $V(\bar w)$.  The arXiv v2 text proves that a factorization
\[
 \overline\Pi_V(w)=F(w)G(w)
\]
with bounded $F$ and $G$ implies
$\mathcal H\sim_s\mathcal M\otimes\Cm$, then asks whether similarity implies
such a factorization.  Its displayed range condition is $\operatorname{ran}
F(w)=V(w)$, although the projection has range $V(\bar w)$.

\section{The published answer}

The final journal version replaces the one-way theorem by an if-and-only-if
statement and uses the consistent condition
\[
 \operatorname{ran}F(w)=V(\bar w).
\]
Thus its Theorem 7.4 states that
\[
 \mathcal H\sim_s\mathcal M\otimes\Cm
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists F\in H^\infty_{B(\mathcal E_*,\mathcal E)}(\D),\ 
 G\in L^\infty_{B(\mathcal E,\mathcal E_*)}(\D):
 \begin{cases}
 \operatorname{ran}F(w)=V(\bar w),\\
 \overline\Pi_V(w)=F(w)G(w).
 \end{cases}
\]

\section{Why the converse holds}

Similarity gives, through the paper's Theorem 7.2, a uniformly bounded and
uniformly bounded-below anti-holomorphic bundle frame
\[
 \Gamma(w):\Cm\longrightarrow\mathcal E,
 \qquad \operatorname{ran}\Gamma(w)=V(w).
\]
Set
\[
 F(w)=\Gamma(\bar w),\qquad
 G(w)=\bigl(F(w)^*F(w)\bigr)^{-1}F(w)^*.
\]
Then $F$ is bounded analytic, the lower frame bound makes $G$ bounded, and
the standard projection formula gives
\[
 F(w)G(w)=F(w)\bigl(F(w)^*F(w)\bigr)^{-1}F(w)^*
 =P_{\operatorname{ran}F(w)}=P_{V(\bar w)}=\overline\Pi_V(w).
\]
This is precisely the requested converse in its type-consistent form.
