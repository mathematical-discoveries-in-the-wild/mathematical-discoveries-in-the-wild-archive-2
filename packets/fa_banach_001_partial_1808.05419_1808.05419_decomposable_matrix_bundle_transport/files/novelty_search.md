## Outcome

No source found in this bounded search explicitly states the countable
weighted-product theorem in the packet.  The 2023 derivation paper supplies
important general differential-calculus infrastructure but not the transport
metric or logarithmic-mean chain rule.  The 2025 bimodule papers give stronger
non-product examples on a restricted finite-index class, so the broad open
question is not claimed to be new or fully resolved here.

Novelty confidence is **moderate-low**: the product argument is elementary and
may be folklore.  The packet is promoted as a candidate partial result because
it is a complete, directly verifiable infinite-dimensional nontracial subcase,
not because priority has been established.
