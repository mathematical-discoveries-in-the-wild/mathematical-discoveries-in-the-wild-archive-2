{\large\bfseries Literature answer: endpoint real interpolation\\[-0.1em]
of Whitney-averaged tent spaces\par}

\vspace{0.35em}
{\color{accent}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}

\statusbox{Status: literature\_already\_answered (affirmative answer)}

\section*{Original question}

P. Auscher, S. Bechtel, and L. Haardt,
\emph{A new scale of function spaces characterizing homogeneous Besov
spaces}, arXiv:2512.07399, Section 4, page 40, immediately before Lemma 4.3,
identify an endpoint omitted from their tent-space interpolation theorem.
Their scale $\Tsp^{p,q,r}_\beta$ was defined only for $0<p<\infty$, and the
question is whether $\Zsp^{\infty,q,r}_\beta$ can likewise be obtained by
real interpolation of endpoint tent spaces.  The source snapshot used by the
queue described this as future work.

\section*{Decisive later theorem}

L. Haardt, \emph{A coherent theory of tent spaces and}\\
\emph{homogeneous Triebel--Lizorkin spaces}, arXiv:2602.20305v2, first
defines
$\Tsp^{\infty,q,r}_\beta$ by the Carleson-box quasi-norm
\[
 \sup_{\tau>0}\sup_{y\in\mathbb R^d}
 \left(\int_0^\tau \fint_{B(y,\tau)}
 \left(\fint_{t/2}^{t}\fint_{B(x,t)}
 |s^{-\beta}f(s,z)|^r\,dz\,ds\right)^{q/r}
 \frac{dx\,dt}{t}\right)^{1/q},
\]
with the usual modifications for infinite exponents.

Proposition 3.31, page 51, proves that whenever
$0<p\leq\infty$, $0<q,q_0,q_1,r\leq\infty$,
$\beta_0\ne\beta_1$, and $0<\theta<1$,
\[
 \bigl(\Tsp^{p,q_0,r}_{\beta_0},
       \Tsp^{p,q_1,r}_{\beta_1}\bigr)_{\theta,q}
 =\Zsp^{p,q,r}_{\beta},
 \qquad
 \beta=(1-\theta)\beta_0+\theta\beta_1,
\]
with equivalent quasi-norms.  Taking $p=\infty$ is exactly the missing
endpoint characterization.

\section*{Identification and proof route}

The supporting paper explicitly says immediately before Proposition 3.31 that
the characterization of $\Zsp^{\infty,q,r}_\beta$ was missing and that the
proposition supplies it.  Its proof extends the nesting argument used in the
source paper: endpoint tent spaces sit between the corresponding $\Zsp$-spaces
with the scale exponents $\min\{p,q_i\}$ and $\max\{p,q_i\}$, and the full-range
real interpolation theorem for $\Zsp$-spaces identifies both outer
interpolation spaces with the same $\Zsp^{p,q,r}_\beta$.

The current PDF of arXiv:2512.07399 now replaces the future-work sentence by a
direct pointer to Proposition 3.31 of the supporting paper.  This is therefore
an explicit later-paper answer, not a new result of the present run.
