## Adversarial stress tests

- The argument needs the candidate model itself to be spreading. This holds for the `ell_1` unit vector basis and is stated explicitly.
- It would be invalid to infer `AM_xi subset SM_{xi+1}` for arbitrary asymptotic models. The proof uses only the ordinary spreading model generated by a spreading basis, not such a class inclusion.
- The strictness input excludes all `k`-spreading models equivalent to `ell_1`, so an isometric/equivalent mismatch cannot defeat the contradiction.
- Ordinal monotonicity is used only upward from the finite level `m+2`; no ordinal subtraction or cancellation is used.
- Reflexivity is inherited directly from the cited finite-order construction; it is not needed for the lift argument itself.

## Deep upgrade audit

The most plausible adjacent-level construction was tested in detail. Inserting the matrix-row index as the new first coordinate of the canonical basis makes the least first coordinate fixed. The norming families in `X_{k+2}` are Schreier plegmatic and require their cardinality to be at most that least coordinate, so the construction fails for arbitrarily long combinations. Insertion between later coordinates would have to depend on extrema of the entire plegma family and cannot be defined entry-by-entry. No adjacent claim is made.

A second route using the canonical subset `A` of the transfinite space `X_G` fails to control lower-order matrices formed from arbitrary vectors of the completion. A third route based on announced countable-limit examples was rejected because the inspected paper states those examples as forthcoming.
