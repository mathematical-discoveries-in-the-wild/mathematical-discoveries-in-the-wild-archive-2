# 1806.08749 — strict higher asymptotic-model levels

Status: `candidate_partial_result_likely_valid_human_review_needed`.

Model: `GPT5.6`.

Source: Calderon-Garcia and Garcia-Ferreira, *Asymptotic models via plegma families*, arXiv:1806.08749, Question 5.18 on source PDF page 22.

## Result

For every positive integer `m` and every countable ordinal `eta >= m+2`, there is a reflexive Banach space `X` such that

```text
AM_m(X) != AM_eta(X).
```

More generally, if a spreading basis belongs to `SM_eta(X)` but not to `SM_{xi+1}(X)`, then it belongs to `AM_eta(X)` but not to `AM_xi(X)`.

The proof combines the source's one-order lift theorem with the Argyros--Kanellopoulos--Tyros spaces `X_{k+1}`, which have `ell_1` at spreading level `k+1` but not at level `k`.

## Scope

This is a substantial partial answer, not a full solution of Question 5.18. It covers every pair whose lower level is a positive integer and whose gap is at least two. It does not settle adjacent levels or pairs with an infinite lower ordinal.

## Deep upgrade attempts

- A proposed adjacent-level matrix obtained by inserting the row number into the canonical finite-order basis fails the Schreier admissibility condition once the tested combination length grows.
- The transfinite canonical-set construction separates spreading models generated inside one distinguished set, but does not exclude lower models generated by arbitrary vectors in the completed space.
- Announced countable-limit whole-space separation examples were not used because the inspected source labels them as forthcoming rather than proving them.

## Verification and novelty

The verification report audits the ordinal indexing, the self-spreading step, the finite-order witness, and the failed adjacent-level reindexing. Bounded local-index and web/arXiv searches found no later solution of Question 5.18 and no statement of this finite buffered separation corollary. Novelty remains subject to specialist review.
