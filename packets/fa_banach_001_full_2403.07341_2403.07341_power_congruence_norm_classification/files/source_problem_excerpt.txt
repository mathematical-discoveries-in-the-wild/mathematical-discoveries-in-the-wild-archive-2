\section{Final remark.}
Let $a\in A_+^{-1}$ and $0<p<\infty$. Put
\[
\phi_{a,p}(x)=(a^{\frac{p}{2}}x^pa^{\frac{p}{2}})^\frac{1}{p},
\quad x\in A_+^{-1}.
\]
\begin{problem}
What is a condition for $a$ and $p$ such that the equation
\[
\|\phi_{a,p}(x)a^{-1}\|=\|x\|
\]
holds for every $x\in A_+^{-1}$?
\end{problem}

The raw note then says that the paper provides the cases p=2 and p=1.
