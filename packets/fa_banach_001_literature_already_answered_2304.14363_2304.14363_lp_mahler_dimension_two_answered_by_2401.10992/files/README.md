# Two-dimensional L^p-Mahler conjectures: explicit later answer

Status: `literature_already_answered (complete n=2 subcase)`

The source paper arXiv:2304.14363 states symmetric and nonsymmetric
`L^p`-Mahler lower bounds as Conjectures 1.3 and 1.4 on PDF page 7.

The later paper arXiv:2401.10992 explicitly restates them and proves:

- Theorem 1.3: the symmetric conjecture holds when `n=2`.
- Theorem 1.4: the nonsymmetric conjecture holds when `n=2`.

Those theorems cover every finite `p>0`; the paper notes that `p=infinity`
in dimension two is Mahler's classical theorem. Thus the full
two-dimensional specialization of both source conjectures is settled.

The general higher-dimensional conjectures remain open and are not suitable
for a focused sparse-queue proof attempt: the source proves that the family of
finite-`p` inequalities implies the classical Mahler conjectures.
