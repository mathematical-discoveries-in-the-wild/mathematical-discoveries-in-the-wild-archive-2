# Primary-source evidence map

1. **arXiv:2511.10769v3**, *Dynamical Sampling: A Survey*.
   - Theorem 2.9: diagonal Carleson-frame characterization.
   - Conjecture 1, typeset page 9: Müntz–Szász sampling plus Besselness is conjectured to imply a frame.
   - Conjecture 3, typeset page 19: normalized orbits of bounded normal operators are conjectured not to be frames.
2. **arXiv:2605.29671v1**, Gallardo-Gutiérrez–Partington.
   - Abstract: explicitly announces counterexamples to the recent survey conjecture.
   - Section 3 and Example 3.4: prime sampling satisfies the reciprocal-divergence condition but destroys the lower frame bound.
3. **arXiv:2606.20848v1**, Krishtal–Pfander.
   - Abstract: explicitly names and refutes Conjecture 3 of the survey.
   - Theorem 1: constructs a bounded invertible normal operator and a vector whose normalized orbit is a frame.
4. **arXiv:2607.18491v1**, Krishtal–Miller.
   - Theorem 1.3: positive-spectrum block-diagonal Carleson suborbits with a natural density are frames exactly when the density is strictly positive and finite.
   - Section 5: explicitly notes that the survey's Müntz condition is necessary but not sufficient for a lower frame bound.

All four primary PDFs are bundled in this packet. No secondary source is needed for the classification.
