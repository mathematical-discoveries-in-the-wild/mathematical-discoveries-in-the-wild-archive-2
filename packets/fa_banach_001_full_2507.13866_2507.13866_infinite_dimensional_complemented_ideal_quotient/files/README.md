# arXiv:2507.13866 — infinite-dimensional complemented ideal quotient

Status: `candidate_full_solution`, pending expert review.

Source: Michael A. Rincón-Villamizar and Carlos Uzcátegui Aylwin,
*On the complementation of spaces of I-null sequences*, arXiv:2507.13866v2,
Remark 6.7 on printed page 17.

## Result

There are proper ideals `Fin ⊊ I ⊊ J` on `N` such that `c_{0,I}` is
complemented in `c_{0,J}` and, more strongly,

`c_{0,J}/c_{0,I}` is linearly isometric to `ell_infinity`.

Partition `N` into infinite blocks, put a free ultrafilter on each block,
take `J` to be the maximal ideal belonging to the first ultrafilter, and let
`I` be the intersection of all the maximal ideals. The remaining ultralimit
functionals map `c_{0,J}` contractively onto `ell_infinity`; the block-constant
map is an isometric right inverse. Its kernel is exactly `c_{0,I}`, and
`Id-RL` is a projection of norm at most two.
