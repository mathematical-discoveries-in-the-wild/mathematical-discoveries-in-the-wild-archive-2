\section*{Status}

This is a \texttt{literature\_already\_answered} packet, not a new theorem of
this run.  Dykema and Zhao \cite{DZ} explicitly answer the two amplification
questions in Section~4.3 of Boutonnet--Drimbe--Ioana--Popa \cite{BDIP}.

\section*{The source questions}

Let $A$ be a diffuse non-separable abelian tracial von Neumann algebra.  The
source proves that the free powers $A^{*n}$, $2\le n\le\infty$, are mutually
non-isomorphic, and that finite free powers have trivial fundamental group.
In Section~4.3 it asks for an identification of $(A^{*n})^t$ at arbitrary
$t>0$.  For homogeneous $A$ it proves only that, for $n\ge2$ and
$k\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 (A^{*n})^{1/k}
 \cong A^{*(nk)}*L\mathbb F_{,1+nk(k-1)-k^2}.
\]
It also obtains
$\mathbb Q\subseteq\Fcal(A^{*\infty})$ and records the expectation
$\Fcal(A^{*\infty})=\mathbb R_+^*$.

\section*{The later all-scale answer}

Dykema--Zhao first prove that every diffuse abelian tracial von Neumann
algebra is \emph{self-symmetric} (Theorem~2.8 of \cite{DZ}); thus their
hypothesis applies to every $A$ in the source problem, without an additional
homogeneity assumption.  They then define factors $\Fcal_{s,r}(A)$ for
\[
 s>0,\qquad 1-s<r\le\infty,
\]
such that $\Fcal_{n,0}(A)\cong A^{*n}$ for every integer $n\ge2$.  Their
Theorem~3.5 proves the rescaling formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescale}
 (\Fcal_{s,r}(A))^t
 \cong
 \Fcal_{s/t,\,(s+r-1)/t^2-s/t+1}(A)
 \qquad(t>0).
\end{equation}
Specializing \eqref{eq:rescale} to $s=n$ and $r=0$ gives the requested full
identification
\begin{equation}\label{eq:answer}
 \boxed{
 (A^{*n})^t
 \cong
 \Fcal_{n/t,\,(n-1)/t^2-n/t+1}(A)
 }
 \qquad(n\ge2,\ t>0).
\end{equation}
For $t=1/k$, the first parameter becomes the integer $nk$, and
\eqref{eq:answer} recovers the source formula.

Finally, Theorem~4.5 of \cite{DZ} proves
\[
 \boxed{\Fcal(A^{*\infty})=\mathbb R_+^*},
\]
upgrading the source's rational subgroup to the full positive multiplicative
group.  The abstract of \cite{DZ} explicitly says that these conclusions
answer two questions in Section~4.3 of \cite{BDIP}.

\section*{Why the interpolation is essential}

The sans-rank amplification law assigns $(A^{*n})^t$ non-separable rank
$n/t$.  When $n/t$ is not an integer, no formula involving only an ordinary
integer number of unital copies of $A$ can encode that parameter.  The family
$\Fcal_{s,r}(A)$ supplies exactly the missing continuous non-separable
multiplicity and proves that it behaves coherently under every rescaling.

\section*{Scope}

The amplification subsection is fully resolved.  This status packet makes no
claim about the source's distinct questions concerning freely complemented
maximal amenable MASAs, the outer automorphism group of $A^{*n}$, or
finite-index subfactors.
