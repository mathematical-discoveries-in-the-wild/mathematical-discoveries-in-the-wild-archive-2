# Commutativity of the natural quaternion convolution

Candidate full solution to the open problem on page 26 of arXiv:2402.06645.

The packet proves that `f ostar g = g ostar f` exactly when the imaginary
vectors of the quaternion Fourier transforms are pointwise collinear.  It
also supplies explicit real and complex component equations, a time-domain
criterion, and examples outside both sufficient cases listed in the source.

Files:

- `solution_packet.pdf` — final proof packet.
- `main.tex` — packet source.
- `source_paper.pdf` — arXiv source PDF.
- `figures/source_open_problem.png` — visually verified source passage.
- `verification.md` — mathematical, novelty, build, and visual checks.
