\noindent\textbf{Status: literature already answered (full Dirichlet-space
subcase).}

\section*{Original question}

In Section~7.5, PDF pages~26--27, of their survey on complete Pick
algebras, Salomon and Shalit ask for the minimal
$d\in\{1,2,\ldots,\infty\}$ such that an irreducible complete Pick algebra
$\mathcal A$ is isomorphic to $\mathcal M_V$ for some
$V\subseteq\B_d$.  They emphasize that the answer was unknown even for
$\mathcal A=\Mult(\D)$, the multiplier algebra of the classical Dirichlet
space \cite{SS}.

\section*{Explicit answer}

Hartz explicitly identifies his result as an answer to the question raised
in Salomon--Shalit Section~7.5 \cite[p.~2]{Hartz}.  His Theorem~4.2, on PDF
page~16, states:
\begin{quote}
There do not exist $d\in\mathbb N$ and $V\subseteq\B_d$ such that
$\Mult(\D)$ is algebraically isomorphic to
$\Mult(H^2_d|_V)$.
\end{quote}
Since $\mathcal M_V=\Mult(H^2_d|_V)$ in the survey's notation, this excludes
every finite embedding dimension under precisely the algebraic-isomorphism
notion asked about.  The universal complete-Pick representation, also
reviewed in both papers, provides an embedding into $\B_\infty$.
Consequently the minimal dimension for $\Mult(\D)$ is exactly
\[
 d_{\min}(\Mult(\D))=\infty.
\]

The supporting authors knew they were answering the source question; this is
therefore an explicit literature answer rather than an agent-inferred
reformulation.

\section*{Scope}

The Dirichlet-space case of the source question is completely resolved.  The
broader request to determine embedding dimension for arbitrary irreducible
complete Pick algebras is not resolved by this identification.
