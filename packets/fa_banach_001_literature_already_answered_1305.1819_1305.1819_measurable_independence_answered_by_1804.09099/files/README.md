# arXiv:1305.1819 — measurable stability formulation answered by arXiv:1804.09099

Status: already answered in later literature.

The 2013 source asks in its `Future work` section for a copositive formulation of the measurable stability number, with the sphere orthogonality graph as the main example.

DeCorte, de Oliveira Filho, and Vallentin, *Complete positivity and distance-avoiding sets* (arXiv:1804.09099), provide the exact requested framework:

- Section 2 identifies Witsenhausen's sphere problem as a measurable independence number.
- Their general completely-positive kernel program is the measurable infinite-graph analogue of the finite stability-number formulation.
- Section 4 identifies its dual cone as the copositive kernels.
- Theorem 5.1 proves exactness and is immediately applied to all single-angle sphere graphs, including orthogonality at angle `pi/2`.
- Theorem 1.1 states the exact spherical formulation directly.

The numerical value of Witsenhausen's parameter remains open; the requested exact conic formulation does not.
