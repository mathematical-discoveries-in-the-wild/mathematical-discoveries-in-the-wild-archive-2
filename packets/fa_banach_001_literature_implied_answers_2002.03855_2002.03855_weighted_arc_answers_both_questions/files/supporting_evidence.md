# Supporting evidence

## Source question

- Ruxi Shi, arXiv:2002.03855, Section 6, final two questions (source TeX lines
  516--524 in the cached extraction).
- The first asks for a singular continuous frame-spectral measure attaining
  equality between supremal restricted Fourier dimension and infimal
  restricted upper entropy dimension.
- The second asks for a singular continuous frame-spectral measure with
  nonzero Fourier dimension.

Official page: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03855>

## Later construction

- Longhui Li and Bochen Liu, arXiv:2506.01280, introduction, source TeX lines
  175--179: the weighted unit-circle arc is spectral with spectrum
  `Z x {0}` and is one-dimensional Salem.
- Section 10, starting at source TeX line 1152, proves the uniform decay
  `|hat sigma(xi)| <= C |xi|^{-1/2}`.
- The bibliography cites Shi's paper, and source TeX line 287 invokes an
  argument of Shi for a different density criterion. The paper does not
  identify the arc as an answer to Shi's two final questions.

Official page: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01280>
