# Verification audit

## 1. Exact source match

The attached arXiv PDF states Theorem 1.2 and the open question on PDF page 2.
The quantifiers inherited from Theorem 1.1 are: for every positive `f`
supported in `S`, there exists an integer `n=n(f)>=1` such that the support of
`T^n f` contains that of `T^(n-1) f`. The proof treats exactly these
quantifiers.

## 2. Fixed modulus

For a peripheral eigenvector `Tz=lambda z`, positivity gives
`T|z|>=|Tz|=|z|`. Hence `T^n|z|` is increasing and norm bounded. Since
`L^p`, `1<=p<infinity`, is a KB-space, it converges in norm to a fixed vector
`y`.

A weak-* cluster point of the Cesaro averages of `(T')^k psi`, where
`psi(y)>0`, is a nonzero positive fixed functional `phi`. Its absolute kernel
is a closed `T`-invariant ideal, hence is zero by irreducibility. Thus `phi`
is strictly positive. Applying it to the positive vector
`T|z|-|z|` proves equality. The closed ideal generated by `|z|` is then
nonzero and invariant, so it is dense; on `L^p` this is equivalent to
`|z|>0` almost everywhere.

## 3. Normalized map

For `e=|z|`, define

```text
R(g) = T(e g)/e  on L^infinity.
```

This is well-defined because
`|T(eg)|<=T(e|g|)<=||g||_infinity e`. It is positive, unital, contractive,
and sends the unitary `u=z/e` to `lambda u`.

## 4. Multiplicative domain

A positive map whose domain is a commutative C*-algebra is completely
positive. For the unitary `u`, both Schwarz inequalities are equalities:

```text
R(u* u)=1=(Ru)* Ru,
R(u u*)=1=(Ru)(Ru)*.
```

Thus `u` lies in the multiplicative domain. For continuous `b` on the circle
and arbitrary bounded `h`,

```text
R(b(u)h)=b(lambda u)R(h).
```

Induction gives
`R^n(1_S b(u))=b(lambda^n u)R^n(1_S)`.

## 5. Existence of a nonzero narrow phase slice

Because `lambda!=1`, the circle has a finite cover by smaller arcs, each
contained in an arc whose closure is disjoint from its `lambda`-rotate.
Choose continuous nonnegative bumps supported in the larger arcs and positive
on the smaller cover. Since `S` has positive measure, at least one bump `b`
satisfies `S intersect {b(u)>0}` of positive measure. Then
`f=e 1_S b(u)` is a nonzero positive element of `L^p` supported in `S`.

## 6. Support contradiction

The iterate formula confines `T^n f` to `{b(lambda^n u)>0}`. The choice of
`b` makes the confinement sets for `n-1` and `n` disjoint for every `n>=1`.
The strictly positive fixed functional gives
`phi(T^n f)=phi(f)>0`, so every iterate is nonzero. Consequently no
consecutive support can contain its predecessor, contradicting the proposed
hypothesis for this one `f`.

## 7. Scope

The proof establishes the exact `L^p`, `1<=p<infinity`, question following
Theorem 1.2. It does not claim to solve the paper's separate question about
removing weak almost periodicity on an arbitrary Banach lattice, nor the
general cyclic-peripheral-spectrum problem.
