# Literature answer: strong continuity is unnecessary in the zero-two law

Status: `literature_already_answered`

## Identification

After proving the local zero-two law for strongly continuous cosine families,
Schwenninger and Zwart (arXiv:1402.1304, PDF page 2) ask whether strong
continuity can be removed.

Chojnacki's *Around Schwenninger and Zwart's zero-two law for cosine families*
(Archiv der Mathematik 106 (2016), Theorem 3, PDF page 8) answers yes for the
strictly larger setting of arbitrary cosine families in unital normed
algebras.  Esterle's arXiv:1505.06065 independently gives a short direct proof
for unital Banach algebras and explicitly identifies the same source question.

## Scope

The answer is complete: if `limsup_{t->0} ||C(t)-I|| < 2`, then
`||C(t)-I|| -> 0`, with no measurability or continuity assumption.  Both
supporting authors know they are addressing the Schwenninger--Zwart extension.
