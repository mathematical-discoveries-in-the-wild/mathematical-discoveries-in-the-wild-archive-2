\paragraph{Status.}
The focused open question in arXiv:1008.3944 is already answered by
arXiv:1612.01893.  This note records the exact identification; it does not claim
a new result.

\paragraph{Source question.}
Luis Rademacher, \emph{On the monotonicity of the expected volume of a random
simplex}, states in the introduction that the dimension-three case was left
open after proving monotonicity in dimensions one and two and disproving it in
dimensions at least four.  The Discussion (item 2, source PDF p.~16) asks
verbatim:
\begin{quote}
``(3-D case) For Meckes's strong conjecture, find an easy argument to disprove
it for $d=3$.''
\end{quote}
Here the conjecture asserts that $K\subseteq L$ implies
$\mathbb E V_K\leq \mathbb E V_L$ for the volume of the simplex spanned by four
i.i.d. uniform points in a three-dimensional convex body.

\paragraph{Exact later answer.}
Stefan Kunis, Benjamin Reichenwallner, and Matthias Reitzner,
\emph{Monotonicity of the Sample Range of 3-D Data: Moments of Volumes of
Random Tetrahedra}, arXiv:1612.01893, proves the following as Theorem~1
(supporting PDF p.~2).
\begin{theorem}
In $\mathbb R^3$ the expected volume of the sample range is in general not
monotone in the underlying distribution: there are three-dimensional convex
sets $L\subset K$ for which the expected volume of the tetrahedron generated by
four uniform points is larger in $L$ than in $K$.
\end{theorem}
The strict inequality driving the construction is Proposition~1 (supporting
PDF p.~3):
\begin{proposition}
For a volume-one tetrahedron $T$, the centroid $c$ of one of its facets, and
i.i.d. uniform $X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4\in T$,
\[
 \mathbb E\,\lvert\operatorname{conv}(X_1,X_2,X_3,c)\rvert
 < \frac{13}{720}-\frac{\pi^2}{15015}
 = \mathbb E\,\lvert\operatorname{conv}(X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4)\rvert.
\]
\end{proposition}
Combined with Rademacher's boundary-point characterization, this yields an
infinitesimal convex-body variation that violates monotonicity.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The later theorem is precisely the negative answer requested in
arXiv:1008.3944.  The target is therefore classified as
\texttt{literature\_already\_answered}; no independent solution packet is
warranted.
