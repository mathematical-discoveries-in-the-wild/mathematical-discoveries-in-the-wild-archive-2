\noindent\textbf{Status.}
\texttt{literature\_already\_answered}: automatic weak-star continuity of
left multipliers of dual operator spaces, and the equivalent module-action
question.

\section*{The exact questions}

Let $X$ be a dual operator space.  A left multiplier of $X$ is a linear map
$u:X\to X$ which, after a completely isometric realization
$\sigma:X\to A$ in a $C^*$-algebra, is implemented by left multiplication:
$\sigma(u x)=a\sigma(x)$.  The target paper proves weak-star continuity for
left \emph{adjointable} multipliers but states that it does not know whether
every $u\in\mathcal M_l(X)$ is weak-star continuous.  It therefore calls
$X$ \emph{left normal} when all of its left multipliers have this property
\cite{BM01}.

Later in the same paper, the authors ask the equivalent module question.  If
$m:B\times X\to X$ is a left operator-module action of a dual operator
algebra and $m$ is weak-star continuous in its first variable, must it also
be weak-star continuous in the second?  Their corollary proves that a
counterexample exists if and only if some dual operator space has a left
multiplier which is not weak-star continuous \cite{BM01}.

\section*{The later theorem}

Blecher and Magajna subsequently proved the exact automatic-continuity
statement:
\[
 \boxed{\text{Every left multiplier of a dual operator space is
 weak-star continuous.}}
\]
This is Theorem~4.1 of arXiv:math/0407220, later published in the
\emph{Journal of Functional Analysis} \cite{BM05}.  Their proof uses the
canonical completely contractive projection $q:X^{**}\to X$ associated
with the given predual.  For $u\in\mathcal M_l(X)$ they establish
\[
 q\bigl(u^{**}(\eta)\bigr)=u\bigl(q(\eta)\bigr)
 \qquad(\eta\in X^{**}),
\]
which is precisely the bidual criterion forcing $u$ to be weak-star
continuous.  The paper explicitly records that the earlier ``left normal''
hypothesis is consequently automatic and can be removed.

The target's own equivalence now settles its second question immediately.
Indeed, the theorem rules out a non-weak-star-continuous left multiplier on
any dual operator space.  Hence a left module action as above which is
weak-star continuous in the algebra variable cannot fail weak-star
continuity in the space variable.  Such actions are separately weak-star
continuous.

\section*{Conclusion and scope}

The selected pair of questions from arXiv:0012032 received a complete
affirmative answer in arXiv:math/0407220.  This classification is deliberately
narrow.  The target also asks two different representation questions:
whether a unital operator algebra which is merely a dual Banach space has an
isometric weak-star-homeomorphic concrete representation, and whether a
unital function algebra with a Banach predual can be represented as a
weak-star-closed subalgebra of an $L^\infty$ space.  The multiplier theorem
does not answer either representation question, and this note makes no
claim about their present status.
