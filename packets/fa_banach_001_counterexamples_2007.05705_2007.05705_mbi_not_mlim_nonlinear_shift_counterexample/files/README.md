# MBI is strictly weaker than MLIM: a nonlinear shift counterexample

Status: candidate full counterexample, likely valid, pending human review.

On `X=ell_infinity(N)`, set

`g(t)=t^2` for `0<=t<=1` and `g(t)=(t+1)/2` for `t>=1`,

and define `A(x)_1=0`, `A(x)_{n+1}=g(x_n)`.  The packet proves that `A` is a
2-Lipschitz monotone max-type gain operator generated by class-`K_infinity`
edge gains, that `id-A` has the monotone bounded invertibility property, and
that the associated discrete-time inequality does not have the monotone limit
property.  Thus the open dichotomy in arXiv:2007.05705 has a negative answer:
MBI is strictly weaker than MLIM.
