# Infinite-rank LCE dominated-convergence counterexample

Candidate full negative answer to the open problem in Remark 4.6 of
arXiv:2008.12070.

The construction combines:

- a classical Menshov orthogonal series with square-summable coefficients and
  pointwise-divergent partial sums;
- disjoint-support tails that converge pointwise under one `L^2` dominator;
- a graph-subspace projection transporting the harmless tails to the Menshov
  tails;
- an `ell^2`-valued conditioning variable whose affine coordinate closure is
  the graph and whose covariance has infinite rank.
