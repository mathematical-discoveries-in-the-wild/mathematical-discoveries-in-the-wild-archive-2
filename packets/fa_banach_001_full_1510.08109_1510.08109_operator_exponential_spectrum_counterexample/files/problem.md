# Source problem

Hubert Klaja and Thomas Ransford, *Non-commutativity of the exponential
spectrum*, arXiv:1510.08109, Question 4.2, PDF page 7:

> Do there exist a Banach space E and operators S,T on E such that
> epsilon(ST) minus {0} is not equal to epsilon(TS) minus {0}?

For a unital complex Banach algebra `A`, let `Exp(A)` be the identity
component of its invertible group, equivalently the finite products of
exponentials. The exponential spectrum of `x` is

```text
epsilon(x) = {lambda in C : lambda I - x is not in Exp(A)}.
```

The packet gives a positive answer with a separable complex Banach space and
the explicit separating value `lambda=1/2`.

The preceding Question 4.1, asking whether every Banach algebra exhibiting
such failure must have torsion in its index group, is not claimed to be
settled.
