# References and search record

## Used in the proof

1. Hubert Klaja and Thomas Ransford, *Non-commutativity of the exponential
   spectrum*, Journal of Functional Analysis 272 (2017), 4158-4164;
   arXiv:1510.08109. Theorem 1.1 supplies the explicit non-null-homotopic
   matrix-function pair, and Question 4.2 is on PDF page 7.
2. Pavlos Motakis, *Separable Spaces of Continuous Functions as Calkin
   Algebras*, arXiv:2110.10868. Proposition 4.1 supplies bounded Lipschitz
   diagonal lifts; Proposition 4.3 and Corollary 5.3 identify their Calkin
   classes and the full quotient with `C(K)`.

## Closest later literature found

3. Sukumar Daniel and Arindam Ghosh, *A note on commutativity of the
   exponential spectrum in the operator algebra B(ell^p direct-sum ell^q)*,
   Acta Mathematica Hungarica 177 (2025), 280-296,
   DOI 10.1007/s10474-025-01571-x. The abstract proves commutativity for that
   special operator algebra and states a one-way transfer from Calkin
   commutativity. It does not state a positive existence answer to Question
   4.2.
