Status: `substantial partial toward the b^{2/3}_{3,infinity} conjecture`

The source conjectures that its (W^{2/3,3}) regularity and developability
results extend to the larger little Besov space
(b^{2/3}_{3,infty}). This packet proves the complete mollification and
geometric compactness layer:

- sharp `o(epsilon^{s-k})` mollifier estimates in little Besov spaces;
- quadratic commutator convergence in `W^{4/3,3/2}`;
- a critical uniform VMO theorem and uniform recovery of compact target
  manifolds under mollification;
- uniform recovery of the Stiefel constraint and nondegeneracy of mollified
  isometric immersions;
- all critical metric estimates used by the source;
- a canonical weak second fundamental form, weak Codazzi equations, and local
  little-Besov potential;
- distributional vanishing of the canonical smooth Gauss determinants.

The full conjecture is not claimed. The remaining endpoint problem is to make
the compensated Jacobian intrinsic at Besov fine index infinity and prove the
planar continuity/degree/developability theorem. Little-scale vanishing does
not supply the scale summability used in the source's `W^{2/3,3}` argument.
