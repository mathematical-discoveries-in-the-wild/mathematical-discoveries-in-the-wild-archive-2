## Identification

Conjecture 4.4 on source-PDF page 24 asks whether every bounded sequence in a
JBW*-triple predual admits, after passing to a subsequence, a Peirce-2 splitting
along mutually orthogonal tripotents with relatively weakly compact remainder.

The supporting paper's introduction explicitly says that its main Theorem 6.1 proves
the source's Conjecture 4.4. Theorem 6.1 gives a weakly convergent component and a
pairwise orthogonal component. Taking support tripotents and applying its Proposition
4.3(v) recovers the source's exact Peirce-2 formulation.

## Scope

This resolves Conjecture 4.4 affirmatively for all JBW*-triples and all bounded
sequences in their preduals. The supporting paper separately leaves open how to
characterize general L-embedded spaces having an analogous splitting property; that
broader problem is outside the source conjecture.
