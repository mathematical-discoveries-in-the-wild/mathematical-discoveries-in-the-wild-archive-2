\textbf{Status.} \texttt{literature\_already\_answered}. This note records an
explicit later-literature solution; it does not claim a new proof.

\section*{The source conjecture}

Fern\'andez-Polo, Peralta, and Ram\'irez, arXiv:1211.4990, Conjecture 4.4
(source-PDF page 24), ask the following. Given a bounded sequence
$(\phi_n)$ in the predual $W_*$ of a JBW$^*$-triple $W$, do there exist a
subsequence $(\phi_{\tau(n)})$ and mutually orthogonal tripotents $(e_n)$
such that
\[
 \left\{\phi_{\tau(n)}-\phi_{\tau(n)}P_2(e_n):n\in\mathbb N\right\}
 \quad\text{is relatively weakly compact?}
\]
Equivalently, the desired decomposition is
$\phi_{\tau(n)}=\xi_n+\psi_n$ with
$\psi_n=\phi_{\tau(n)}P_2(e_n)$ and
$\xi_n=\phi_{\tau(n)}-\psi_n$ \cite{source}.

\section*{The exact later answer}

Peralta and Pfitzner explicitly state in their introduction that their main result
``gives a positive answer'' to an earlier question and ``a proof to
[21, Conjecture 4.4]''; their reference [21] is precisely the source paper.
Their Theorem 6.1 (supporting-PDF page 16, journal page 92) proves that a
subsequence has a decomposition
\[
  \phi_{n_k}=\psi_k+\xi_k,
\]
where the $\psi_k$ are pairwise orthogonal normal functionals and
$(\xi_k)$ converges weakly in $W_*$ \cite{answer}.

For completeness, this statement yields the source's exact Peirce formulation.
Let $e_k=s(\psi_k)$ be the support tripotent. Then the $e_k$ are mutually
orthogonal and $\psi_k=\psi_kP_2(e_k)$. The set
$K=\{\xi_k:k\in\mathbb N\}$ is relatively weakly compact. Proposition 4.3(v)
of the supporting paper says that for every orthogonal sequence $(e_k)$,
\[
 \sup_{\xi\in K}\|\xi P_2(e_k)\|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \phi_{n_k}-\phi_{n_k}P_2(e_k)
   =\xi_k-\xi_kP_2(e_k).
\]
This is a weakly convergent sequence perturbed by a norm-null sequence, hence
its range is relatively weakly compact. Thus Conjecture 4.4 is affirmative in
full generality.

\section*{Scope}

The identification is explicit, not inferred only from similar terminology, and
the source and supporting PDFs are preserved beside this note. The later paper
also asks for reasonable conditions guaranteeing such splitting in arbitrary
$L$-embedded spaces. That broader classification problem is not part of
Conjecture 4.4 and is not claimed resolved here.
