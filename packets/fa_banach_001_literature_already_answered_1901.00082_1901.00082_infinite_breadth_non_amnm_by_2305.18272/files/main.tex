Literature already answered: full affirmative answer.

\section*{Source question}

Yemon Choi, Mahya Ghandehari, and Hung Le Pham ask in Section~4 of
\emph{Stability of characters and filters for weighted semilattices}
(arXiv:1901.00082; source PDF page~13):
\[
 \boxed{\begin{minipage}{0.84\linewidth}
 Let \(S\) be a semilattice with infinite breadth.  Does there exist a
 log-weight on \(S\) for which propagation fails at some level?
 \end{minipage}}
\]
Their Theorem~4.8 answers this only when \(S\) is isomorphic to a
subsemilattice of the finite-subset semilattice
\((\operatorname{FIN}(\Omega),\cup)\).  By their AMNM--propagation
equivalence, the full question is equivalent to asking whether every
infinite-breadth semilattice admits a submultiplicative weight \(\omega\)
for which \(\ell^1(S,\omega)\) is not AMNM.

\section*{Exact later answer}

The same three authors return to this exact question in
\emph{Constructing non-AMNM weighted convolution algebras for every
semilattice of infinite breadth} (arXiv:2305.18272).  In its introduction
they quote the general question, identify their earlier Theorem~4.8 as a
partial answer, and explicitly say that the new article extends that partial
answer to a full answer.  Their Theorem~B on supporting PDF page~3 states:
if \(S\) has infinite breadth, then one can construct a submultiplicative
weight \(\omega\) such that \(\ell^1(S,\omega)\) is not AMNM.

Thus the answer to the source question is affirmative for every semilattice
of infinite breadth, with no additional representability hypothesis on
\(S\).
