## Exact source question

Remark 2.4 of arXiv:2011.04140 asks whether the Benyamini approximation in
Proposition 2.3 can preserve the lattice-isometry group, or even a subgroup.
The result here proves preservation as a conjugate subgroup whenever the
subgroup fixes a quasi-interior point, and therefore for all SOT-compact
subgroups.

## Proof checks

- The canonical compact carrier
  `H = Hom_lat(X,R) intersect B_(X*)` is weak-star compact: the lattice
  homomorphism identities are weak-star closed. Its evaluation representation
  is lattice isometric for an AM-space.
- A quasi-interior point `e` is strictly positive on every nonzero positive
  lattice homomorphism. Hence its evaluation vanishes only at the common zero
  of the canonical representation.
- The level sets defined by `C^{-n} <= phi(e) <= C^{1-n}` are compact and
  invariant under `phi -> phi o g` whenever `g e = e`.
- The multiplier `C^{1-n}/phi(e)` belongs to `[1,C]`, giving exactly the
  source distortion bounds.
- The identity `S(gx)=Sx o alpha_g` is pointwise exact. It implies invariance
  of the within-level quotient relation used to create the compact pieces
  `K_n`, so the group action descends to the final Benyamini carrier.
- Composition with each descended homeomorphism is a surjective lattice
  isometry. Thus `A G A^{-1}` is contained in the new lattice-isometry group.
- Conjugation by fixed bounded inverse isomorphisms is a homeomorphism onto
  its image for the strong operator topology.

## Compact-group check

- Every separable Banach lattice has a quasi-interior point `e_0`.
- For an SOT-compact subgroup, `g -> g e_0` is norm-continuous and has a
  Bochner Haar average `e` in the positive cone.
- Haar invariance makes `e` group-fixed.
- For every nonzero positive functional `f`, the continuous nonnegative
  function `g -> f(g e_0)` is positive at the identity. Its Haar integral is
  therefore positive, so `f(e)>0`.
- Strict positivity against all nonzero positive functionals implies that the
  principal ideal generated by `e` is dense; hence `e` is quasi-interior.

## Scope guard

The packet proves subgroup inclusion, not equality with the entire new
isometry group. It leaves open noncompact groups without a fixed
quasi-interior point. These limitations are explicit in the theorem packet
and ledger search text.
