# Compact subgroups survive the Benyamini approximation

This packet gives a strong partial affirmative answer to Remark 2.4 of
Oikhberg and Tursi, arXiv:2011.04140.

If a subgroup `G` of the lattice-isometry group of a separable AM-space fixes
a quasi-interior point, the construction in Proposition 2.3 can be performed
equivariantly. The resulting Benyamini space contains the conjugate subgroup
`A G A^{-1}` in its lattice-isometry group, with the same arbitrarily small
distortion as in the source proposition.

Every SOT-compact subgroup has a fixed quasi-interior point, obtained by Haar
averaging. Consequently every compact subgroup, and in particular every
finite subgroup, can be preserved. This also preserves the entire original
isometry group whenever that group is compact.

The result does not show that the new isometry group is exactly the prescribed
group, and it does not settle arbitrary noncompact subgroups.
