# Remark 4.5 of arXiv:1305.5146 answered, with a necessary scope correction, by arXiv:1405.1276

Status: `literature_already_answered_scoped`

Applebaum--van Neerven ask in Remark 4.5 on source PDF page 14 whether their
Gaussian and pure-jump second-quantisation theorems extend to arbitrary
infinitely divisible measures.

Their separate follow-up arXiv:1405.1276 explicitly cites the source and says
in its introduction (PDF page 2) that it “complete[s] the programme” for
general infinitely divisible measures. Theorem 3.7 on supporting PDF page 10
constructs the combined Gaussian--Poisson Fock-space representation over
`H_gamma direct_sum L^2(E,nu)` and intertwines the transition operator with
the symmetric second quantisation of `T*`.

The exact scope is important. Theorem 3.7 assumes that the skew convolution
factor is itself infinitely divisible. Proposition 2.1 then splits the skew
relation into Gaussian and pure-jump skew relations. Example 2.2 (Rosiński) on
supporting PDF page 4 shows that infinite divisibility of the two endpoint
measures does not force infinite divisibility of the convolution factor.
Thus the follow-up gives the affirmative natural theorem with an
infinitely-divisible-factor hypothesis; it does not prove that the unrestricted
arbitrary-factor wording follows from endpoint infinite divisibility alone.
