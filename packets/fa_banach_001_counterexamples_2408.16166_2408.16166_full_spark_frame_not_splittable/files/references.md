# References and novelty audit

## Primary sources

- Xuemei Chen, Christian Kummerle, and Rongrong Wang, *Sparse Recovery for
  Overcomplete Frames: Sensing Matrices and Recovery Guarantees*,
  arXiv:2408.16166. Definition 3.5 states splittability; the following
  paragraph conjectures that all full-spark frames are splittable.
- Xuemei Chen, *A Unified Recovery of Structured Signals Using Atomic Norm*,
  Information and Inference 13 (2024), iaad050; arXiv:2207.06484. Definition
  2.5 introduces splittability and explicitly notes that best sparse
  approximants may be nonunique.
