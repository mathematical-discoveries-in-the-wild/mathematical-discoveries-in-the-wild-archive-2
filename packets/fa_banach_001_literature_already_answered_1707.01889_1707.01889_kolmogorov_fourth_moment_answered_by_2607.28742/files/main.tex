Remark~1.4(b)--(c) of arXiv:1707.01889 asks for a Kolmogorov fourth-moment

finite fourth moment.  Theorem~1.9 of arXiv:2607.28742 proves precisely
\[
 d_{\K}(F,N)\le 15.6\sqrt{\E[F^4]-3}
\]
for every variance-one random variable in a fixed Poisson chaos with finite

\end{center}

\section{Question in the source}

Let $F$ belong to the $q$th Poisson Wiener chaos, let
$\sigma^2=\E[F^2]>0$, and assume only $\E[F^4]<\infty$.  Theorem~1.2 of
D\"obler, Vidotto, and Zheng~\cite{source} gives the Wasserstein estimate
\[
 d_{\W}(F,\sigma N)\le C_{q,\sigma}
 \sqrt{\E[F^4]-3\sigma^4}.
\]
On printed page~4, Remark~1.4(b) observes that the generic comparison between
Kolmogorov and Wasserstein distances loses a square root and yields only a
fourth-root estimate.  Remark~1.4(c) then asks whether one can obtain a
Kolmogorov bound with the same square-root accuracy.  At that time such a
bound was known only under an additional local regularity assumption.

The mathematically concrete issue is therefore whether the extra regularity
can be removed while retaining a bound proportional to the square root of the
fourth cumulant.

\section{Exact later answer}

Zheng~\cite{answer} states this problem as the motivation of the paper on its
first page.  Theorem~1.9 (printed page~5) says: if $q\ge1$ and
$F$ lies in the $q$th Poisson chaos, $F\in L^4$, and $\E[F^2]=1$, then
\[
 d_{\K}(F,N)\le 15.6\sqrt{\E[F^4]-3}.
\]
This has exactly the requested exponent and assumes no regularity beyond the
finite fourth moment.  The paragraph following the theorem explicitly says
that it removes Assumptions~A and $\mathbf A^{\mathrm{loc}}$ from the earlier
Kolmogorov estimate of D\"obler and Peccati.

The mechanism is a fixed-chaos martingale core.  Theorem~1.7 constructs
conditional expectations $F_n$ which simultaneously remain in the original
Poisson chaos, have bounded step kernels with finite-measure support, and
converge to $F$ in $L^4$.  The regular Kolmogorov estimate applies to $F_n$;
fourth cumulants converge, and lower semicontinuity of Kolmogorov distance
(Lemma~2.2) passes the estimate to $F$.  Thus the later theorem supplies the
missing closure argument rather than imposing the old local assumption.

For a non-unit variance $\sigma^2$, applying the theorem to $F/\sigma$ gives
the equivalent scaled statement
\[
 d_{\K}(F,\sigma N)
 \le \frac{15.6}{\sigma^2}
 \sqrt{\E[F^4]-3\sigma^4}.
\]

\section{Scope}

This is a direct answer to the Poisson-chaos fourth-moment problem motivating
Remark~1.4: the desired Kolmogorov accuracy holds under the weakest stated
moment assumption.  The 2026 proof is not advertised as a general black-box
Kolmogorov theorem for every exchangeable-pair construction; it combines a
martingale-core approximation with the established regular Poisson-chaos
bound.  Hence the concrete bound is settled, while the broadest possible
methodological reading of ``via exchangeable pairs'' is not claimed here.
