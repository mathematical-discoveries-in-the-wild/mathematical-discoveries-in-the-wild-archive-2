# Dimension-free total coherence for Parseval frames

Source question: PDF page 25 of Jameson Cahill and Peter G. Casazza,
*Optimal Parseval frames: Total coherence and total volume*, arXiv:1910.01733.

Result: the proposed dimension-free lower bound exists over both real and
complex scalars.  A Haar-random rank-\(N\) projection supplies a random
Parseval-frame Gram matrix.  Its total coherence has an exact gamma-function
expectation and is at least
\[
  \frac{2\sqrt2}{\pi^{3/2}}\sqrt{N(M-N)(M-1)}.
\]
Therefore some Parseval frame realizes this bound.
