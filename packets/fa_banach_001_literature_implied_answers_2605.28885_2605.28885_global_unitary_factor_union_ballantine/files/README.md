# Global attainable unitary factors: literature-implied characterization

Status: **literature_implied_answer (full characterization of the stated global union)**.

This is not an original mathematical result of the run. It is a direct identification between:

- Trung Dung Vuong, *Polar Fidelities, Holevo Bases, and Unitary Factors of Generalized Fidelity*, arXiv:2605.28885 (2026), Theorem 5.9 and Discussion, PDF p. 27; and
- A. R. Sourour, *A Factorization Theorem for Matrices*, Linear and Multilinear Algebra 19(2) (1986), 141–147, Theorem 2 (Ballantine).

For dimension `d`,

`union_{P,Q>0} U_att(P,Q) = SU(d) \ {omega I : omega^d=1, omega!=1}`.

The bridge is that global attainability is equivalent to factorization into four positive-definite matrices. Sourour's Theorem 2 classifies those products exactly.

The source's other open directions—full data processing, related convexity, and semidefinite-program formulations—remain open.
