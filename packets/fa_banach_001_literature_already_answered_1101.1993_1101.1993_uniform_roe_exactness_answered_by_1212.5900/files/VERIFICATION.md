# Verification

## Source and answer

- Source arXiv:1101.1993v3, Section 5.2, PDF page 13, asks whether exactness
  of a bounded-geometry uniform Roe algebra implies nuclearity.
- Sako arXiv:1212.5900v4, Theorem 1.1 on PDF page 1, states for every
  bounded-geometry metric space `X` that Property A, nuclearity of `C_u^*(X)`,
  exactness of `C_u^*(X)`, local reflexivity, and operator norm localization
  are equivalent.
- The abstract and introduction explicitly say that exactness for general
  bounded-geometry spaces had been open until the draft was announced.

## Scope

- The source's specific box space does not have Property A.  Sako therefore
  implies its reduced uniform Roe algebra is neither nuclear nor exact.
- This does not answer the source's Section 5.1 maximal-versus-reduced
  isomorphism question.  Willett arXiv:1504.05615, Remark 3.5, still describes
  the coarse-space equality problem as open and says the nonamenable groupoid
  construction does not resolve it.
- A bounded arXiv and citation-neighborhood search through 2026-08-11 found no
  later primary theorem settling that particular iterated-square box space.
