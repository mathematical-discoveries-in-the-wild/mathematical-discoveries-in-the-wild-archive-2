\section*{Status}

\textbf{Literature already answered (affirmative), with limited scope.}
Sako's Theorem 1.1 answers the general exactness-versus-nuclearity question
from Section 5.2 of Arzhantseva--Guentner--Spakula.  The distinct question in
Section 5.1 about equality of maximal and reduced uniform Roe algebras is not
claimed here.

\section*{Source question}

For a bounded-geometry metric space $X$, let $C_u^*(X)$ be the norm closure on
$\ell_2(X)$ of the finite-propagation matrices with uniformly bounded entries.
On PDF page 13 of arXiv:1101.1993, the authors recall that
\[
 X\text{ has Property A}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad C_u^*(X)
 \text{ is nuclear}
\]
and ask whether, for arbitrary bounded-geometry $X$,
\[
 C_u^*(X)\text{ exact}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad C_u^*(X)\text{ nuclear}.
\]
They also ask whether the uniform Roe algebra of their particular
coarsely-Hilbert-embeddable, non-Property-A box space is exact, before noting a
box-space argument that already makes it nonexact.

\section*{Explicit answering theorem}

Sako, arXiv:1212.5900, Theorem 1.1 (PDF page 1), proves that for every metric
space $X$ with bounded geometry the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $X$ has Property A;
  \item $C_u^*(X)$ is nuclear;
  \item $C_u^*(X)$ is exact;
  \item $C_u^*(X)$ is locally reflexive;
  \item $X$ has the operator norm localization property.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, exactness implies nuclearity, so the source's general question
has an affirmative answer.  Sako's abstract and introduction explicitly say
that exactness for general bounded-geometry spaces had been open until the
draft was announced, making this an exact literature answer rather than a
coincidental theorem match.

For the source's box space
\[
 X=\bigsqcup_n \operatorname{Cay}(F_2/\Gamma_n),\qquad
 \Gamma_{n+1}=\langle g^2:g\in\Gamma_n\rangle,
\]
the source proves failure of Property A.  Sako's equivalence therefore gives
that $C_u^*(X)$ is neither nuclear nor exact.

\section*{Scope limitation}

Section 5.1 separately asks whether
\[
 C^*_{u,\max}(X)\longrightarrow C_u^*(X)
\]
is an isomorphism for this same box space.  Coarse Hilbert embeddability gives
an isomorphism on K-theory, not injectivity of the algebra map.  Willett's
arXiv:1504.05615, Remark 3.5, still describes the coarse-space equality
problem as open and explains that his nonamenable groupoid with equal maximal
and reduced algebras does not resolve the coarse case.  A bounded search
through 2026-08-11 found no later exact answer for the iterated-square box
space.  The present run records a separate serious attempt, but promotes no
claim on that companion question.

\section*{Search evidence}

The statements were checked in the official PDFs and parsed sources of the
three cited arXiv papers.  Searches for the exact question, titles, authors,
maximal uniform Roe algebras, box spaces, and Property A were run in the local
corpus, arXiv, and bounded citation neighborhoods.  Later
hits extend the K-theory result or study adjacent groupoid phenomena; none
found in this audit settles the remaining isomorphism question.
