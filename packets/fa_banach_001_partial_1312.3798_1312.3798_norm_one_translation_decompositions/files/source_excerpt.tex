\begin{quote}
Find the best constant $C_n$ such that any
$x\in\ker(T_1-I)\cdots(T_n-I)$ has some decomposition
$x=x_1+\cdots+x_n$, with $x_j\in\ker(T_j-I)$, and
\[
  \max_{j=1,\ldots,n}\|x_j\|\le C_n\|x\|.
\]
We saw $C_n\le 2^{n-1}$ in general and $C_n\le 2^{n-2}$ for
translations on $BC(\mathbb R)$. Are these estimates sharp? Is it true that
$C_n=1$ for translations on $BC(\mathbb R)$ for every $n$? Under which
conditions on $E$ and/or $T_1,\ldots,T_n$ does $C_n=1$ hold?
\end{quote}

The source immediately before this problem states that the translation bound
is sharp for $n=2$, hence the known two-period constant is exactly one.
