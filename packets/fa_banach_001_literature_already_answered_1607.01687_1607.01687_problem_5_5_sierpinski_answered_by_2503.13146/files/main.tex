\section{Original question}

P. Szewczak and B. Tsaban,
\emph{Products of general Menger spaces}, arXiv:1607.01687, later
published in \emph{Topology and its Applications} \textbf{255} (2019),
41--55, pose the following on arXiv PDF page~13~\cite{source}.

\begin{question}
Does the Continuum Hypothesis imply that no Sierpi\'nski set is
productively Hurewicz?
\end{question}

Here a space $S$ is productively Hurewicz if $S\times H$ is Hurewicz
for every Hurewicz space $H$.

\section{Separate later answer}

W. Marciszewski, R. Pol, and P. Zakrzewski,
\emph{On Sierpi\'nski sets, Hurewicz spaces and Hilgers functions},
arXiv:2503.13146, published in \emph{Annals of Pure and Applied Logic}
\textbf{177} (2026), 103680, state immediately before Theorem~3.1 on
arXiv PDF page~6 that their result gives ``a positive answer (in a strong
form)'' to Problem~7.5 of their reference [ST1], repeated as Problem~5.5
of [ST2].  Their [ST2] is precisely the source paper above.  The theorem
is as follows~\cite{answerpaper}.

\begin{answer}
For every Sierpi\'nski set $S\subseteq 2^{\mathbb N}$ of cardinality at
least $\mathfrak b$, there is a Hurewicz $\lambda'$-set
$H\subseteq 2^{\mathbb N}$ such that $S\times H$ is not Hurewicz.  If
$\mathfrak b=\mathfrak d$, the factor $H$ may be chosen so that
$S\times H$ is not even Menger.
\end{answer}

Under CH, every Sierpi\'nski set has cardinality
$\mathfrak c=\aleph_1=\mathfrak b=\mathfrak d$.  Theorem~3.1 therefore
applies to every Sierpi\'nski set $S$ and supplies a Hurewicz factor $H$
for which $S\times H$ is not Hurewicz (indeed, not Menger).  Thus no
Sierpi\'nski set is productively Hurewicz, exactly as Problem~5.5 asks.

\section{Scope and search status}

This packet records a complete later-literature answer to Problem~5.5
only.  The source also poses Problems~5.2--5.4.  A bounded exact-label and
core-phrase search through 13 August 2026 did not locate comparably
explicit later resolutions of those three problems.  Direct checks of
their most immediate routes encounter, respectively, the need to preserve
Lindel\"ofness in products with general spaces and the gap between a
forcing consistency construction of a Menger cofinite-filter scale and a
consequence of CH alone.  No claim about the current status of
Problems~5.2--5.4 is made here.

No new mathematical result is claimed in this packet.
