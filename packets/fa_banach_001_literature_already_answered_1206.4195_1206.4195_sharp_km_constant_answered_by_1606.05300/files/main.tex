\section*{Source question}

Let $C$ be a bounded convex subset of a normed space, let
$T:C\to C$ be nonexpansive, and let
\[
 x_n=(1-\alpha_n)x_{n-1}+\alpha_nTx_{n-1},
 \qquad 0\le\alpha_n\le1.
\]
Cominetti, Soto, and Vaisman, arXiv:1206.4195, prove the universal bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:upper}
 \|x_n-Tx_n\|
 \le \frac{1}{\sqrt\pi}\,
 \frac{\operatorname{diam}(C)}
 {\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i(1-\alpha_i)}}.
\end{equation}
In their conclusion (source PDF page 12), they ask for the smallest
universal constant $\kappa$ that can replace $1/\sqrt\pi$.  Their
right-shift example only establishes
$0.4688\ldots\le\kappa\le1/\sqrt\pi=0.5641\ldots$.

\section*{Later full answer}

Bravo and Cominetti, arXiv:1606.05300, Theorem 1.1 (supporting PDF page
4), prove that the upper constant in \eqref{eq:upper} is tight.  Precisely,
for every $\kappa<1/\sqrt\pi$ they construct a nonexpansive map
\[
 T:[0,1]^{\mathbb N}\longrightarrow[0,1]^{\mathbb N}
 \quad\text{in }\ell_\infty(\mathbb N),
\]
an initial point, and a constant relaxation sequence
$\alpha_n\equiv\alpha$ for which, at some iterate,
\[
 \|x_n-Tx_n\|>
 \kappa\,
 \frac{\operatorname{diam}(C)}
 {\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i(1-\alpha_i)}}.
\]
Consequently no smaller universal constant works, while
arXiv:1206.4195 supplies the matching upper bound.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{\kappa_{\mathrm{opt}}=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}}.
\]

The construction is not merely an improvement of the source's affine
right-shift example.  The answering paper first formulates sharp recursive
distance bounds as nested optimal-transport problems.  Its Theorem 2.1
builds a nonlinear nonexpansive map on the infinite cube that attains all
of those bounds simultaneously.  A Markov-chain interpretation then gives
the $1/\sqrt\pi$ asymptotics used in Theorem 1.1.  This fully resolves the
source question.
