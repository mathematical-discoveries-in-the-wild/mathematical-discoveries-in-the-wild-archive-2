# KSGNS universal property and Paschke dilation: literature-implied answer

Status: `literature_implied_answer` (full in the von Neumann/self-dual-module
setting where Paschke dilations are defined).

## Source question

Bastiaan Eelco Westerbaan, *Dagger and Dilation in the Category of Von
Neumann Algebras*, arXiv:1803.01911v3 (2019), PDF page 57, paragraph 155 III,
asks whether KSGNS has a Stinespring-like universal property and whether it is
a Paschke dilation.

## Supporting result

Robert P. Allen and Dominic T. Verdon, *CP-infinity and beyond:
2-categorical dilation theory*, arXiv:2310.15776v3 (2024):

- Proposition 1.2 represents every von Neumann algebra as an endomorphism
  algebra of a generating W*-module.
- Theorem 1.3 (pp. 3-4; proof beginning p. 17) gives the arbitrary-target
  W*-module Stinespring/KSGNS representation and states that a minimal
  representation is an initial object, hence has the requested universal
  comparison isometries.
- The discussion on PDF page 7 explicitly says that the endomorphism-algebra
  factorization associated with such a minimal representation is a Paschke
  dilation.

The supporting authors do not identify Westerbaan's thesis question by arXiv
number; the implication is therefore agent-identified rather than an explicit
claim of priority. Outside the von Neumann/self-dual-module setting, the
source's own definition of Paschke dilation does not apply literally.
