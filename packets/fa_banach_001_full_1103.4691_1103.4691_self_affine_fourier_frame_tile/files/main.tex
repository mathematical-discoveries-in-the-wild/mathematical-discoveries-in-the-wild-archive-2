\section{The question and the precise result}

Lai proves that a compactly supported absolutely continuous probability
measure $d\mu=\varphi\,dx$ admits a Fourier frame exactly when its density is
bounded above and bounded away from zero almost everywhere on its support
\cite[Theorem~1.1]{Lai}.  After applying this criterion to equal-weight
self-similar measures on the line, the paper asks whether the analogous
conclusion holds for self-affine measures on $\R^d$ \cite[p.~13, Q2]{Lai}.

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{figures/question_q2.png}
\end{center}

We use the natural higher-dimensional version of the class treated in the
paper.  Let $R$ be an expansive invertible real $d\times d$ matrix, let
$B\subset\R^d$ consist of $N$ distinct digits, and set
\[
 \tau_b(x)=R^{-1}(x+b) \qquad (b\in B).
\]
Let $K$ be the attractor and $\mu$ the equal-weight invariant probability:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:invariance}
 K=\bigcup_{b\in B}\tau_b(K),
 \qquad
 \mu(E)=\frac1N\sum_{b\in B}\mu(\tau_b^{-1}E).
\end{equation}
Write $q=|\det R|$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Suppose that $\mu$ is absolutely continuous and that $L^2(K,d\mu)$ admits a
Fourier frame.  Then
\[
 q=N,
 \qquad
 \mu=\frac{1}{\Leb(K)}\,\Leb\!\restriction_K,
\]
and the sets $\{\tau_b(K):b\in B\}$ partition $K$ up to null sets.
Consequently $RK=K+B$ is a self-affine tile in the standard sense of
Lagarias--Wang \cite{LW}; in particular, $K$ tiles $\R^d$ by translations.
Thus Q2 has an affirmative answer in this class, with the harmless
probability-normalization correction that the density is
$\Leb(K)^{-1}\chi_K$, not literally $\chi_K$ unless $\Leb(K)=1$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof intuition}

Positive Lebesgue measure first gives the easy volume inequality $q\leq N$.
For the reverse inequality, choose a generic linear functional.  At every
digit level it selects a unique minimizing digit, producing one address
whose point $x_*$ is exposed not merely in $K$, but in a unique cylinder at
every depth.  Near $x_*$, the iterated density equation therefore has only
one surviving summand.  Each refinement multiplies that summand by $q/N$.
The Fourier-frame criterion bounds the density above and below, so this
factor cannot decay through arbitrarily many refinements; hence $q\geq N$.
Equality forces no overlap in measure, and uniqueness of the invariant
probability then identifies $\mu$ with normalized Lebesgue measure on $K$.

\section{A cylinder isolated at every depth}

Every point of $K$ has an address and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:address}
 K=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}R^{-j}c_j:c_j\in B\right\}.
\end{equation}
For a word $w=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)$ write
$\tau_w=\tau_{c_1}\circ\cdots\circ\tau_{c_n}$ and
$K_w=\tau_w(K)$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:exposed}
There are digits $b_1,b_2,\ldots$ and a point
$x_*=\sum_{j\geq1}R^{-j}b_j\in K$ such that, for every $n$,
\[
 x_*\in K_{(b_1,\ldots,b_n)}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 x_*\notin K_w\quad\text{for every other word }w\in B^n.
\]
Consequently, for every $n$ there is $r_n>0$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:isolation}
 K\cap B(x_*,r_n)\subset K_{(b_1,\ldots,b_n)}
 \setminus\bigcup_{w\ne(b_1,\ldots,b_n)}K_w.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose $u\in\R^d$ outside the countable union of proper hyperplanes
\[
 \bigcup_{j\geq1}\ \bigcup_{b\ne b'}
 \{u:\langle u,R^{-j}(b-b')\rangle=0\}.
\]
For each $j$, let $b_j$ be the unique digit minimizing
$\langle u,R^{-j}b\rangle$ over $b\in B$.  The series defining $x_*$
converges because $R$ is expansive.  If $(c_j)$ is any other address, then
term by term
\[
 \langle u,R^{-j}(c_j-b_j)\rangle\geq0,
\]
and the inequality is strict at the first index where $c_j\ne b_j$.
The absolutely convergent sum is therefore strictly positive.  Hence $x_*$
is the unique minimizer of $x\mapsto\langle u,x\rangle$ on $K$.

More is true.  If a depth-$n$ word $w$ differs from
$(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$, then every address beginning with $w$ has a strict
positive contribution at one of its first $n$ levels and nonnegative
contributions thereafter.  Thus $x_*\notin K_w$.  The union of the finitely
many other compact cylinders is compact and omits $x_*$, so its distance
from $x_*$ is positive.  A sufficiently small ball gives
\eqref{eq:isolation}.
\end{proof}

\section{Proof of the theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
By Lai's Fourier-frame criterion there are constants $0<m\leq M<\infty$
such that, after extending the density by zero off $K$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bounds}
 m\leq\varphi(x)\leq M
 \quad\text{for almost every }x\in K.
\end{equation}
In particular $\Leb(K)>0$.  Taking Lebesgue measure in
$K=\bigcup_{b\in B}\tau_b(K)$ gives
\[
 \Leb(K)\leq\sum_{b\in B}\Leb(\tau_b(K))
 =\frac{N}{q}\Leb(K),
\]
and hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qleN}
 q\leq N.
\end{equation}

Changing variables in \eqref{eq:invariance} gives the density refinement
identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:refinement}
 \varphi(x)=\frac qN\sum_{b\in B}\varphi(Rx-b)
 \quad\text{for almost every }x\in\R^d.
\end{equation}
Its $n$-fold iterate is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:iterate}
 \varphi(x)=\left(\frac qN\right)^n
 \sum_{w\in B^n}\varphi(\tau_w^{-1}x)
 \quad\text{for almost every }x.
\end{equation}

Fix $n$ and use Lemma~\ref{lem:exposed}.  Put
$E_n=K\cap B(x_*,r_n)$.  Since every positive-weight IFS measure has support
equal to its attractor, $x_*\in\operatorname{supp}\mu$ and
$\mu(E_n)>0$.  Absolute continuity then implies $\Leb(E_n)>0$.
For $x\in E_n$, only the selected cylinder can contribute to
\eqref{eq:iterate}; all other inverse images lie outside $K$.  Combining
\eqref{eq:bounds} and \eqref{eq:iterate}, and discarding the union of the
relevant null exceptional sets, yields on a positive-measure subset of
$E_n$
\[
 m\leq\varphi(x)
 =\left(\frac qN\right)^n
   \varphi(\tau_{(b_1,\ldots,b_n)}^{-1}x)
 \leq\left(\frac qN\right)^n M.
\]
This holds for every $n$.  If $q<N$, the right side is eventually smaller
than $m$, a contradiction.  Thus $q\geq N$, which together with
\eqref{eq:qleN} proves $q=N$.

Now the finite cover in \eqref{eq:invariance} satisfies
\[
 \Leb(K)=\sum_{b\in B}\Leb(\tau_b(K)).
\]
Therefore its pieces overlap only on a null set.  Define normalized
Lebesgue measure on $K$ by
$\nu(E)=\Leb(E\cap K)/\Leb(K)$.  For every Borel set $E$,
\begin{align*}
 \frac1N\sum_{b\in B}\nu(\tau_b^{-1}E)
 &=\frac{q}{N\Leb(K)}
   \sum_{b\in B}\Leb(E\cap\tau_b(K))\\
 &=\frac{\Leb(E\cap K)}{\Leb(K)}=\nu(E),
\end{align*}
where the second equality uses $q=N$ and the almost-everywhere partition.
Thus $\nu$ satisfies the same invariant equation as $\mu$.  Uniqueness of
the invariant probability for a contractive IFS gives $\mu=\nu$.

Finally $RK=K+B$, $K$ has positive measure, and $B$ has
$N=q=|\det R|$ digits.  This is precisely the self-affine-tile condition of
Lagarias--Wang; their tiling theorem supplies a translation tiling of
$\R^d$.  This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\section*{Checks, novelty, and scope}

The exposed-address argument does not assume an open set condition, a lattice
digit set, diagonalizability, or any ordering of $\R^d$.  Its only geometric
input is that an expansive matrix makes \eqref{eq:address} converge.  The
measure-theoretic step is also insensitive to boundary points: support gives
$\mu(E_n)>0$, and absolute continuity gives $\Leb(E_n)>0$.

The word ``self-affine measure'' is not separately defined around Q2.  This
packet therefore retains the equal weights and common linear part of the
self-similar class explicitly treated immediately before the question, while
allowing a general expansive matrix and arbitrary dimension.  Unequal
weights or an IFS with varying linear parts are not claimed here; in those
broader conventions, the factor along the isolated address is no longer
$(q/N)^n$, so a genuinely new argument would be needed.

A bounded literature search used the run indexes and arXiv/web queries for
the source identifier, exact question, self-affine Fourier frames, bounded
self-affine densities, and the exposed-address/determinant mechanism.  It
found later work on uniformity and frame-spectral self-affine measures, but
no paper stating this resolution of Q2.  The tile terminology and final
tiling implication are classical \cite{LW}.  Novelty is therefore
provisional pending expert review.
