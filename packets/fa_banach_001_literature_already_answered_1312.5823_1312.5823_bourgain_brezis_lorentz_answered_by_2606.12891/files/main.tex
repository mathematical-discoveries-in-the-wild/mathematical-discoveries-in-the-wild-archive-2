\section*{Original signal and exact question}

Yoshikazu Giga and Xingfei Xiang, \emph{Lorentz space estimates for vector
fields with divergence and curl in Hardy spaces}, arXiv:1312.5823v1
\cite{GX}, write on source-PDF page~1 that their work originates from
Bourgain and Brezis's Open Problem~1 and that they still do not know how to
treat the case of a divergence-free field whose curl is merely in $L^1$.
Their own theorem instead assumes atomic-Hardy data on a bounded domain.

The cited problem is Bourgain--Brezis Open Problem~1: if
$F\in L^1(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)$ is divergence-free and has mean zero, and
$Z$ is the mean-zero solution of
\[
  \curl Z=F,\qquad \diver Z=0,
\]
must $Z$ belong to the critical Lorentz space
$L^{3/2,1}(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)$?  Equivalently, is there a uniform bound
\[
  \|Z\|_{L^{3/2,1}(\mathbb T^3)}\le C\|F\|_{L^1(\mathbb T^3)}?
\]

\section*{Decisive later answer}

Jesse Goodman, Felipe Hern\'andez, and Daniel Spector,
\emph{Potential Estimates and Hodge Systems with $L^1$ data on compact
manifolds}, arXiv:2606.12891v1 \cite{GHS}, explicitly say in the abstract and
Introduction that they answer Bourgain--Brezis Open Question~1.

Their Corollary~1.4 (source-PDF pages 4--5) proves exactly that, for every
divergence-free mean-zero
$F\in L^1(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)$, the canonical solution
$Z=\curl(-\Delta_{\mathbb T^3})^{-1}F$ satisfies
\[
 \curl Z=F,\qquad \diver Z=0,\qquad
 \|Z\|_{L^{3/2,1}(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)}
 \le C\|F\|_{L^1(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)}.
\]
It also proves the particular consequence
$Z(x)/|x-a|\in L^1(\mathbb T^3)$ for every $a\in\mathbb T^3$ asked in the
original problem.  Corollary~1.4 follows from their more general
Theorem~1.3, an endpoint Lorentz estimate for Hodge systems on smooth compact
Riemannian manifolds.

\section*{Identification, provenance, and scope}

The match is exact and author-acknowledged: the later paper cites the original
Bourgain--Brezis problem by number and page, states that it gives an
affirmative answer, and its Corollary~1.4 has precisely the same hypotheses
and conclusion.  The run's cheap indexes also already contained the
arXiv:2606.12891 extraction record, which led to this cross-check; a bounded
search of the exact title, exact problem phrase, and the two arXiv identifiers
confirmed the match.

The answer concerns the cited periodic problem on $\mathbb T^3$ (and the
supporting paper substantially generalizes it to compact manifolds).  It does
not by itself assert every possible bounded-domain analogue with boundary
conditions.  However, arXiv:1312.5823 does not formulate such an analogue as
a separate numbered question: its open-problem signal expressly points to
Bourgain--Brezis Open Problem~1.  Thus that extracted target is fully answered.
The packet stores the original paper as \texttt{source\_paper.pdf} and the
answer paper as \texttt{supporting\_paper\_2606.12891.pdf}.
