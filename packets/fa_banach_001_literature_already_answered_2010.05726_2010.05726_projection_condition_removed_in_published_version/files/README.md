# arXiv:2010.05726 — projection condition removed in published revision

Status: `literature_already_answered (published revision; full)`

The arXiv v2 paper proves weak convergence of iterates of a
nonexpansive quasi-α-firmly nonexpansive mapping on a complete CAT(0)
space, but makes strong convergence of their metric projections depend
on an extra condition (34). Its cyclic- and averaged-projection results
likewise use condition (40), whose necessity the paper says needs
investigation.

The 2022 journal revision removes the condition. Theorem 3 proves
unconditionally that `P_{Fix T} x_n` converges strongly to the weak
limit. Theorems 4 and 5 give the unconditional cyclic- and
averaged-projection consequences. The revised proof finishes the arXiv
Cauchy argument by applying the Opial property of bounded weakly
convergent sequences in CAT(0) spaces.

Exact locations:

- arXiv:2010.05726v2: Theorem 3, PDF pages 11–12; Theorem 4 and
  historical remark, page 13.
- Published Russian original: Theorem 3, journal pages 46–47 (PDF pages
  9–10); Theorem 4, pages 47–48; Theorem 5, pages 48–49.
