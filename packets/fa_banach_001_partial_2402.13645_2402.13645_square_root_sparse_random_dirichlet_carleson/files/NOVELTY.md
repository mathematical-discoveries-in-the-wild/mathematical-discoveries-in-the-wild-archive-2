# Novelty audit

## Claimed contribution

The packet does not claim the full conjecture. It records an explicit
Hilbert--Schmidt sufficient condition in the unresolved range `0<a<=1/2`
and its simple square-root-sparse corollary

`N_m <= C 2^{(1/2-epsilon)|m|}`.

The source supplies Lemma 4.4, but only applies its Hilbert--Schmidt argument
to `1/2<a<=1`. The displayed double sums and the uniform missing-range
corollary do not appear in the source text.

## Searches performed

Cheap run indexes were searched for arXiv:2402.13645 and combinations of
`random Carleson`, `Dirichlet polydisc`, `finite measure`, `Gramian`,
`Hilbert-Schmidt`, and `0<a<=1/2`; no existing packet or duplicate answer was
found. Bounded title/citation/keyword searches through 13 August 2026 found
the 2024 JFA publication, one-variable random-Dirichlet antecedents, and later
Hardy-polydisc work, but no paper resolving the stated Dirichlet-polydisc
conjecture.

The principal geometric comparison checked was Mozolyako--Psaromiligkos--
Volberg--Zorin-Kranich, arXiv:2001.02373 / RMI 2022. Its bi-/tri-tree
embedding theory requires uniform box/energy control; it does not turn the
source's total-mass condition directly into the desired random theorem.

## Bounded claim

The mathematical deduction is elementary once Lemma 4.4 is available, so
independent rediscovery is plausible. Novelty is therefore recorded
conservatively as `candidate partial result pending human review`, not as a
priority claim. No matching statement was located in the bounded search.
