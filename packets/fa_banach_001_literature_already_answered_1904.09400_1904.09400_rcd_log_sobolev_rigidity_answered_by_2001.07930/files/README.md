# Literature Answer: Log-Sobolev Rigidity on RCD(K,∞) Spaces

## Source Question

- Source paper: S.-i. Ohta and A. Takatsu, *Equality in the logarithmic Sobolev inequality*, arXiv:1904.09400.
- Location: Section 4, “Further problems,” item (B), page 10 of the locally compiled source PDF.
- Question: whether the paper's Gaussian-splitting rigidity theorem for equality in the logarithmic Sobolev inequality extends from smooth weighted Riemannian manifolds to `RCD(K,∞)` metric-measure spaces.

## Status

`literature_already_answered (Further problem (B), affirmative)`.

Bang-Xian Han, *Rigidity of some functional inequalities on RCD spaces*, arXiv:2001.07930, Theorem 1.7, proves precisely this extension. For `K>0`, a nonconstant extremal of the corresponding `Φ`-entropy inequality forces an isometric splitting by the one-dimensional Gaussian space. In the logarithmic-Sobolev case `Φ(x)=x log x`, the extremal density is

`f(r,y) = exp(a r - a^2/(2K))`,

which is exactly the density of a translation in the Gaussian factor.

## Explicit Provenance

The later paper explicitly cites Ohta--Takatsu and says in Remark 1.9 that the `RCD(K,∞)` rigidity problem was open in their Section 4 because dimension-free needle decomposition was unavailable. It then states that its result extends Ohta--Takatsu to `RCD(K,∞)` spaces. This is therefore an explicit later-literature answer, not an agent-inferred implication.
