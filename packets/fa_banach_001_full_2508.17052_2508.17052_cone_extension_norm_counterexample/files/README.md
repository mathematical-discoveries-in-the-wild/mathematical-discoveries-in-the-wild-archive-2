# Cone-extension norm topology counterexample

This packet gives a candidate full negative answer to the natural question
after Lemma 2.22 of arXiv:2508.17052.

The explicit cone is the positive hull in `c_00` of the algebraic basis

`f_(2j-1)=e_(2j-1)`, `f_(2j)=e_(2j-1)+j^(-1)e_(2j)`.

It is proper, Euclidean-norm closed, generating, and has empty interior. For
`x_j=f_(2j)-f_(2j-1)=j^(-1)e_(2j)`, the ambient norm tends to zero while every
positive-minus-positive decomposition has total ambient norm at least two.
Thus the cone-extension norm and ambient norm have different topologies.
