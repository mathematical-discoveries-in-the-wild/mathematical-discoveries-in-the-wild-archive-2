# Literature resolution: BD relaxation without a strong recession function

**Status:** `literature_already_answered` (negative answer to recession-function existence; positive relaxation theorem by a generalized recession function).

Rindler's arXiv:1008.2089, Remark 6.9 (source PDF p. 44), asks for the relaxation of a general integrand after replacing it by its symmetric-quasiconvex envelope `SQ f`, but notes two obstacles: it was unknown whether `(SQ f)^infinity` exists and lower semicontinuity could not then be proved with `(SQ f)^#`.

Kosiba--Rindler, arXiv:1903.05771, Theorem 1.3 (supporting PDF pp. 3--4), explicitly cites the source paper and proves the weak-star BD relaxation formula for continuous two-sided linear-growth integrands without any assumption on a strong recession function. The singular density is `(SQ f)^#`. De Philippis--Rindler, arXiv:1911.01356, pp. 25--26 and Theorem 4.5, explicitly states both that `(SQ f)^infinity` need not exist in general and that this generalized-recession formula is the essentially optimal later relaxation theorem.
