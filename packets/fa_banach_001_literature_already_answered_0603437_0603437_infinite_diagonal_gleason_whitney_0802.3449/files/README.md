# Infinite-dimensional diagonal question answered by Ueda

Status: `literature_already_answered (full for the finite-dimensional-diagonal removal question)`.

After its Gleason--Whitney and Kaplansky-density results, arXiv:math/0603437
says on PDF page 12 that the authors do not know whether the last few results
hold without assuming the diagonal `D=A intersection A*` finite dimensional.

Ueda's arXiv:0802.3449 explicitly answers this. Section 4 states that the
source proofs of Corollary 3.5, Theorems 4.1--4.2, and Corollary 4.3 need only
Ueda's weaker F. and M. Riesz consequence and therefore all hold without any
dimensional assumption. The following theorem on supporting PDF pages 6--7
lists the four conclusions, including normality/uniqueness of Hahn--Banach
extensions and bounded weak* approximation from `A+A*`.

The source authors independently confirm the identification in
arXiv:1605.08932. Their introduction says Ueda removed the dimensional
restriction from four or five of their theorems; Theorem 3.2 (PDF page 8) and
Corollary 5.9 (page 17) restate the Kaplansky and Gleason--Whitney conclusions
in the broader sigma-finite setting.
