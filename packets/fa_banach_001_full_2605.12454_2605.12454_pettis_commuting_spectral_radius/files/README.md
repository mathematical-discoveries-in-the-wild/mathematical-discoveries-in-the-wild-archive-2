# Pettis-integrable commuting spectral-radius inequality

Status: `candidate_full_likely_valid` for the literal concluding question in
arXiv:2605.12454.

## Result

For a Pettis-integrable family in a complex unital Banach algebra whose values
commute, and for measurable pointwise spectral radius,

```text
r(Pettis integral of A) <= integral of r(A_t).
```

The proof uses the closed commutative algebra generated by the values and
integrates its characters.  Spectral radius is unchanged on passage to this
closed subalgebra because it is computed by the same inherited norms of the
powers.

In the setting of Theorem 4.1 of the source paper, its original norm-strong
measurability assumption implies Pettis integrability: the paper's
norm-countably-additive generated vector measure and a countable partition
into Bochner pieces give scalar integrability for every functional.  Thus
Pettis integrability is a genuine replacement, and the new theorem itself
does not require the hypothesis that `X*` contain no copy of `c0`.

## Scope

This fully answers the literal question whether *a* weaker assumption can
replace norm-strong measurability.  It does not settle the sharper endpoint
under bare pointwise `L_s^1` measurability without Pettis compatibility.  A
nonseparable Hilbert-space example in the packet shows strictness at the level
of operator-valued representatives.
