## Mathematical checks

1. The exact source question was checked against arXiv:2605.12454v1, PDF
   page 36 and source lines 1676--1679.
2. The source definitions were checked: `L_s^1` supplies Bochner
   measurability/integrability of each orbit; the questioned extra assumption
   is norm-valued strong measurability in `B(X)`.
3. The Pettis proof was checked in the closed unital algebra generated by the
   pointwise values.  The Pettis integral lies in that algebra by quotient
   scalarization.
4. The possible inverse-closedness defect was checked: spectral radius is the
   same in a closed subalgebra with inherited norm because the norms of all
   powers are identical.
5. Every character extends by Hahn--Banach, so the Pettis integral identity
   applies.  The pointwise character bound is therefore by the ambient
   spectral radius.
6. The implication from the source hypothesis to Pettis integrability was
   checked using its Theorem 3.3, a countable finite-measure/norm-bounded
   partition, and the finite total variation of each scalar measure.
7. In the strictness example, finite phase sums of the orthogonal projections
   have norm one, forcing every functional's coordinate values into
   `ell_1([0,1])`; this proves weak measurability and the Pettis identity.
8. The scope is explicit: the packet answers the literal existence of a
   weaker replacement, not the bare pointwise-measurable endpoint.
