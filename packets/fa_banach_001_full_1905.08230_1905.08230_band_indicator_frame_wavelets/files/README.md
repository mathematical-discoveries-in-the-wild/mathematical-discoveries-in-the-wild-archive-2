# The missing band-indicator wavelets are frames

Status: **candidate full affirmative solution to Problem 2.7; likely valid;
send to human review**.

Source: Marcin Bownik and Ziemowit Rzeszotnik, *Open problems in wavelet
theory*, arXiv:1905.08230, Problem 2.7 on source PDF page 7.

## Result

Let

`hat(psi_b) = 1_{(-1,-b) union (b,1)}`.

For every `1/8 < b <= 1/6`, the dyadic affine family generated by `psi_b` is a
frame for `L^2(R)`.  Thus every parameter in the interval asked about in
Problem 2.7 has the frame property.

With the unitary Fourier convention used in the packet, the proof gives the
explicit (non-optimal) frame bounds `1/54` and `6`.

The key step is an exact periodization of the affine frame quadratic form.  It
becomes the Dirichlet energy of a measured bipartite alias graph.  Almost every
vertex has total incidence three, except for a two-edge class whose vertices
are never adjacent to each other when `b <= 1/6`.  Orienting edges toward the
smaller magnitude gives out-degree at most one.  These two observations imply
a measured Cheeger constant at least `1/3`, and coarea plus Cauchy--Schwarz
gives the lower frame bound.
