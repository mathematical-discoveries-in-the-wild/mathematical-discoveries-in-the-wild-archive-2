## Mathematical checks

- Different source blocks have different total `z,w` degree, so their Wiener coefficients do not overlap. The exact Wiener norm is therefore `sum_n 1/n^2`.
- Symmetry is immediate from `(w^2+z^2)^n=(z^2+w^2)^n`.
- The fundamental symmetric-polynomial identity is `z^2+w^2=s1^2-2s2`; hence the displayed formal series is exactly the one generated by the source's prescribed homogeneous decomposition.
- A formal monomial `s1^(2n-2k)s2^k` determines `n` through the weighted degree `(2n-2k)+2k=2n`, so coefficients from different `n` cannot cancel or merge.
- At `(s1,s2)=(2r,r^2)`, the binomial theorem gives exact absolute block mass `(3r^2)^n/n^2`. For `r=3/4` the exponential base is `27/16>1`, so even the block masses fail to tend to zero.
- The point is in the symmetrized bidisc because it is the image of `(r,r)` with `r<1`.
- A multivariable power series converges absolutely throughout the interior of its convergence set. Thus failure of absolute convergence at an interior image point excludes every convergence domain containing the symmetrized bidisc.
- The corrected weighted-block statement is checked separately and is not used to weaken the counterexample.
