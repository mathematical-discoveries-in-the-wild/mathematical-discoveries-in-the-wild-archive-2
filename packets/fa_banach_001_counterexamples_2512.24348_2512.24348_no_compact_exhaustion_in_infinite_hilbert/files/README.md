# No compact exhaustion in infinite-dimensional Hilbert space

This packet gives a candidate full negative answer to the proposal in
Section 10.3 of arXiv:2512.24348 to define a heat kernel on a nonseparable
Hilbert space by analogy with compact exhaustion of a noncompact Riemannian
manifold.

Main result: with the norm topology, a real or complex Hilbert space admits
a manifold-style compact exhaustion if and only if it is finite-dimensional.
Thus the proposed method cannot even be initiated on a nonseparable Hilbert
space. The result is stronger than requested: it excludes every
infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.

The proof combines the Baire category theorem with noncompactness of the
infinite-dimensional unit ball. A shorter nonseparable-only proof observes
that every sigma-compact metric space is separable. The packet also explains
why weakly compact balls, nets, and finite-dimensional Galerkin sequences do
not constitute the proposed norm-topology exhaustion.
