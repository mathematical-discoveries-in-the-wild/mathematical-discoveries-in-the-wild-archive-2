# Boundary trace is noninjective on the generated monogenic algebra

Status: candidate full negative answer to open item (ii) of arXiv:2203.00118,
likely valid pending expert review.

The paper asks whether the algebra generated by monogenic spinor fields is
isometrically isomorphic, under boundary trace, to the algebra generated by
their boundary values. On the Euclidean unit three-ball the answer is no.

With `I=e1e2`, `J=e1e3`, `K=e2e3`, the fields
`Z=x2-x1 I` and `W=x3-x1 J` are monogenic. Exact Clifford multiplication
shows

```text
|x|^2 = Z^2 + (1/2) Z W K + Z I W J + (1/2) W Z K - W I W I.
```

Thus `1-|x|^2` belongs to the algebra generated by monogenic spinors. It is
nonzero in the ball and has identically zero boundary trace, so the trace map
is not even injective.
