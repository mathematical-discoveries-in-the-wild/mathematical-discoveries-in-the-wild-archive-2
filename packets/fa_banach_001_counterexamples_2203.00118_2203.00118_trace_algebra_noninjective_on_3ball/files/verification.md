# Verification record

## Target match

- Source: arXiv:2203.00118v1, page 22, item (ii).
- Target: whether the algebra generated by monogenic spinor fields and the
  algebra generated by their boundary traces are isometrically isomorphic.
- Result: no; on `B^3` the trace homomorphism has a nonzero kernel element.

## Proof audit

1. With the paper's positive Clifford convention, `e_i^2=1`, hence
   `I=e1e2`, `J=e1e3`, and `K=e2e3` square to `-1`.
2. `D(x2-x1 I)=e2-e1I=0` and `D(x3-x1 J)=e3-e1J=0`.
3. Right constant multiples of these fields remain monogenic.
4. Exact expansion of the five terms in the packet sums to the scalar
   `x1^2+x2^2+x3^2`; the verifier uses rational coefficients only.
5. Therefore `P=1-|x|^2` is in the pointwise algebra generated by monogenic
   spinors, `P` vanishes on `S^2`, and `P(0)=1`.
6. Boundary restriction is consequently noninjective, which is stronger than
   failure of isometry.

## Scope

The counterexample resolves item (ii) only. It does not settle whether the
spinor spectrum itself identifies an arbitrary compact Riemannian manifold.
The maximum principle for monogenic fields does not apply to `P`, because
products in the generated algebra need not remain monogenic.
