# A completely localized ccc measure singular off the diagonal

This packet gives a negative answer to the unrestricted off-diagonal-density
question in arXiv `1008.5344`. The exact broad counterexample is a scalar
finite-range family on `ell^2(Z)` with standard one-site covariance: a random
choice of phase for a period-two potential. Its interband ccc measure has
positive mass on a one-dimensional off-diagonal Floquet curve.

The packet also gives a stronger localization obstruction. An ergodic random
Jacobi family made of independent two-site cells is completely localized,
satisfies a bounded-density Wegner estimate, and has randomness-independent
finite-range velocity, yet its ccc measure is supported on the two
off-diagonal lines `E_1-E_2=+/-2t`.

At every positive Lebesgue point of the cell law on either line, the normalized
mass of an `epsilon` by `epsilon` rectangle grows like `1/epsilon`. Hence no
finite two-dimensional ccc density exists there.

Status: candidate full counterexample to the broad formulation, likely valid
subject to human review; plus a sharply scoped localized strengthening.

Scope: neither construction settles the stricter canonical Anderson model with
independent randomness at every physical site and uniform nonzero
nearest-neighbor hopping.
