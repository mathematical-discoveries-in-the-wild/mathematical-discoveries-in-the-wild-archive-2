# arXiv:1304.7189 — Open Problem 4.13 answered by arXiv:1503.07095

Status: literature_already_answered.

Open Problem 4.13 of Tomasz Ciaś, *On the algebra of smooth operators* (2013), asks whether every closed commutative star-subalgebra of `L(s',s)` is isomorphic to a closed star-subalgebra of the pointwise algebra `s`.

Tomasz Ciaś, *Commutative subalgebras of the algebra of smooth operators* (arXiv:1503.07095v1, 2015), explicitly says that its Theorem 6.10 solves Open Problem 4.13 negatively. Theorem 6.10 constructs a closed commutative star-subalgebra of `L(s',s)` not isomorphic to any closed star-subalgebra of `s`. Theorem 6.2 characterizes the positive cases by Vogt's property `(Omega)`.
