# Literature answer: rank-two 4x4 partial isometries are UECSM

Status: `literature_already_answered`

## Identification

Conjecture 1 of Tener, *Unitary equivalence to a complex symmetric matrix: an
algorithm* (arXiv:0908.2201, PDF page 9), asks whether every rank-two
`4 x 4` partial isometry is unitarily equivalent to a complex symmetric
matrix.

Garcia and Wogen, *Complex symmetric partial isometries*
(arXiv:0907.4486, PDF page 2), explicitly identify Tener's four-dimensional
assertion and prove the stronger Corollary 1 that every partial isometry of
rank at most two is complex symmetric.  Their Corollary 2 also says that every
partial isometry on a Hilbert space of dimension at most four is complex
symmetric.  Either result gives a complete affirmative answer.

## Provenance and scope

This is a separate-paper answer whose authors cite Tener's manuscript and
know they are resolving the same assertion.  The supporting arXiv posting
happened shortly before Tener's arXiv posting, so the relation is best viewed
as an explicitly linked independent answer rather than a chronologically
later discovery.  No part of the source conjecture remains open.
