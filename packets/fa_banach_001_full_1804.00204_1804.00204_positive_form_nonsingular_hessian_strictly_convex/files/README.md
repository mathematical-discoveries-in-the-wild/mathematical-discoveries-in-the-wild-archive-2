# Positive form + nonsingular Hessian implies strict convexity

This packet gives a full affirmative answer to the question in Remark 1 after
Theorem 9 of Friedland--Gaubert, arXiv:1804.00204 (PDF page 12).

## Result

If a real homogeneous polynomial `F` of even degree satisfies `F(x)>0` for
every nonzero `x` and its Hessian is invertible for every nonzero `x`, then
the Hessian is in fact positive definite at every nonzero point and `F` is
strictly convex. The tensor nonnegativity and irreducibility assumptions from
the surrounding source theorem are not needed.
