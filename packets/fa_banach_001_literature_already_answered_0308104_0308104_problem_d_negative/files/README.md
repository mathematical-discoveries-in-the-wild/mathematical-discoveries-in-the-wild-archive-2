# Arveson Problem D: literature answer

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

William Arveson's *p-Summable Commutators in Dimension d* (arXiv:math/0308104; J. Operator Theory 54 (2005), 101--117) asks in Problem D whether every finite-rank pure commuting `d`-contraction is Fredholm and whether the curvature-index formula holds. The question is on PDF page 16, in the concluding section.

Jim Gleason, Stefan Richter, and Carl Sundberg, *On the index of invariant subspaces in spaces of analytic functions of several complex variables* (J. Reine Angew. Math. 587 (2005), 49--76; DOI: 10.1515/crll.2005.2005.587.49), explicitly cites Arveson's Problem D and answers it. Their example following Theorem 4.3 yields, for `d=2`, a pure finite-rank `d`-contraction that is not Fredholm. Their Theorem 1.3/Theorem 4.5 proves the curvature-index formula whenever such a tuple is Fredholm. Thus the universal Fredholm claim is false, while the index formula is valid on the Fredholm locus.

The authors explicitly knew they were addressing Arveson's question: the introduction says that pure finite-rank non-Fredholm examples answer the cited problems negatively and points directly to "Problem D of [12]", where [12] is Arveson's paper.

Scope: this settles Problem D, not Arveson's Conjectures A--C on polynomially generated or essentially normal quotient modules.
