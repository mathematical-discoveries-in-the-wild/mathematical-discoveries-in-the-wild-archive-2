\textbf{Status: literature\_already\_answered.}

\section*{Original question}
William Arveson's \emph{$p$-Summable Commutators in Dimension $d$}
(arXiv:math/0308104; \emph{J. Operator Theory} 54 (2005), 101--117)
asks in Problem D on PDF page 16, in the concluding section:

\begin{quote}
Let $\bar T$ be a finite-rank $d$-contraction. Is $\bar T$ a Fredholm
$d$-tuple? Does the index formula hold?
\end{quote}

The immediately preceding sentence makes clear that the intended class is
finite-rank \emph{pure} $d$-contractions. The index formula is the identity
relating Arveson's curvature invariant $\kappa(T)$ to the Fredholm index,
$\kappa(T)=(-1)^d\operatorname{ind}(T)$.

\section*{Identification of the answer}
Gleason, Richter, and Sundberg prove in Theorem 1.3 (restated as Theorem 4.5)
that for every pure finite-rank $d$-contraction,
\[
  \kappa(T)=(-1)^d\operatorname{ind}(T-\lambda)
  \qquad(\lambda\in\mathbb B_d\setminus\sigma_e(T)).
\]
In particular, if $T$ itself is Fredholm, then the formula requested by
Problem D holds at $\lambda=0$.

The example following Theorem 4.3 (supporting PDF pages 21--22) constructs an
invariant subspace $M\subset H^2_2$ for which
$(M_{z_1}-\lambda,M_{z_2})|_M$ is not Fredholm at any $(\lambda,0)$ in its
zero set. Via the standard compression model, Theorem 4.5 then yields pure
finite-rank $2$-contractions with $0\in\sigma_e(T)$, hence non-Fredholm
examples. The introduction states this consequence explicitly on supporting
PDF page 6 and points directly to ``Problem D of [12]'', with [12] being
Arveson's paper.

Therefore the universal Fredholm assertion in Problem D is false for $d>1$,
whereas its index formula holds for every tuple in the stated class that is
Fredholm. This is a complete literature answer to Problem D.

\section*{Scope limitation}
This identification does not settle Arveson's Conjectures A--C concerning
polynomially generated invariant subspaces, compact commutators, essential
normality, or their more specialized quotient modules.

The source question was checked against the local arXiv source and PDF. The
