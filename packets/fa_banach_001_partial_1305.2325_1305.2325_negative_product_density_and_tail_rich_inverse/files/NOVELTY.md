# Novelty audit

## Literature status

The source arXiv:1305.2325 asks the inverse question and singles out bilateral
weighted shifts on `c_0(Z)`. Grosse-Erdmann, arXiv:1707.03994, proves the
upper-frequent inverse theorem and gives a more general characterization, but
explicitly leaves the frequent bilateral-shift case open. Menet,
arXiv:1910.04452, constructs a general invertible frequently hypercyclic
operator on `ell^1` whose inverse is not frequently hypercyclic; that operator
does not settle the weighted-shift specialization.

Cheap run indexes and bounded primary-source searches through 2026-08-13
found no later solution of the bilateral `c_0(Z)` case. Searches included the
exact source question, its later `condition (ii)` formulation, inverse
frequent hypercyclicity, and bilateral weighted shifts.

## Bounded novelty claim

The packet claims only:

1. uniform positive lower density of simultaneous two-sided product escape;
2. a positive-density diagonal escape set;
3. a tail-rich witness criterion implying frequent hypercyclicity of the
   inverse; and
4. the monotone-negative-side corollary.

No matching statements were located in the bounded search. They are direct
but nontrivial consequences of the published characterization and should be
treated as candidate partial results pending expert review, not as a full
resolution or priority claim.
