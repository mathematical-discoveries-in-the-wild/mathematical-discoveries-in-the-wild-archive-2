# Normal-subobject modularity characterization

This packet gives a candidate full proof of the conjecture in footnote 12 of
arXiv:2206.11630.

For every real seminormed space `(V,p)` and every `0 < epsilon < 1`, the
normal-subobject lattice for the ideal of maps of norm at most `epsilon` is
modular exactly when `dim(V/V_0) <= 1`.

The proof converts admissible seminorms into centrally symmetric convex unit
balls between `epsilon U` and `U`. In dimension at least two, an exposed cap
contains a short line segment. Three compact cap sets reproduce a universal
nonmodular convex-set configuration; a quantitative cap-localization estimate
makes the construction work for arbitrary norms, arbitrary epsilon, and
infinite-dimensional spaces.
