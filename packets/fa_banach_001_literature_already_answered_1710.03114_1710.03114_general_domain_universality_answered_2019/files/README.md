# arXiv:1710.03114 — full conjecture answered in the 2019 literature

Status: `literature_already_answered (full conjecture, affirmative)`

The abstract and Section 1 of Christoforos Panagiotis, *Universal partial
sums of Taylor series as functions of the centre of expansion*,
arXiv:1710.03114, record Nestoridis's conjecture that for every simply
connected domain `Omega` avoiding `0`, the functions with a dense orbit under
the variable-centre Taylor partial sums form a dense `G_delta` subset of
`H(Omega)`. The source paper proves only the disc case.

Theorem 2.10 (paper page 8; journal page 487) of L. Bernal-González,
H. J. Cabana-Méndez, G. A. Muñoz-Fernández, and J. B. Seoane-Sepúlveda,
*Universality of sequences of operators related to Taylor series*, JMAA 474
(2019), 480–491, DOI 10.1016/j.jmaa.2019.01.056, proves the exact general
result and more: `0 notin Omega` is equivalent to universality, mixing,
residuality of `S(Omega)`, and dense-lineability of `S(Omega)`.

The triage attack independently found a short log-coordinate/eigenvector
argument, recorded in the status note and attempt log, but no new theorem is
claimed because the full conjecture is already settled.
