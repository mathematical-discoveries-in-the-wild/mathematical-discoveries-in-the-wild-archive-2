# Berger--Coburn conjecture: later counterexample

Status: `literature_already_answered`

The introduction of Haoren Xiong, *Boundedness of metaplectic Toeplitz
operators and Weyl symbols* (arXiv:2305.03948, PDF page 1), describes the
general Berger--Coburn conjecture: boundedness of a Bargmann--Fock Toeplitz
operator should be equivalent to boundedness of its Weyl symbol.  Xiong proves
the statement for exponential inhomogeneous-quadratic symbols.

Sam Looi, *A counterexample to the Berger--Coburn conjecture*
(arXiv:2601.20859), explicitly restates the measurable-symbol conjecture as
Conjecture 1.1 (PDF pages 2--3) and disproves it in Theorem 1.2 (PDF page 3).
In every complex dimension there is a measurable `g in L2(dmu)` for which all
kernel-domain hypotheses hold and `T_g` extends boundedly, while the critical
heat transform `g^(1/4)`—the corresponding Weyl symbol—is unbounded.

The supporting authors explicitly identify the metaplectic/quadratic papers,
including Xiong's target, as positive special cases.  Thus this is an exact
later negative answer to the general conjecture, not a newly inferred
reformulation.  Xiong's structured theorem is unaffected.
