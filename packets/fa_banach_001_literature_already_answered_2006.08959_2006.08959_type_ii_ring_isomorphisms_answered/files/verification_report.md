## Claim matching

The source's Conjecture 5.1 asks for every type-II ring isomorphism
LS(M) -> LS(N) to be conjugate by an invertible locally measurable
operator to a real star-isomorphism. Its Conjecture 5.2 asks for the induced
classification of projection lattices.

The supporting papers were checked at theorem level:

- arXiv:2010.01627, Theorem 1.4, gives exactly that form for type II_1;
  Corollary 1.5 gives the projection-lattice equivalence.
- arXiv:2206.00875, Theorem 1.2, gives exactly that form for type
  II-infinity. Theorems 1.3 and 1.4 explicitly state the combined general
  ring and lattice conclusions.

Thus the later literature proves the stronger conjecture on both type-II
parts, and the weaker conjecture follows.
