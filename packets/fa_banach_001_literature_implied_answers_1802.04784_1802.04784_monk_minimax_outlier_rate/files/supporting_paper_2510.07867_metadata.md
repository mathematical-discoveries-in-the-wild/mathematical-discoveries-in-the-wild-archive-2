# Supporting paper metadata

- Xabier de Juan and Santiago Mazuelas, *On the Optimality of the Median-of-Means Estimator under Adversarial Contamination*.
- arXiv:2510.07867, submitted 9 October 2025.
- NeurIPS 2025.
- Stable links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07867 and https://openreview.net/forum?id=A0aUS60Kvk
- Decisive locations: equation (2) and its minimax discussion on PDF pages 3--4; Theorem 3.2 on PDF pages 5--6.
- Exact implication used: for finite-variance distributions and adversarial corruption fraction `alpha`, scalar median-of-means with `k` of order `log(1/delta) + alpha n` attains error `sigma (sqrt(log(1/delta)/n) + sqrt(alpha))`, which the paper identifies as the minimax order.
