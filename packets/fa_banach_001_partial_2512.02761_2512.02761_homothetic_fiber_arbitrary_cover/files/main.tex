\section{Source conjecture}

For \(\sigma\subset[n]\), write
\(H_\sigma=\operatorname{span}\{e_i:i\in\sigma\}\).  A family
\((\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_m)\), with \(\sigma_j\subset\sigma\), is an
\(s\)-cover of \(\sigma\) if every coordinate in \(\sigma\) occurs in
exactly \(s\) members.  Conjecture~1.4 of Al\'ias, Gonz\'alez Merino, and
Mar\'in Gimeno \cite{AGM} asserts that every convex body \(K\subset\R^n\)
satisfies
\[
 \vol(K)^{m-s}
 \max_x\vol\bigl(K\cap(x+H_\sigma^\perp)\bigr)^s
 \ge
 \frac{\prod_{j=1}^m(|\sigma|-|\sigma_j|)!}
      {|\sigma|!^{\,m-s}}
 \prod_{j=1}^m\vol\bigl(K\cap H_{\sigma_j}^\perp\bigr).
 \tag{1}
\]

\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{figures/source_conjecture_crop.png}
\caption{Source Conjecture~1.4 (PDF page 4).}
\end{figure}

The source proves (1) when the complementary \((m-s)\)-cover is
\emph{1-reducible}, meaning that it splits into \(m-s\) genuine partitions.
The restriction is real: the three edges
\(\{12,13,23\}\) form a 2-cover of \([3]\), but no two edges are disjoint,
so this cover cannot split into two 1-covers.

\section{A functional arbitrary-cover theorem}

Let \(p\ge1\), let \(C\subset\R^p\) be a convex body with the origin in its
interior, and let \(\|\cdot\|_C\) be its gauge.  A function
\(F:\R^p\to[0,\infty)\) has an \emph{aligned homothetic profile} if
\[
 F(x)=M\psi(\|x\|_C), \tag{2}
\]
where \(M>0\), \(\psi:[0,\infty)\to[0,1]\) is nonincreasing and
right-continuous, \(\psi(0)=1\), and \(\psi\) has bounded support.  Thus all
nonempty superlevel sets of \(F\) are dilates of the same \(C\), about the
same origin.  No symmetry or log-concavity is needed in the next theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary uniform covers]
\label{thm:functional}
Let \((\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_m)\) be an \(r\)-cover of \([p]\), where
\(r\ge1\), and put \(d_j=|\tau_j|\).  Every \(F\) of the form (2) satisfies
\[
 M^{m-r}\left(\int_{\R^p}F\right)^r
 \ge
 \frac{\prod_{j=1}^m d_j!}{p!^{\,r}}
 \prod_{j=1}^m\int_{H_{\tau_j}}F.
 \tag{3}
\]
There is no reducibility assumption on the cover.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Proof intuition.}
The common body \(C\) and the one-dimensional radial profile \(\psi\) can be
separated exactly.  The geometric factors are governed by Liakopoulos's
sharp section inequality for arbitrary covers.  The profile factors are
moments of one random radius; the cover identity
\(\sum_jd_j=rp\) makes Lyapunov's moment inequality give exactly the missing
comparison, with no loss in the conjectured constant.

\begin{proof}
Choose a nonnegative random variable \(R\) with survival function
\(\Pr\{R>t\}=\psi(t)\), and set
\(\mu_d=\mathbb E R^d\), including \(\mu_0=1\).  For every coordinate
subspace \(H_\tau\) of dimension \(d\), Tonelli's theorem gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \int_{H_\tau}\psi(\|x\|_C)\,dx
 &=\mathbb E\,\vol_d\{x\in H_\tau:\|x\|_C<R\}\\
 &=\mu_d\,\vol_d(C\cap H_\tau).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
The formula also holds for \(d=0\) under the usual zero-dimensional volume
convention.

Lyapunov's inequality says
\(\mu_d^{1/d}\le\mu_p^{1/p}\) for \(1\le d\le p\).  Since an \(r\)-cover
satisfies \(\sum_jd_j=rp\),
\[
 \prod_{j=1}^m\mu_{d_j}
 \le \mu_p^{\sum_jd_j/p}=\mu_p^r. \tag{5}
\]
Zero-dimensional terms equal one and cause no change.

Liakopoulos's dual uniform-cover inequality \cite{Liakopoulos}, applied to
\(C\) and the arbitrary \(r\)-cover \((\tau_j)\), is
\[
 \vol_p(C)^r\ge
 \frac{\prod_jd_j!}{p!^{\,r}}
 \prod_j\vol_{d_j}(C\cap H_{\tau_j}). \tag{6}
\]
By (4), the left side of (3) is
\(M^m\mu_p^r\vol_p(C)^r\).  Combining (5) and (6) bounds it below by
\[
 \frac{\prod_jd_j!}{p!^{\,r}}
 \prod_j\bigl[M\mu_{d_j}
 \vol_{d_j}(C\cap H_{\tau_j})\bigr],
\]
which is the right side of (3).
\end{proof}

\section{Application to the source conjecture}

Let \(p=|\sigma|\), \(r=m-s\), and
\(\tau_j=\sigma\setminus\sigma_j\).  Then \((\tau_j)\) is an \(r\)-cover
of \(\sigma\), and \(d_j=p-|\sigma_j|\).  Define the fiber-volume function
\[
 F(x)=\vol_{n-p}\bigl(K\cap(x+H_\sigma^\perp)\bigr),
 \qquad x\in H_\sigma. \tag{7}
\]

\begin{corollary}[Homothetic fibers]
\label{cor:geometric}
If the function (7), after identifying \(H_\sigma\) with \(\R^p\), has
the aligned homothetic form (2), then the full inequality (1) holds for
\(K\) and every \(s\)-cover \((\sigma_j)\), whether reducible or not.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Fubini's theorem gives
\[
 \vol_n(K)=\int_{H_\sigma}F,
 \qquad
 \vol\bigl(K\cap H_{\sigma_j}^\perp\bigr)
 =\int_{H_{\tau_j}}F. \tag{8}
\]
Moreover \(\max F=M\).  Substitution of (8) into
Theorem~\ref{thm:functional} is exactly (1).
\end{proof}

The structural hypothesis is realized by a large concrete family.  Let
\(C\subset H_\sigma\) and \(B\subset H_\sigma^\perp\) be arbitrary convex
bodies containing the origin, put \(q=n-p\), and form their gauge
\(\ell_1\)-sum
\[
 K=C\oplus_1B
 :=\{(x,y):\|x\|_C+\|y\|_B\le1\}. \tag{9}
\]
Its fiber over \(x\) is \((1-\|x\|_C)_+B\), so
\[
 F(x)=\vol_q(B)(1-\|x\|_C)_+^q. \tag{10}
\]
Thus Corollary~\ref{cor:geometric} proves (1) for every cover and every body
in (9).  Neither \(B\), \(C\), nor \(K\) need be unconditional, and the
fibers are nonconstant when \(q>0\).

For the first nondecomposable case, take \(p=3\),
\((\tau_j)=(12,13,23)\), \(r=2\), and any \(q\ge1\).  The corresponding
source cover is \((\sigma_j)=(\{3\},\{2\},\{1\})\), with \(s=1\).
For (10),
\[
 \mu_d=\frac{d!q!}{(d+q)!},\qquad
 \frac{\mu_3^2}{\mu_2^3}
 =\frac92\frac{(q+1)(q+2)}{(q+3)^2}
 =1+\frac{q(7q+15)}{2(q+3)^2}>1. \tag{11}
\]
The exact accompanying script verifies (11) and the cover's
non-1-reducibility.  This supplies explicit cases not reached by the source's
main theorem.

\section{Boundary of the result and upgrade audit}

The homothetic hypothesis is doing real work.  For arbitrary nested convex
superlevel sets, applying (6) at each level leaves a reverse-H\"older coupling
between different sectional level profiles; standard H\"older and Chebyshev
inequalities point in the wrong direction.  Reverse Brascamp--Lieb has total
cover weight \(m/r>1\), so log-concavity does not compare its supremal
convolution with \(F\).  A convex-body lift is valid but yields the weaker
factorial constant
\(\prod_j(d_j+q)!(p!)^s/(p+q)!^m\).  Finally, the maximizing fiber in (1)
need not be centered on the coordinate sections, preventing a direct common
translation.

Eight focused attempts covered the exact functional reduction, the triangle
cover, polyhedral and ellipsoidal stress tests, level sets, reverse
Brascamp--Lieb, convex lifting, the successful moment factorization, and a
final Berwald/localization upgrade.  No localization preserving all
coordinate restrictions supplied the missing coupling.  Thus this packet
does not claim (1) for arbitrary \(K\).

\paragraph{Verification and novelty.}
Review should focus on the Fubini identification (8), the random-radius
formula (4), and the direction of Lyapunov's inequality in (5).  All are exact
and the proof contains no numerical step.  On 12 August 2026, the four run
indexes and exact/current searches for the title, conjecture, arbitrary
covers, and homothetic fibers found no later resolution or this structural
subcase.  Novelty confidence is moderate and provisional.

\paragraph{Human-review recommendation.}
Review as a substantial partial result.  It proves the sharp conjectured
constant for all covers in a natural infinite-dimensional parameter family
of convex bodies and crosses the source's combinatorial reducibility barrier,
while leaving the unrestricted convex-body problem open.
