\section*{Original question}

The source is D.~Alpay and I.~Lewkowicz, \emph{State space realization of
even generalized positive and odd rational function. Applications to static
output feedback}, arXiv:1202.1099 \cite{source}.

For a realization with state matrix $A$, input matrix $B$, output matrix $C$,
and $A_{\rm cl}=A+BKC$, Lemma~4.1 (source PDF pages 9--10) and
Proposition~6.1 (source PDF pages 13--15) establish converse minimality
criteria under either
\[
 \operatorname{rank}B=\operatorname{rank}C
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \min(\operatorname{rank}B,\operatorname{rank}C)\ge r,
\]
where $r$ is the number of Jordan blocks of $A$.  Immediately after the two
proofs, on source PDF pages 10 and 15, respectively, the authors state that
they do not know whether these conditions are inherent or artifacts of their
technique.

The two precise converse questions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does minimality alone imply that some constant $K$ satisfies
\[
 \spec(A)\cap\spec(A+BKC)=\varnothing ?
\]
\item In the square realization-matrix setting, does minimality alone imply
\[
 \left(\bigcap_{D\in\C^{p\times p}}
   \spec\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D\end{pmatrix}\right)
   \cap\spec(A)=\varnothing ?
\]
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Later theorem}

The later paper D.~Alpay and I.~Lewkowicz, \emph{Minimal state space
realization, static output feedback and matrix completion of families of
systems}, arXiv:1305.1756v2 \cite{support}, gives exactly the unconditional
answer in Theorem~1.2 on supporting PDF pages 3--4.

Theorem~1.2 states the equivalence of:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] the realization is minimal;
\item[(ii)] there exists one constant $K\in\C^{m\times p}$ such that
\[
 \spec(A)\cap\spec(A+BKC)=\varnothing;
\]
\item[(iv)] in the square case (or after the paper's minimal square
reduction),
\[
 \left(\bigcap_{D\in\C^{p\times p}}
   \spec\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D\end{pmatrix}\right)
   \cap\spec(A)=\varnothing.
\]
\end{itemize}
No equality or lower bound involving $\operatorname{rank}B$,
$\operatorname{rank}C$, or the number of Jordan blocks appears in these
equivalences.  Therefore the source hypotheses are not inherent: minimality
alone suffices for both formal converse statements.

\section*{Provenance and scope}

This is an agent-identified implication, not a new proof and not an explicit
claim by the supporting paper that it resolves arXiv:1202.1099.  The match is
nevertheless exact: the authors are the same, the feedback matrix is the same
unstructured constant $K$, and Theorem~1.2(ii) is the rank-free version of
source Proposition~6.1.  Item~(iv) is the rank-free version of the formal
converse in source Lemma~4.1.  The supporting paper also handles rectangular
systems by its minimal square reduction, a modest extension beyond the source
lemma's square setting.

The identification does not address structured or decentralized feedback,
nor arbitrary pole placement by static output feedback.  Those are different
problems from avoiding the finite set $\spec(A)$.
