# Literature Answer: endpoint questions from arXiv:1712.01374

Status: `literature_already_answered`

Source paper: N. Randrianantoanina, L. Wu, and Q. Xu,
“Noncommutative Davis type decompositions and applications,”
arXiv:1712.01374 (published in *J. London Math. Soc.* 99 (2019), 97–126).

## Exact source questions

Remark 3.11 asks whether the first part of the symmetric-space Davis
decomposition remains true for every `E in Int(L_1,L_q)`, `1<q<infinity`,
and whether the resulting Hardy-space identity remains true for every
`E in Int(L_1,L_2)`.  The remark following the Burkholder/Rosenthal theorem
also asks whether either usual sum/intersection formula applies to
`L_{2,infinity}`, or more generally `L_{2,q}`, `q != 2`.

## Later answers

Randrianantoanina, “P. Jones' interpolation theorem for noncommutative
martingale Hardy spaces,” arXiv:2212.08714, explicitly identifies
arXiv:1712.01374 (its citation `Ran-Wu-Xu`) and says it resolves the problem
left there. Proposition `Davis-symmetric` proves the requested decomposition
for `E in Int(L_1,L_q)`, and Corollary `Davis` proves

`H_E^c = h_E^d + h_E^c`

for `E in Int(L_1,L_2)`. The paper also proves sharpness: the latter identity
forces `E in Int(L_1,L_2)`.

Cadilhac, “Noncommutative Khintchine inequalities in interpolation spaces of
L_p-spaces,” arXiv:1809.06091, Proposition `counterexample` and Section 6,
constructs Schur–Horn examples showing that neither usual row/column
Khintchine formula works in weak `L_2=L_{2,infinity}`. Since either
Burkholder/Rosenthal alternative in the source would imply its corresponding
Rademacher/Khintchine estimate, this gives a negative answer to the source's
specifically highlighted weak-`L_2` case. Cadilhac's arXiv:1812.03861,
Theorem `khrad`, subsequently gives the general interpolation
characterization of the two standard Rademacher formulas.

This packet records later literature; it claims no new mathematics.
