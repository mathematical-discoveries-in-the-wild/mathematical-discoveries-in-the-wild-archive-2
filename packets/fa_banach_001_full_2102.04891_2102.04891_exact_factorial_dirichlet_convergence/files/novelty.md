## Important prior-art caveat

The source explicitly observes, for `beta=1`, that the factorial series and a
corresponding Dirichlet series have the same convergence range, referring to
Lauwerier and Temme. The packet does **not** claim this classical comparison as
new.

The candidate contribution is the self-contained, two-way equivalence for all
source parameters (including conditional convergence), the sharp radius
trichotomy for the transformed germ, the proof that the germ transformation
is universal and hence that Dirichlet behavior is optimal, and the explicit
pair with equal original Taylor radius but opposite convergence.

Novelty confidence: moderate. A specialist should compare the synthesis and
especially the universality/equal-radius statements with classical Landau,
Lauwerier, Nörlund, and Temme treatments before publication.
