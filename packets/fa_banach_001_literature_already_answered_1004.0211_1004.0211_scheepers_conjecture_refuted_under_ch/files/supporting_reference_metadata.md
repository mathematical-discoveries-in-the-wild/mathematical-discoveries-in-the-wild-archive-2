The publisher/Crossref abstract explicitly says that the paper refutes
Scheepers' conjecture under CH. More precisely, under CH it constructs a
subset `X` of the reals for which `C_p(X)` has property `alpha_2` but `X`
does not satisfy `S_1(Gamma,Gamma)`. The Academy of Mathematics and Systems
Science publication announcement repeats that main-result statement and gives
the same DOI and journal metadata.

The journal PDF was paywalled and unavailable for lawful local copying during
this run. The packet therefore cites the stable DOI and official institutional
statement and does not include a purported copy of the supporting PDF.
