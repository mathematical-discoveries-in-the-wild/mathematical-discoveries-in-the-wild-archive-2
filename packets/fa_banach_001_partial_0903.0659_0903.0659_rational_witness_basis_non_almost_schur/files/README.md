## Result

The source asks for explicit bases both for almost-Schur filters and for the
negation class. This packet fully answers the negation half.

Let `Q_0` be the finitely supported rational unit vectors in `ell_1`. For
every sequence `u=(u_n)` in `Q_0` for which zero is a tail weak cluster point,
form the free filter `W_u` generated by all tail traces of finite weak
neighborhoods of zero. Then:

- every `W_u` is not almost Schur, witnessed by `u_n` itself; and
- every non-almost-Schur filter `F` dominates some `W_u`.

Thus the family of rational weak-neighborhood filters is an explicit basis
for the increasing class of all non-almost-Schur filters. Equivalently,

```text
F is almost Schur iff no W_u is contained in F.
```

The proof normalizes an arbitrary bad witness and approximates its terms in
`ell_1` norm by rational finite-support unit vectors with error at most `1/n`.

## Remaining half and obstruction

The requested explicit basis for the decreasing almost-Schur class remains
open. The packet records a concrete obstruction: assuming CH, there is an
increasing `omega_1`-chain of countably based Schur filters whose union is a
canonical non-almost-Schur witness filter. Hence almost-Schur filters are not
closed under unions of chains, so the obvious maximal-extension/Zorn route
cannot supply the missing basis.
