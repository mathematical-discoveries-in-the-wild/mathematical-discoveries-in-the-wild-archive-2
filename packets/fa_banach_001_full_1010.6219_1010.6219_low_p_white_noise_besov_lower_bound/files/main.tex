\section*{Status and source question}

This is a \texttt{candidate\_full\_likely\_valid} solution to Remark~3.5(i)
of Mark Veraar, \emph{Regularity of Gaussian white noise on the
$d$-dimensional torus}, arXiv:1010.6219 \cite{Veraar}.  Theorem~3.4(2) of the
source proves that for every $p\in[2,\infty)$ there is $c_{p,d}>0$ such that
\[
 \Pp\!\left(\|W\|_{B^{-d/2}_{p,\infty}(\T^d)}\ge c_{p,d}\right)=1.
\]
Remark~3.5(i), on PDF page~7, asks whether this remains true for
$p\in[1,2)$ and notes that Hausdorff--Young is insufficient.

\section*{Definitions and idea of the proof}

Use the source's Littlewood--Paley function $\phi$, supported in
$\{\frac12\le |\xi|\le2\}$ and equal to one when
$2^{-1/2}\le|\xi|\le2^{1/2}$.  Put
$\varphi_j(k)=\phi(2^{-j}k)$.  For the canonical white noise
\[
 W=\sum_{k\in\Z^d}\gamma_k e^{ik\cdot x},
 \qquad
 W_j(x)=\sum_{k\in\Z^d}\varphi_j(k)\gamma_k e^{ik\cdot x},
\]
where the $\gamma_k$ are independent standard complex Gaussians, the critical
norm is
\[
 \|W\|_{B^{-d/2}_{p,\infty}}
 =\sup_{j\ge0}2^{-jd/2}\|W_j\|_{L^p(\T^d)}.
\]

The source obtains its lower bound for $p\ge2$ by comparing $L^p$ with $L^2$
and using Parseval.  That comparison reverses below two.  Instead, regard the
normalized $L^p$ mass itself as a nonnegative random variable.  Its first
moment stays uniformly away from zero and its second moment stays uniformly
bounded.  Paley--Zygmund therefore gives a scale-independent positive chance
of a fixed lower bound.  Every third dyadic block uses disjoint Gaussian
coefficients, so Borel--Cantelli upgrades that chance to an almost-sure
infinitely-often statement.

\section*{Main result}

\begin{theorem}[low-$p$ lower bound]\label{thm:main}
Let $d\ge1$, let $W$ be a Gaussian white noise on $\T^d$, and use the
Littlewood--Paley decomposition fixed above.  For every
$p\in[1,\infty)$ there exists a deterministic constant $c_{p,d}>0$ such that
\[
 \Pp\!\left(\|W\|_{B^{-d/2}_{p,\infty}(\T^d)}\ge c_{p,d}\right)=1.
\]
In particular, the answer to Remark~3.5(i) is affirmative for every
$p\in[1,2)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~3.2 and the reduction at the beginning of the proof of Theorem~3.4
in \cite{Veraar}, the Besov norm of an arbitrary white noise has the same law
as that of the canonical series above.  It is therefore enough to prove the
claim for that series.

Fix $p\in[1,\infty)$ and set
\[
 Z_j=2^{-jd/2}W_j,
 \qquad
 Y_j=\|Z_j\|_{L^p(\T^d)}^p.
\]
For every $x\in\T^d$, unitary invariance of the standard complex Gaussian
shows that $Z_j(x)$ has the same law as $v_j^{1/2}\gamma$, where $\gamma$ is
a standard complex Gaussian and
\[
 v_j=2^{-jd}\sum_{k\in\Z^d}|\varphi_j(k)|^2.
\]
This variance is independent of $x$.  The annulus
\[
 S_j=\{k\in\Z^d:2^{j-1/2}<|k|\le2^{j+1/2}\}
\]
lies where $\varphi_j=1$, while $\varphi_j$ is supported in
$\{k:2^{j-1}\le|k|\le2^{j+1}\}$.  Elementary lattice counting therefore
gives constants $a_d,b_d>0$ and $j_0$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:variance}
 0<a_d\le v_j\le b_d<\infty \qquad (j\ge j_0).
\end{equation}

Let $V_d=|\T^d|=(2\pi)^d$ and $m_r=\E|\gamma|^r$.  Tonelli's theorem and
Gaussian scaling give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:first}
 \E Y_j
 =\int_{\T^d}\E|Z_j(x)|^p\,dx
 =V_d m_p v_j^{p/2}
 \ge V_dm_pa_d^{p/2}=:A_{p,d}>0.
\end{equation}
For the second moment, Cauchy--Schwarz in probability gives, for all $x,y$,
\[
 \E\bigl(|Z_j(x)|^p|Z_j(y)|^p\bigr)
 \le \bigl(\E|Z_j(x)|^{2p}\,\E|Z_j(y)|^{2p}\bigr)^{1/2}
 =m_{2p}v_j^p.
\]
After integration and \eqref{eq:variance},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:second}
 \E Y_j^2\le V_d^2m_{2p}b_d^p=:B_{p,d}<\infty.
\end{equation}
No independence between the values at $x$ and $y$ has been used.

Paley--Zygmund, \eqref{eq:first}, and \eqref{eq:second} imply
\begin{align*}
 \Pp\!\left(Y_j\ge\frac{A_{p,d}}2\right)
 &\ge \Pp\!\left(Y_j\ge\frac12\E Y_j\right) \\
 &\ge \frac{(\E Y_j)^2}{4\E Y_j^2}
 \ge \frac{A_{p,d}^2}{4B_{p,d}}=:\delta_{p,d}>0
 \qquad(j\ge j_0).
\end{align*}

It remains to turn this uniform probability into probability one.  The
Fourier supports of $\varphi_{3n}$ are pairwise disjoint: the support for
$3n$ is contained in
$[2^{3n-1},2^{3n+1}]$ in radial frequency, whereas the next begins at
$2^{3n+2}$.  Hence the random fields $(Z_{3n})_n$, and therefore the events
\[
 E_n=\left\{Y_{3n}\ge A_{p,d}/2\right\},
\]
are independent.  Since $\Pp(E_n)\ge\delta_{p,d}$ for all large $n$, the
second Borel--Cantelli lemma gives $\Pp(E_n\ \text{infinitely often})=1$.
On that event,
\[
 \|W\|_{B^{-d/2}_{p,\infty}}
 =\sup_{j\ge0}\|Z_j\|_p
 \ge \left(\frac{A_{p,d}}2\right)^{1/p}.
\]
Thus one may take $c_{p,d}=(A_{p,d}/2)^{1/p}>0$.  Equality in distribution
transfers the conclusion to every Gaussian white noise.
\end{proof}

\section*{Verification, scope, and novelty status}

The proof needs no numerical experiment.  The accompanying verification
report separately audits the pointwise Gaussian law, the two moment bounds,
the direction of Paley--Zygmund, disjointness of the $3n$ supports, and the
equidistribution reduction.  In particular, the endpoint $p=1$ causes no
loss: all positive Gaussian moments are finite.

The constant displayed here is not claimed sharp.  It depends on the fixed
cutoff through the lower lattice-count constant; equivalence of admissible
Besov norms transfers the qualitative deterministic lower bound to any other
standard cutoff.

Eight focused attempts are recorded in the attempt note.  The decisive
upgrade was to replace the failed deterministic $L^2$ comparison by a
two-moment small-ball argument, then recover almost sure behavior on an
independent shell subsequence.

Cheap run indexes and bounded web/arXiv searches on 17 August 2026 used the
exact Remark~3.5(i) sentence, the paper title and author, and the phrases
\emph{low $p$ critical Besov white noise lower bound} and
\emph{Paley--Zygmund dyadic white noise Besov}.  They found the source and its
published version but no explicit later resolution.  Novelty confidence is
moderate-high, not definitive.

Verify the multiplier-support convention for the independent $3n$
