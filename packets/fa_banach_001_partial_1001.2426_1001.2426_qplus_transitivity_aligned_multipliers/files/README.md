# Transitivity of positive extreme-point hulls for aligned rearrangements

Status: likely valid substantial partial result.

Source: F. Sukochev and D. Zanin, “Orbits in symmetric spaces,” J. Functional
Analysis 257 (2009), 194–218, arXiv:1001.2426. Remark 18 asks whether the
family of positive extreme-point hulls Q_+(x) is transitive.

The packet proves that if

    z*(t) = a(t) x*(t)

for a nonincreasing measurable multiplier a with values in [0,1], then
z belongs to Q_+(x) and Q_+(z) is contained in Q_+(x). The proof uniformly
approximates a by dyadic averages of indicators of initial intervals, turning
the product a x* into a norm limit of convex combinations of extreme points.
It works without order continuity or a Fatou-norm assumption.

The packet also records the complete zero-dilation subcases already implied
by Theorems 23, 25, and 27 of the source. The general transitivity question
remains open because an arbitrary intermediate rearrangement can redistribute
mass and need not be a decreasing pointwise multiplier of x*.
