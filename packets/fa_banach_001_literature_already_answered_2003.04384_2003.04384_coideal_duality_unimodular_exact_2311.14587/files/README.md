# Coideal coamenability/relative-amenability duality: scoped later answer

Status: `literature_already_answered (partial regimes; one direction in full generality)`.

Question 1.3 of arXiv:2003.04384 asks whether coamenability of the compact
quotient coideal is equivalent to relative amenability of its discrete codual;
the next sentence extends this to compact quasi-subgroups.

Anderson--Sackaney and Khosravi, arXiv:2311.14587, later prove:

- coamenability of the compact coideal implies G-injectivity, hence relative
  amenability, of the codual in full generality (Theorem 3.2, PDF page 12);
- G-injectivity of the codual implies coamenability (Theorem 6.5, PDF page 25);
- relative amenability suffices for the converse when the ambient discrete
  quantum group is unimodular or when the compact coideal C*-algebra is exact
  (Remark 6.6, PDF page 27). Ambient exactness implies the latter condition.

Thus the exact equivalence requested by the source holds for unimodular or
exact ambient discrete quantum groups. In arbitrary ambient groups, the
forward implication holds and the full exact characterization uses
G-injectivity in place of relative amenability. The unrestricted converse
remains open.
