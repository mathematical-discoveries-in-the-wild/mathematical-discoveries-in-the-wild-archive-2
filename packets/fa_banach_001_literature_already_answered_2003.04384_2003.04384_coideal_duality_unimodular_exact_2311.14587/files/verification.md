# Verification audit

## Exact source signal

- arXiv:2003.04384v4, PDF page 4, Question 1.3 asks the quotient-type
  coamenability/relative-amenability equivalence.
- The immediately following sentence states the compact-quasi-subgroup
  extension for `N_omega` and its codual `tilde N_{P_omega}`.
- The source says it only makes progress on that extension.

## Later statements checked

- arXiv:2311.14587v2 abstract, PDF page 1: compact coideal coamenable iff its
  codual is G-injective; relative amenability replaces G-injectivity for
  unimodular or exact G.
- Introduction, PDF page 2: the same equivalence is displayed explicitly.
- Theorem 3.2, PDF page 12: coamenable compact coideal implies G-injective
  codual.
- Theorem 6.5, PDF page 25: G-injective codual implies coamenable compact
  coideal.
- Remark 6.6, PDF page 27: relatively amenable codual implies coamenable
  compact coideal if G is unimodular or the coideal C*-algebra is exact; the
  following paragraph observes that ambient exactness implies the latter.

## Convention audit

The source writes its compact quasi-subgroup coideal as `N_omega` and its
codual using the group-like projection. The supporting paper writes the paired
objects as `Xhat\Ghat` and `ell^infty(X)`. It explicitly calls them a compact
coideal and its codual. The status claim uses this coideal/codual pairing and
does not infer an equivalence merely from ambiguous left/right quotient
symbols.

## Scope guard

No claim is made that relative amenability implies coamenability for arbitrary
non-unimodular, non-exact ambient G. The full arbitrary-G equivalence in the
supporting paper uses the stronger condition of G-injectivity.
