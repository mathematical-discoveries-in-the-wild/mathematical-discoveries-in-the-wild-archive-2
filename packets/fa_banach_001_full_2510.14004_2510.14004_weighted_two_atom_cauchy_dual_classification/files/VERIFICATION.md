# Verification record

## Mathematical checks

1. Re-expanded the weighted boundary trigonometric polynomial and checked the
   monic outer factor
   `q=z^2-(u+v xi)z+b xi`, the displayed formulas for `u,v`, and the physical
   coefficient relation.
2. Reconstructed the rank-two kernel numerically from the Costara data. The
   numerator row Gram uses the direct coefficient convention
   `(z,z^2) A (conj(w),conj(w)^2)^T`; no transpose is used.
3. Checked the boundary identity
   `G(z,z)=b[c1|z-xi|^2+c2|z-1|^2]`, the off-diagonal Gram coefficients, and
   the diagonal sum.
4. `code/verify_identities.py` checks with exact SymPy arithmetic:
   - the unequal-weight root and realness resultant;
   - exclusion of `uv=b` and the exceptional factor `L=0`;
   - the equal-weight common-ray cross-Gram factorization;
   - the fact that its only possible numerator zero forces coincident support;
   - the affine repeated-pole determinant and its negative leading
     coefficient;
   - positivity of the squared factor under the repeated-root relation.
5. `code/equal_pair_algebra.py` derives the equal-weight kernel pair matrix
   and the exact formula for `A22` in the quadratic extension generated by
   the support point.
6. The numerical checker scanned 19 by 19 logarithmically spaced positive
   weight pairs and 81 non-antipodal angles: 29,241 parameter triples. It
   found no zero cross Gram. Direct spot checks confirm that low-order minors
   can be positive while a higher-order minor is negative, as required by the
   proof's large-order mechanism.
