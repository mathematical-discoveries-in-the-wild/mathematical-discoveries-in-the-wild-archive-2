# One-nonscalar-row Poisson representation

This packet proves a new affirmative case of the open question in
arXiv:1511.08852.

If at most one coordinate of the regular polyball has row size greater than
one, every positive free `k`-pluriharmonic function is the Poisson transform
of a completely positive map.  The proof combines the source's Naimark
structure theorem with a simultaneous unitary direct limit for the scalar
rows and Fuglede's theorem.

The arbitrary-row problem remains open: the direct-limit step has no known
analogue that simultaneously Cuntz-dilates two nonscalar commuting rows while
preserving their row-isometric relations.
