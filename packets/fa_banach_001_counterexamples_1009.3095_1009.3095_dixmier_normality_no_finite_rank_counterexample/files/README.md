# Counterexample packet: no finite-rank operators do not force normality

Status: `candidate_counterexample_likely_valid`

Source: Steven Lord and Fedor Sukochev, *Measure Theory in
Noncommutative Spaces*, arXiv:1009.3095, Open Question 7.1 in Section 7.2,
printed page 28.

## Result

Open Question 7.1 has a negative answer under the spectral-triple definition
stated in the source paper.

On
\[
H=\ell_2(\mathbb N)\otimes\ell_2(\mathbb N),
\qquad D(e_i\otimes f_j)=(i+j)e_i\otimes f_j,
\]
let `A` be the unital algebra generated by the first-coordinate projections
`P_i=|e_i><e_i| tensor I`.  Then
\[
\mathcal A''=\ell_\infty(\mathbb N)\,\bar\otimes\,I,
\]
and every nonzero member of this von Neumann algebra has infinite operator
rank.  Nevertheless, for every Dixmier trace in the source conventions,
\[
\Phi_\omega(I)=\operatorname{Tr}_\omega(\langle D\rangle^{-2})=\frac12,
\]
whereas `Phi_omega(F_r)=0` for the finite sums
`F_r=P_1+...+P_r`.  Since `F_r` increases strongly to `I`, the positive
functional `Phi_omega` is not normal.

The construction is commutative at the von Neumann-algebra level, so the
functional is tracial there.  The obstruction is multiplicity: each atom
`P_i` has infinite Hilbert-space rank but cuts out only a trace-class row of
`<D>^{-2}`.  Thus absence of finite-rank operators does not prevent a
Dixmier-trace functional from assigning zero to every atom and positive mass
to their countable union.
