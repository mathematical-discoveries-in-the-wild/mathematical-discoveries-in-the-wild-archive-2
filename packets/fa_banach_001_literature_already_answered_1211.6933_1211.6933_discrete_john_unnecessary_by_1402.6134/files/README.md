# Literature answer: 1211.6933 via 1402.6134

## Status

`literature_already_answered` — full affirmative theorem without the
extra accessibility hypothesis.

Lehrbäck and Shanmugalingam ask whether their discrete John condition is
necessary when the complement has Aikawa codimension greater than `p`.
Lehrbäck's later Theorem 1.1 proves the corresponding `p`-Hardy inequality
from the codimension bound alone, and Corollary 1.4 explicitly concludes
quasiadditivity of variational `p`-capacity for Whitney covers.  The later
paper says that this makes the additional accessibility condition
unnecessary.
