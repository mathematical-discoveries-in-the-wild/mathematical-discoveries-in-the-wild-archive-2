# Verification record

## Exact source question

- Source: Juha Lehrbäck and Nageswari Shanmugalingam, arXiv:1211.6933.
- Location: PDF page 13, immediately after Proposition 4.1.
- Question: whether the discrete John condition is really necessary for the
  thin-complement quasiadditivity and Hardy conclusion.

## Later answer

- Source: Juha Lehrbäck, arXiv:1402.6134.
- Theorem 1.1, specialized to `beta = 0`, proves the unweighted `p`-Hardy
  inequality from lower Assouad codimension greater than `p` without an
  accessibility assumption.
- Remark 2.1 identifies lower Assouad codimension with the source's Aikawa
  codimension terminology.
- PDF page 3, paragraph before Corollary 1.4, explicitly says that Theorem 1.1
  makes the additional accessibility condition unnecessary.
- Corollary 1.4 on the same page states quasiadditivity of variational
  `p`-capacity for Whitney covers under the codimension condition alone.
