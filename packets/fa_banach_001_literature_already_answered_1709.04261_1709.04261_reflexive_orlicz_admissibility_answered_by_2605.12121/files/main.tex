\section*{Source question}

Jacob, Schwenninger, and Zwart study control systems
\[
 \dot x(t)=Ax(t)+Bu(t),\qquad B:U\longrightarrow X_{-1},
\]
and ask when $L^\infty$-admissibility improves to admissibility for an Orlicz
space $E_\Phi$, which in turn yields continuity of mild solutions and the
equivalence of input-to-state stability (ISS) and integral ISS~\cite{source}.

Their Section~6, arXiv PDF page~16, Question~27 asks: if $A$ generates an
exponentially stable analytic semigroup on a Hilbert space, the
$H^\infty$-calculus of $A$ is not bounded, and $b\in X_{-1}$ is a scalar
$L^\infty$-admissible control operator, must there exist a Young function
$\Phi$ for which $b$ is $E_\Phi$-admissible?

\section*{Exact later answer}

Preu\ss ler and Schwenninger prove in Corollary~3.8, arXiv PDF page~14:
\begin{quote}
If $X$ is a reflexive Banach space, then any $C$-admissible operator is
$E_F$-admissible for some Young function $F$.
\end{quote}
Every $L^\infty$-admissible operator is $C$-admissible by restriction to
continuous inputs, and every Hilbert space is reflexive. Therefore
\[
 b\text{ is }L^\infty\text{-admissible}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 b\text{ is }E_F\text{-admissible for some }F.
\]
No analyticity, exponential stability, scalar-input hypothesis, or assumption
on the $H^\infty$-calculus is needed for this implication. Remark~3.10 on the
same page explicitly states that source Question~27 is resolved positively by
Corollary~3.8~\cite{answer}. Thus this is an author-explicit later answer, not
an agent-only implication.

\section*{Mechanism and wider scope}

The key new mechanism is weak compactness. Preu\ss ler--Schwenninger use sun
duality to represent the relevant adjoint map in $L^1$. Weak compactness gives
uniform integrability by the Dunford--Pettis theorem; de la Vall\'ee Poussin
then supplies a Young function, and Orlicz H\"older yields the extension.
Reflexivity makes the continuous-input map weakly compact automatically.

The later paper also gives several broader positive cases for source
Question~24: state spaces of nontrivial type, suitable Pe\l czy\'nski
property-(V) hypotheses, and Grothendieck/WCG combinations. Its Corollary~4.7
establishes ISS--integral-ISS equivalence in these classes. Remarks~3.16 and
4.4 address source Questions~28 and~25 in broad settings.

The unrestricted Question~24 for arbitrary Banach state and input spaces is
not claimed solved: the later paper explicitly leaves the general ISS/iISS
equivalence open outside its geometric cases. The source's negative
$L^1$-state-space observation example remains valid.
