\section*{Source questions}

Carlen, Frank, and Lieb, arXiv:1806.03985, formulate on PDF page 7
(Conjecture 1) the Audenaert--Datta conjecture: for
\(1\le p\le2\), \(-1\le q<0\), and any \(K\in\M\),
\[
 (A,B)\longmapsto
 \operatorname{Tr}\left(B^{q/2}K^*A^pKB^{q/2}\right)^{1/(p+q)}
\]
should be jointly convex on \(\Pp\times\Pp\).  The singular corner
\((p,q)=(1,-1)\) is implicitly excluded because \(1/(p+q)\) is not
defined.

On PDF page 10, Conjecture 4 asks for the stronger statement that
\[
 \Psi_{p,q,s}(A,B)=
 \operatorname{Tr}\left(B^{q/2}K^*A^pKB^{q/2}\right)^s
\]
is jointly convex throughout the same parameter range whenever
\(s\ge1/(p+q)\).

\section*{Answering paper}

Haonan Zhang, arXiv:1811.01205, explicitly reproduces the
Audenaert--Datta conjecture as its Conjecture 1 and the stronger
Carlen--Frank--Lieb conjecture as its Conjecture 2 on PDF page 2.  The paper
then says that it confirms the stronger conjecture.

More precisely, Theorem 1.1(3) on PDF page 3 proves that, for every invertible
\(K\),
\[
 -1\le q\le0,\qquad 1\le p\le2,\qquad (p,q)\ne(1,-1),
 \qquad s\ge\frac1{p+q}
\]
implies joint convexity of \(\Psi_{p,q,s}\).  A remark immediately after the
introduction explains that arbitrary, possibly singular \(K\) follow by
approximating \(K\) with invertible matrices and using stability of convexity
under pointwise limits.

Thus Zhang proves Conjecture 4 of arXiv:1806.03985 in full.  Taking
\(s=1/(p+q)\) proves Conjecture 1, the Audenaert--Datta conjecture.  The same
paper also states that this supplies the full parameter range for the
data-processing inequality of the \(\alpha\)-\(z\) R\'enyi divergences.

\section*{Status}

This is an explicit later-literature affirmative answer, not a new proof from
the present run.  Both source conjecture signals are closed by the stronger
Theorem 1.1(3) of arXiv:1811.01205.
