# Bounded novelty check

Search date: 2026-08-13.

The local run indexes were searched for arXiv:1809.11001, the exact title,
`minimal subspaces`, `Sobolev tensor product`, and wording around membership in
the tensor product of minimal subspaces. No duplicate result packet or later
resolution was found.

Bounded web searches used the exact conjecture sentence, the source title,
`tensor product of its minimal subspaces`, and combinations of `Sobolev`,
`unconditional`, and `minimal subspace`. They located:

- Ali--Nouy Part I, arXiv:1809.11001, which poses the conjecture.
- Ali--Nouy Part II, arXiv:1912.11293, which repeats that membership is known
  only under conditions and that the authors do not believe it holds in
  general.
- Falcó--Hackbusch, *On minimal subspaces in tensor representations*, Found.
  Comput. Math. 12 (2012), 765--803. Corollary 5.10 and Example 5.11 prove
  density of the algebraic tensor product of the full component Sobolev spaces
  in the relevant Sobolev intersection. They do not show density after every
  factor is restricted to the tensor-dependent spaces `U_j^min(u)`.

No source explicitly stating the unconditional theorem or the simultaneous
spectral-cutoff argument was located. This is bounded evidence only; expert
literature review is still required before asserting novelty.
