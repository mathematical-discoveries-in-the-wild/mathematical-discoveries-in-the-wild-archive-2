\section*{Source conjecture}
Caputo and Salez ask whether cone measure on every $\ell_p$ sphere satisfies
the same arbitrary-block entropy factorization known for the Euclidean sphere
\cite[Conjecture 1, p.~16]{CS}.  Their 2026 journal publication retains this
conjecture.  The exact arXiv source text is reproduced below.

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.97\textwidth]{figures/open_problem_crop.png}
\end{center}

For a probability measure $\nu$ and a nonnegative $F\in L\log L(\nu)$, write
\[
 \Ent_\nu(F)=\int F\log F\,d\nu-
 \left(\int F\,d\nu\right)\log\left(\int F\,d\nu\right).
\]
If the variables are indexed by $[n]$, let $\Ent_A(F)$ denote entropy in the
coordinates in $A$, conditionally on all coordinates in $A^c$.  For a
probability vector $\theta=(\theta_A)_{A\subset[n]}$, put
\[
 \theta_{**}:=\min_{1\le i<j\le n}
       \sum_{A\supset\{i,j\}}\theta_A,
 \qquad
 \theta_*:=\min_{1\le i\le n}\sum_{A\ni i}\theta_A.
\]

\section*{Proof intuition}
Under cone measure, the signs of the coordinates are independent fair signs,
independent of the magnitudes
\[
 Z_i=|X_i|^p,
 \qquad Z\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1/p,\ldots,1/p).
\]
The magnitude layer already has the desired $\theta_{**}$ factorization: it is
a direct entropy-dual form of the Dirichlet Brascamp--Lieb inequality in
\cite[Proposition 14, p.~13]{BCELM}.  The sign layer has ordinary product
Shearer factorization with the larger constant $\theta_*\ge\theta_{**}$.

The only issue is that a block update resamples signs and magnitudes together.
The entropy chain rule splits each joint block entropy into exactly those two
pieces.  Convexity of conditional entropy shows that the magnitude term needed
after averaging all signs is no larger than the magnitude term actually
present in the joint block update.  Adding the two estimates proves the
conjecture, without constructing any geometric regularizer.

\section*{A Dirichlet factorization lemma}
Let
\[
 \Delta_{n-1}=\{z\in[0,1]^n:\textstyle\sum_i z_i=1\}
\]
and let $\mu_\alpha$ be the Dirichlet law with arbitrary parameters
$\alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)\in(0,\infty)^n$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:dirichlet}
For every nonnegative $h\in L\log L(\mu_\alpha)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dir-factor}
 \theta_{**}\Ent_{\mu_\alpha}(h)
 \le \sum_{A\subset[n]}\theta_A\,
       \E_{\mu_\alpha}[\Ent_A(h)].
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $\theta_{**}=1$, every set with positive weight contains every pair and
hence equals $[n]$, so the claim is immediate.  Suppose $\theta_{**}<1$ and
set $c_A=\theta_A/(1-\theta_{**})$.

Apply the Dirichlet Brascamp--Lieb inequality
\cite[Proposition 14]{BCELM} to functions $g_A$ depending on the coordinates
in $A^c$.  Its coefficient condition holds because, for every $i\ne j$,
\begin{align*}
 \sum_{A:A^c\cap\{i,j\}\ne\varnothing}c_A
 &=\frac{1-\sum_{A\supset\{i,j\}}\theta_A}
         {1-\theta_{**}}\le1.
\end{align*}
(When $A=[n]$, $g_A$ is constant and its factor is harmless.)
Thus
\[
 \int\prod_A g_A^{c_A}\,d\mu_\alpha
 \le\prod_A\left(\int g_A\,d\mu_\alpha\right)^{c_A}.
\]
The standard entropy--Brascamp--Lieb duality, recalled as Theorem 1 in the
source paper \cite{CS}, converts this into
\[
 \sum_A\theta_A\Ent_{\mu_\alpha}(\E_Ah)
 \le(1-\theta_{**})\Ent_{\mu_\alpha}(h).
\]
Finally, the conditional entropy chain rule
\[
 \Ent(h)=\Ent(\E_Ah)+\E[\Ent_A(h)]
\]
and $\sum_A\theta_A=1$ give \eqref{eq:dir-factor}.
\end{proof}

\section*{The symmetrized Dirichlet theorem}
Let $\sigma_n$ be uniform measure on $\{-1,1\}^n$, independent of
$Z\sim\mu_\alpha$.  Equivalently, $(Z,\eps)$ induces on
\[
 \Sigma_{n-1}^{\pm}=\{y\in\R^n:\textstyle\sum_i|y_i|=1\}
\]
the symmetrized Dirichlet law via $y_i=\eps_iZ_i$.  This is precisely the
class for which \cite[Remark 16, p.~14]{BCELM} identified a sign/ergodicity
obstruction.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:sym-dir}
For arbitrary $\alpha_i>0$, every probability vector $\theta$, and every
nonnegative $F\in L\log L(\mu_\alpha\otimes\sigma_n)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:joint}
 \theta_{**}\Ent(F)
 \le\sum_{A\subset[n]}\theta_A\,
       \E[\Ent_A(F)].
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $\bar F(z)=\E_\eps F(z,\eps)$.  The global entropy chain rule gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:global-chain}
 \Ent(F)=\Ent_Z(\bar F)+\E_Z\Ent_\eps(F).
\end{equation}

Fix a block $A$.  Given $(Z_{A^c},\eps_{A^c})$, the block variables consist
of the conditional Dirichlet vector $Z_A$ and independent fair signs $\eps_A$.
Applying the chain rule inside this conditional law gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block-chain}
 \E\Ent_A(F)=M_A+S_A,
\end{equation}
where
\begin{align*}
 M_A&=\E_{Z_{A^c},\eps_{A^c}}
 \Ent_{Z_A\mid Z_{A^c}}\!\left(\E_{\eps_A}F\right),\\
 S_A&=\E_Z\E_{\eps_{A^c}}\Ent_{\eps_A}(F).
\end{align*}

Lemma~\ref{lem:dirichlet}, applied to $\bar F$, controls the magnitude part.
For each fixed $Z_{A^c}$, conditional entropy is a convex functional of its
nonnegative argument.  (Its second variation in direction $u$ is
$\E[u^2/h]-(\E u)^2/\E h\ge0$.)  Since
\[
 \bar F=\E_{\eps_{A^c}}\bigl[\E_{\eps_A}F\bigr],
\]
this convexity and Lemma~\ref{lem:dirichlet} yield
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mag}
 \theta_{**}\Ent_Z(\bar F)
 \le\sum_A\theta_A M_A.
\end{equation}

For every fixed $z$, ordinary Shearer's inequality on the product sign space
gives
\[
 \theta_*\Ent_\eps(F(z,\cdot))
 \le\sum_A\theta_A\E_{\eps_{A^c}}\Ent_{\eps_A}(F(z,\cdot)).
\]
Moreover $\theta_{**}\le\theta_*$: choose an index attaining the minimum in
$\theta_*$ and pair it with any other index.  Integrating in $z$ therefore
gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sign}
 \theta_{**}\E_Z\Ent_\eps(F)\le\sum_A\theta_A S_A.
\end{equation}
Adding \eqref{eq:mag} and \eqref{eq:sign}, then using
\eqref{eq:global-chain}--\eqref{eq:block-chain}, proves \eqref{eq:joint}.
The argument first applies to bounded positive $F$; standard $L\log L$
truncation gives the stated form.
\end{proof}

\section*{Resolution of the source conjecture}
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:lp}
For every $n\ge2$, $p>0$, probability vector $\theta$, and cone measure
$\mathbb P$ on the $\ell_p$ sphere $\mathbb S_p^{n-1}$,
\[
 \theta_{**}\Ent_{\mathbb P}(f)
 \le\sum_{A\subset[n]}\theta_A\,
       \E_{\mathbb P}[\Ent_A(f)]
 \qquad(f\in L\log L).
\]
Thus Conjecture 1 in \cite{CS} is true.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Under cone measure,
\[
 Z_i=|X_i|^p\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1/p,\ldots,1/p),
 \qquad \eps_i=\operatorname{sgn}(X_i)
\]
are independent, with the signs fair and independent.  The coordinatewise
map $(z,\eps)\mapsto(\eps_i z_i^{1/p})_{i=1}^n$ is an almost-sure bijection
onto $\mathbb S_p^{n-1}$ and preserves every complementary-coordinate
$\sigma$-field.  Conditional block entropies are therefore unchanged, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:sym-dir} with $\alpha_i=1/p$ applies.
\end{proof}

\section*{Scope, verification, and novelty}
The proof covers all $p>0$, including $p<1$, because it uses only Dirichlet
laws with positive parameters; no norm convexity or boundary regularity is
invoked.  Dirichlet coordinates are positive almost surely, so coordinate
signs and complementary $\sigma$-fields are unambiguous modulo null sets.
The theorem proves the conjectured constant but does not assert that it is
optimal for every choice of $p$ and $\theta$.

The run's four cheap indexes contained no entry for arXiv:2407.13457.
Targeted searches through 13 August 2026 used the exact conjecture phrase,
``symmetrized Dirichlet'', ``cone measure'', and arbitrary-block
$\ell_p$-sphere entropy factorization.  They found the source, its 2026
journal publication (which retains the conjecture), and the cited 2011
Dirichlet result, but no later primary-source resolution.  Novelty remains
provisional pending expert literature review.

The accompanying deterministic Gauss--Jacobi script tests fifteen genuinely
sign-dependent functions for $n=3$ and Dirichlet parameters
$0.2,0.5,1,2,5$; all pass.  This is supporting QA, not part of the proof.
The recommended human-review focus is the one-line Brascamp--Lieb coefficient
calculation in Lemma~\ref{lem:dirichlet} and the convexity comparison leading
to \eqref{eq:mag}.
