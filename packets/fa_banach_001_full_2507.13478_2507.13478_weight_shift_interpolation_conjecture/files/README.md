# One weight shift proves the conjectured interpolation identity

Candidate full affirmative answer to Remark 4.9, equation (4.3), of
arXiv:2507.13478.

For `p-1 < gamma < 2p-1`, multiplication by the normal coordinate shifts the
weight to `beta=gamma-p` in the Muckenhoupt range.  At order three it sends the
Dirichlet endpoint onto the kernel of the first two normal traces, while it
sends the full-zero endpoint onto the kernel of the first three normal traces.
Roodenburg's arbitrary-smoothness interpolation theorem then shows that the
extra second-normal-trace condition is invisible precisely below the
conjectured threshold.  Reiteration supplies the two excluded critical trace
indices.
