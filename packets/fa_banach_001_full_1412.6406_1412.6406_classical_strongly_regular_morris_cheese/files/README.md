# Classical strongly regular Morris Swiss cheese

Status: **candidate full affirmative answer, likely valid, pending human review**.

This packet answers the existential half of Question 1 in J. F. Feinstein,
S. Morley, and H. Yang, *Swiss Cheeses and Their Applications*
(arXiv:1412.6406): the classical cheese in Theorem 8.3 can be chosen so that
`R(X_B)` is strongly regular, while retaining the protected unit circle and
the weighted boundary estimate that makes the algebra non-weakly-amenable.

The proof combines a Cayley-chart transfer of Alexander Izzo's weighted
strong-regularity construction (arXiv:2211.14684) with a new quantitative
fixed-boundary classicalisation lemma.  The packet does not answer the
universal “Must it be regular?” sentence, the broader no-point-derivations
question, or the weak-amenability/triviality question.
