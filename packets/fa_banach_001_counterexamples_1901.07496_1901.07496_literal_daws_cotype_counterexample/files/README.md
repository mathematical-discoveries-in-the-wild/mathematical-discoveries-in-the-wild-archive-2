# The displayed Daws cotype conjecture is false as written

**Status:** candidate counterexample (likely valid; scope/notation review requested)

Conjecture 2 on printed page 7 of arXiv:1901.07496 states, without an exponent restriction, that (B_p(\Gamma)) has cotype (q) whenever (1/p+1/q=1). Taking (p=3), (q=3/2), and even the trivial group disproves the statement: (B_3(\{e\})\cong\mathbb C), while no nonzero Banach space has Rademacher cotype (r<2).

The result is deliberately scoped to the literal displayed conjecture. Daws's cited paper actually conjectures cotype (\max(p,p')), and the notation for (B_p) changes conventions between the two papers. Thus the intended corrected conjecture remains open; the packet diagnoses an omitted range/convention, not a disproof of Daws's original formulation or of Conjecture 1 in arXiv:1901.07496.
