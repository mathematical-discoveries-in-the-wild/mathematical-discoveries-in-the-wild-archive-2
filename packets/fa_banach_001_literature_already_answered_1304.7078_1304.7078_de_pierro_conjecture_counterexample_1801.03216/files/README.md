# De Pierro's conjecture: explicit later counterexample

Status: `literature_already_answered`

Original source: J.-B. Baillon, P. L. Combettes, and R. Cominetti,
*Asymptotic behavior of compositions of under-relaxed nonexpansive
operators*, arXiv:1304.7078.  Section 3 states that De Pierro's conjecture
remained open for general convex sets, while the paper settles the case of two
closed convex sets in Euclidean space.

Answer source: R. Cominetti, V. Roshchina, and A. Williamson,
*A counterexample to De Pierro's conjecture on the convergence of
under-relaxed cyclic projections*, arXiv:1801.03216.

The later paper explicitly names the same conjecture and disproves it by
constructing three compact convex sets in `R^3` whose under-relaxed limit
cycles fail to converge as the relaxation parameter tends to zero.  Thus the
general conjecture extracted from arXiv:1304.7078 is false.  The positive
two-set Euclidean theorem in the original paper is unaffected.
