\section*{Source question}

Becerra Guerrero, L\'opez-P\'erez, and Rueda Zoca,
arXiv:1407.6038, prove in Corollary 3.6 that if Banach spaces $X$ and
$Y$ both have the strong diameter two property (SD2P), then
$X\widehat\otimes_\pi Y$ has SD2P.  Immediately afterward they ask whether
one SD2P factor is enough, and suggest
$X\widehat\otimes_\pi\ell_2^2$ as a possible negative candidate.  The
question occurs on source PDF page 7.

The scanner's earlier introductory ``natural open question'' is not a fresh
target: it asks about stability from both factors and is answered by the same
paper's Corollary 3.6.

\section*{Later full negative answer}

Langemets, Lima, and Rueda Zoca, arXiv:1609.02062, Corollary 3.9
(supporting PDF page 10), prove that for every $2<p<\infty$ and $n\ge3$,
\[
 \ell_\infty\widehat\otimes_\pi\ell_p^n
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 L_\infty[0,1]\widehat\otimes_\pi\ell_p^n
\]
do not have SD2P.  On the other hand, $L_\infty[0,1]$ has the Daugavet
property and therefore has SD2P.  Thus the second tensor product is an
explicit counterexample to preservation from one factor.

Remark 3.11 of the answering paper (supporting PDF page 11) records the
conclusion verbatim in mathematical substance: projective tensor products
preserve SD2P when both factors have it, but not in general when only one
does.  This fully and negatively resolves the source question.
