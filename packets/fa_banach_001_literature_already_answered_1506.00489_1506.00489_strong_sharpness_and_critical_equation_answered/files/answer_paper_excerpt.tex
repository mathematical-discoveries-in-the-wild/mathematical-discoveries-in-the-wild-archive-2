\section*{Verified theorem excerpt from Hyder, arXiv:1510.06662}

This is a review transcription, not a facsimile of the answer paper.  It was
checked against the official 30-page arXiv PDF on 11 August 2026:
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.06662}{arXiv:1510.06662}.

\paragraph{Theorem 1.1 (official PDF page 3).}
Let $\Omega\subset\R^n$ be open with finite measure, let $1<p<\infty$, and let
$f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ be Borel measurable with $f(t)\to\infty$ as
$t\to\infty$.  With $p'=p/(p-1)$ and the sharp Martinazzi constant
$\alpha_{n,p}$,
\[
 \sup_{\substack{u\in\widetilde H^{n/p,p}(\Omega)\\
 \|u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}^p+
 \|(-\Delta)^{n/(2p)}u\|_{L^p(\R^n)}^p\le1}}
 \int_\Omega f(|u|)e^{\alpha_{n,p}|u|^{p'}}\,dx=\infty.
\]
The paper explicitly identifies this as a positive answer to Martinazzi's
question and notes that the use of the full norm makes the result stronger.

\paragraph{Theorem 1.3 (official PDF page 5).}
Let $\Omega\subset\R^n$ be open with finite measure, $0<\lambda<\lambda_1$,
and $b>0$.  Then there is a nonzero weak solution of
\[
 (-\Delta)^{n/2}u=\lambda u e^{bu^2}\quad\hbox{in }\Omega,
 \qquad u\in\widetilde H^{n/2,2}(\Omega).
\]
No parity restriction on $n$ is imposed.
