# Literature answer: strong fractional sharpness and the model critical equation

status: `literature_already_answered`

source_arxiv: `1506.00489`

answer_arxiv: `1510.06662`

## Result

Hyder's Theorem 1.1 explicitly answers Martinazzi's strong endpoint-sharpness
question affirmatively, and does so under the smaller admissible class defined
by the full fractional Sobolev norm.  Hyder's Theorem 1.3 also proves existence
for the highlighted critical equation in all dimensions, including odd
dimensions at least three.

The packet records later partial progress on the infinite-domain and
Sobolev--Slobodeckij questions without claiming those exact sharp formulations
are fully resolved.
