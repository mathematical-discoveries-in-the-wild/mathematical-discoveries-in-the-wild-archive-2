# arXiv:2503.23248 — both two-dimensional wave-operator problems are affirmative corollaries of Xia (2004)

Status: literature_already_answered (direct corollary; likely valid, pending
human review because the 2025 survey does not cite the applicable theorem).

Problems 1 and 2 ask, for two commuting self-adjoint pairs differing
coordinatewise by the Lorentz ideal C_2^-, whether a directional strong wave
operator exists and whether two such directional limits agree.

Jingbo Xia's 2004 Theorem 3 proves existence and sequence-independence of wave
operators on the non-diagonalizable summand for perturbations in the
finite-rank closure of every norm ideal other than trace class. For C_2^-:

- finite-rank operators are dense;
- the ideal is not trace class;
- the source survey's exact quasicentral-modulus formula shows that an
  absolutely continuous pair has no nonzero diagonalizable summand;
- directional exponentials are admissible by the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma.

Xia's theorem therefore acts on the whole Hilbert space. It gives the
continuous-time limit in Problem 1, and sequence-independence (or an interlaced
sequence) gives equality in Problem 2.
