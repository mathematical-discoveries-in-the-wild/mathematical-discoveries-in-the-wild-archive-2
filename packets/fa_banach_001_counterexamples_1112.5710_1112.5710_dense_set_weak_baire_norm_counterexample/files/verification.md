# Verification report

Status: candidate counterexample, likely valid pending human review.

## Mathematical audit

- Countable dependence: the family of sets belonging to a sigma-algebra
  generated by a countable subfamily is itself a sigma-algebra. Applying this
  to countably many rational sublevel sets proves the lemma exactly.
- Borel coding: D_n=n sqrt(2)+Q are pairwise disjoint countable dense Borel
  sets; their union is countable and its complement is Borel. Therefore the
  countable-valued psi is Borel.
- Density: the graph of psi meets every open rectangle in
  R x [0,infinity). Radial rescaling in the Hilbert summand turns this into
  norm density of A and yields the exact distance calculation in the proof.
- Relative measurability: on A, the norm agrees with a Borel function of the
  continuous linear first-coordinate projection, so it is measurable for the
  trace of Ba(X,w).
- Global obstruction: countably many Riesz vectors have separable closed span
  in a nonseparable Hilbert space. A nonzero orthogonal vector has the same
  recorded coordinates as zero, contradicting norm dependence.
- Scope: this is a counterexample for arbitrary norm-dense sets. The dense
  linear-subspace variant is explicitly not claimed.
