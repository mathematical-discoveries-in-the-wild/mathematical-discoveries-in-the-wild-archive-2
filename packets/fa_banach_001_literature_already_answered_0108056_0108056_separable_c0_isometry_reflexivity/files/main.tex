\section*{Source question}

F. Cabello S\'anchez and L. Moln\'ar,
\emph{Reflexivity of the isometry group of some classical spaces},
arXiv:math/0108056; Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 18 (2002), 409--430.

At the end of the paper (arXiv PDF page 18, journal page 430), the authors
ask:
\begin{quote}
Let $X$ be a locally compact Hausdorff space.  If $C_0(X)$ is separable
(equivalently, the one-point compactification of $X$ is metrizable), must
$\operatorname{Iso}(C_0(X))$ be algebraically reflexive?
\end{quote}
They observe that an affirmative answer would cover every separable
commutative $C^*$-algebra.

\section*{Exact supporting theorem}

F. Cabello S\'anchez,
\emph{Local isometries on spaces of continuous functions},
Math. Z. 251 (2005), 735--749, DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-005-0814-9}
{10.1007/s00209-005-0814-9}.

Appendix, Theorem 1 (journal page 748; supporting PDF page 14) states:
\begin{quote}
Let $L$ be a locally compact space whose one-point compactification is
metrizable.  Then $\operatorname{Iso}(C_0^{\mathbb C}(L))$ is
algebraically reflexive.
\end{quote}
The supporting paper cites the source paper as reference 5 and explicitly
provides a correction of the proof of this result.  Its introduction also
lists the same assertion as one of the two basic known complex-case results.

\section*{Identification}

The hypotheses and conclusion coincide exactly.  The source itself states
that separability of $C_0(X)$ means metrizability of the one-point
compactification.  Applying the 2005 theorem with $L=X$ therefore gives an
affirmative answer:
\[
 C_0(X)\text{ separable}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Iso}(C_0(X))\text{ algebraically reflexive}.
\]
No part of the stated complex-valued question remains open.  The supporting
paper separately emphasizes that analogous questions for real-valued
function spaces are harder; that distinction does not limit the source's
complex Banach-algebra question.
