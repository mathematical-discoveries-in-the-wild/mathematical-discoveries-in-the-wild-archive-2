\section*{Source questions}

Plebanek and Sobota, \emph{Countable tightness in the spaces of regular
probability measures}, arXiv:1405.2527 (2014), ask:
\begin{enumerate}
\item in Main Problem~1.1, whether countable tightness of \(\PK\) forces
every \(\mu\in\PK\) to have countable Maharam type;
\item in Problem~5.1, whether countable tightness of \(\PK\) forces countable
tightness of \(P(K\times K)\);
\item in Problem~5.4, whether Corson's property (C) for \(C(K)\) forces
property (C) for \(C(K\times K)\).
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Supporting answer}

Koszmider and Silber, \emph{Countably tight dual ball with a nonseparable
measure}, arXiv:2312.02750 (2023), prove in Theorem~1, assuming
\(\diamondsuit\), that there is a compact Hausdorff space \(K\) such that
\(\PK\) has countable tightness but \(K\) carries a Radon probability measure
of uncountable Maharam type.  This directly answers Question~1 negatively.

The first corollary immediately after their theorem states that the same
\(K\) satisfies
\[
  \PK\text{ has countable tightness},\qquad
  P(K\times K)\text{ has uncountable tightness}.
\]
This is exactly a negative answer to source Problem~5.1.

Their next corollary states
\[
 C(K)\text{ has property (C)},\qquad
 C(K\times K)\text{ does not have property (C)},
\]
which is exactly a negative answer to source Problem~5.4.

The identification is explicit rather than retrospective: the supporting
authors cite the Plebanek--Sobota result, explain that their example separates
the tightness of \(P(K\times K)\) from that of \(\PK\times\PK\), and identify
the property-(C) corollary as answering Pol's question.

\section*{Scope and remaining questions}

The construction requires \(\diamondsuit\).  The supporting paper also recalls
Fremlin's consistency result that such examples need not exist, so the packet
records relative consistency and not a ZFC disproof.

Source Problem~5.3, asking whether property (C) of \(C(K)\) implies countable
tightness of \(\PK\), is explicitly said in the supporting paper to remain
open in ZFC.  The source question concerning Fr\'echet--Urysohn spaces of
measures and countably determined measures is not settled by this construction.
