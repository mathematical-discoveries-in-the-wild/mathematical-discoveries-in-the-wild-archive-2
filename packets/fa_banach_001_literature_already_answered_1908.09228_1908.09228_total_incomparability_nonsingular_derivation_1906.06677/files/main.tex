\noindent\textbf{Status: literature already answered (negative).}

\section*{Original problem}
In Problem 8.10 of \cite{DPTI} (source PDF p.~20), after Propositions 8.8
and 8.9, the authors ask:
if $X_\theta$ is totally incomparable with $X_t$ for every
$t\ne\theta$ in a neighborhood of $\theta$, must the associated
interpolation differential $\Omega_\theta$ be singular?  The next sentence
explicitly points to \cite{HOD} for the negative solution.

\section*{Supporting counterexample}
Section 8.1 of \cite{HOD} (supporting PDF p.~35), titled ``A totally incomparable family with
nonsingular derivation at any point'', gives the promised construction.
Starting from the couple $(\ell_\infty,\ell_1)$ and its second Rochberg
spaces, the authors obtain a family whose spaces at distinct real parameters
are pairwise totally incomparable (by $\ell_{1/t}$-saturation), whereas the
associated differentials are not singular at any parameter.  This violates
the proposed implication exactly.

The supporting authors explicitly know they are answering this problem; the
relation is not an agent-derived reformulation.  The existing
\texttt{1906.06677\_rochberg\_E\_mn\_embedding} packet concerns a separate question and
is unaffected.

\section*{Search evidence}
The original paper names arXiv:1906.06677 in the sentence announcing the
negative solution.  Inspection of that source located the explicit
proposition and proof in its applications/counterexamples section.
