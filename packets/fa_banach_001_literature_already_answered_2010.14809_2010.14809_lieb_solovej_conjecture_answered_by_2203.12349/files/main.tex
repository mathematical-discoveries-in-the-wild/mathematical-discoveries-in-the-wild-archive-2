\section*{Status}

This is a \textbf{literature\_already\_answered} packet for the full
problem. It records a separate later paper that explicitly resolves the
Lieb--Solovej conjecture studied in arXiv:2010.14809. It is not a new proof
packet.

\section*{Source Conjecture}

The source is D. B\'ekoll\`e, J. Gonessa, and B. F. Sehba,
\emph{About a conjecture of Lieb--Solovej}, arXiv:2010.14809
\cite{BGS}. Equation (1.1), beginning on PDF page 1 and continuing on PDF
page 2, asks whether for every real $s\geq 1$ and every
$F\in A^2(\Pi^+)$,
\begin{equation}
 \int_{\Pi^+}|F(x+iy)|^{2s}y^{2s-2}\,dx\,dy
 \leq
 \frac{\pi^{1-s}}{(2s-1)2^{2s-2}}
 \left(\int_{\Pi^+}|F(x+iy)|^2\,dx\,dy\right)^s.
 \label{eq:source}
\end{equation}
The text on PDF page 2 explains that the open part is the non-integer case and
says that the authors do not know a general proof. Section 4 gives the
equivalent disk form
\begin{equation}
 \int_{\mathbb D}|f(z)|^{2s}(1-|z|^2)^{2s-2}\,dx\,dy
 \leq \frac{\pi^{1-s}}{2s-1}
 \left(\int_{\mathbb D}|f(z)|^2\,dx\,dy\right)^s.
 \label{eq:disk}
\end{equation}

\section*{Supporting Answer}

The supporting paper is A. Kulikov, \emph{Functionals with extrema at
reproducing kernels}, arXiv:2203.12349, published in \emph{Geometric and
Functional Analysis} 32 (2022), 938--949 \cite{Kulikov}. Its abstract and
introduction explicitly state that it confirms the Lieb--Solovej
Wehrl-type/Bergman contractivity conjecture.

Kulikov normalizes hyperbolic measure and weighted Bergman norms by
\[
 dm(z)=\frac{dx\,dy}{\pi(1-|z|^2)^2},\qquad
 \|f\|_{A^p_\alpha}^p
 =\int_{\mathbb D}(\alpha-1)|f(z)|^p(1-|z|^2)^\alpha\,dm(z).
\]
Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.3 on supporting-PDF page 3 prove that
\[
 \|f\|_{A^q_\beta}\leq \|f\|_{A^p_\alpha}
 \quad\text{when}\quad
 0<p<q,\quad 1<\alpha<\beta,\quad \frac p\alpha=\frac q\beta.
\]

\section*{Exact Identification}

Fix $s>1$ and substitute
\[
 (p,\alpha)=(2,2),\qquad (q,\beta)=(2s,2s).
\]
The ratio condition is satisfied, and the definitions give
\[
 \|f\|_{A^2_2}^2=\frac1\pi\int_{\mathbb D}|f|^2\,dx\,dy
\]
and
\[
 \|f\|_{A^{2s}_{2s}}^{2s}
 =\frac{2s-1}{\pi}
 \int_{\mathbb D}|f|^{2s}(1-|z|^2)^{2s-2}\,dx\,dy.
\]
Raising Kulikov's contractive inequality to the power $2s$ and rearranging
produces exactly \eqref{eq:disk}, including its sharp constant. The Cayley
transfer used in the source paper then gives \eqref{eq:source}. The endpoint
$s=1$ is an identity. Thus arXiv:2203.12349 answers the full conjecture.

\section*{Scope and Search Evidence}

No part of the stated inequality remains open in this packet's scope.
Kulikov's theorem is stronger: it treats general convex functionals and
identifies normalized reproducing kernels as maximizers. Local cheap indexes
had no exact record for arXiv:2010.14809 at reservation time. A bounded search
on August 11, 2026 for the exact conjecture name, the sharp Bergman embedding,
and its $SU(1,1)$ formulation found arXiv:2203.12349; its explicit attribution
and the normalization calculation above determine the classification as an
already-known later answer.
