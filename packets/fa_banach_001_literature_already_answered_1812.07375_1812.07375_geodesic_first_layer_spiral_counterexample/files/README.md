# Geodesic first-layer conjecture: literature answer and spiral counterexample

Conjecture 2.14 of arXiv:1812.07375 says that a separable nilpotent metric
scalable group with geodesic distance should be generated by its first layer.
The conjecture is false and was already answered negatively in Terhi
Moisala's 2020 thesis.

This packet gives the simpler finite-dimensional witness

`G=(R^2,+)`, `d=d_2`, `delta_t(x)=t R_{log|t|}x` for `t != 0`.

It checks every scalable-group axiom and proves `V_1(G)={0}` from the single
identity at `s=t=1`.  The construction is an independently derived continuous
specialization of the rotation template in Remark 3.4 of arXiv:2101.03979.
No novelty claim is made.
