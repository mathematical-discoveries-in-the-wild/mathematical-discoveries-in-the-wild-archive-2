S. Thangavelu, *Toeplitz operators with special symbols on Segal-Bargmann
spaces* (arXiv:0907.2844), conjectures that on the Hermite--Bergman space
`H_t(C^n)`, under condition `(*)`, `T_g` is bounded exactly when
`g*q_{sinh(4t)/8}` is bounded.

Sam Looi, *A counterexample to the Berger--Coburn conjecture*

standard-Fock symbol with bounded Toeplitz extension and unbounded critical

to a Fock space of Gaussian scale `S=sinh(4t)`.  Under the dilation
`z -> sqrt(2/S)z`, Looi's critical time `1/4` becomes `S/8`.  His symbol thus
transports to a counterexample for every `t>0` and `n>=1`.
