## Primary-source search

Bounded searches through 13 August 2026 covered:

- the exact conjecture and title;
- ordinary/standard Hausdorff density in equiregular sub-Riemannian geometry;
- local and tangent isodiametric constants;
- the exact text of Ghezzi--Jean Question 2;
- measured Hausdorff blow-ups and nilpotent approximations.

The searches found the source, Rigot's Carnot-group identity, the 2017 arXiv
version of Ghezzi--Jean's later paper, and work on spherical measures and
isodiametric sets. Ghezzi--Jean explicitly restate the density issue as open
and isolate the needed limit `I(p,epsilon) -> I_p`. No later primary source
answering that limit or the original conjecture was found.

The key apparently new observation is to rescale each arbitrary small
competitor at its own diameter. This converts the standard uniform pairwise
metric blow-up into a relative diameter estimate uniform over all shapes and
all scales below the cutoff.
