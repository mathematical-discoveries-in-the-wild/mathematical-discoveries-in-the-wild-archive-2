# Ordinary Hausdorff density from tangent isodiametric volume

Full proof packet for the conjecture in arXiv:1005.0540.

The packet proves that at every regular point `p`, the Federer density
`d mu / d H^Q(p)` equals the maximal induced Haar volume of a diameter-one
set in the nilpotent approximation. It also proves continuity of this
constant and answers Questions 1--3 about ordinary Hausdorff measure in
arXiv:1702.00241.

Key files:

- `solution_packet.pdf`: compiled proof.
- `source_paper.pdf`: official arXiv PDF for 1005.0540.
- `figures/source_conjecture_crop.png`: readable crop of the exact conjecture.
- `supporting_papers/1702.00241.pdf`: later equivalent formulation and
  uniform metric blow-up facts.
- `supporting_papers/1004.1369.pdf`: tangent isodiametric/Hausdorff identity.
- `verification_report.md`: mathematical and artifact audit.
- `novelty.md`: bounded novelty search record.
