\begin{abstract}
The source proves regularity of a generalized inverse for
$f\in W^{1,p}$, $p>n-1$, and conjectures the endpoint $p=n-1$.
The source's Morrey diameter estimate cannot survive critically.  We prove
instead that three explicit structural properties---multiplicity one on the
positive-Jacobian set, projection-linking of inverse images of target
segments, and a critical radial coarea inequality---imply the full desired
$W^{1,1}$ and finite-distortion conclusion.  The proof gives an
$L^1$-Poincar\'e inequality and isolates critical surface Lusin $(N)$ as the
remaining analytic obstruction.  We also verify the full endpoint conclusion
for all radial finite-distortion cavitation and collapsed-core maps.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact source problem}

The source proves Theorem 1.2 for $p>n-1$ and then states verbatim:
\begin{quote}
``We conjecture that it suffices to take $p=n-1$ in Theorem 1.2. However,
under this weaker assumption, one cannot conclude that $h$ is differentiable
almost everywhere.''
\end{quote}

\includegraphics[width=0.75\linewidth]{figures/open_problem_crop.png}

Here finite distortion is used in the source's sense:
$J_f\ge0$ and $J_f(x)=0$ implies $Df(x)=0$ a.e.  Consequently
$\adj Df=0$ a.e. on $\{J_f=0\}$.

\section{The three structural gateway properties}

Put $m=n-1$.  Let $G$ be a measurable subset of $\Omega$ on which
$J_f>0$, $f$ is approximately differentiable, and $f$ is one-to-one.  Let
$h\in L^\infty_{\rm loc}(\Omega';\R^n)$ be a proposed generalized inverse
with $h(f(x))=x$ for a.e. $x\in G$.

We isolate the following properties.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(M)] Up to null sets, $G$ contains $\{J_f>0\}$ and $f|_G$ has
multiplicity one.
\item[(L)] For a.e. $y_0,y_1\in\Omega'$ and each coordinate projection
$\pi_j:\R^n\to\R$,
\[
 |h_j(y_1)-h_j(y_0)|
 \leq \mathcal H^1\!\left(
 \pi_j\bigl(f^{-1}([y_0,y_1])\bigr)\right).
\]
The inverse image is understood topologically at cavity values.  This is the
projection-linking conclusion obtained when nested topological images prevent
a hyperplane from separating a fiber over a connected target set.
\item[(C)] For a.e. $y_0$, every measurable $E\Subset\Omega$, and
$q_{y_0}(x)=(f(x)-y_0)/|f(x)-y_0|$, one has
\[
 \int_{\sphere}\mathcal H^1\!\left(
 \pi_j(E\cap q_{y_0}^{-1}(z))\right)d\mathcal H^{n-1}(z)
 \leq \int_E |\adj Dq_{y_0}(x)|\,dx .
\]
\end{enumerate}

Property (C) is the radial coarea inequality.  For a general critical Sobolev
map, its null-set clause is not automatic.  Cs\"ornyei--Hencl--Mal\'y prove it
for endpoint homeomorphisms by first establishing Lusin $(N)$ on almost every
source hyperplane.

\section{The gateway theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Critical radial-coarea gateway]\label{thm:gateway}
Let $n\ge3$ and let $f\in W^{1,n-1}_{\rm loc}(\Omega;\R^n)$ have finite
distortion.  Suppose that a bounded generalized inverse $h$ satisfies
\textup{(M)}, \textup{(L)}, and \textup{(C)} locally.  Then
$h\in W^{1,1}_{\rm loc}(\Omega';\R^n)$.  More precisely, there is
$g\in L^1_{\rm loc}(\Omega')$ such that every ball
$B=B(a,r)\Subset\Omega'$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:poincare}
 \int_B |h-h_B|\,dy\leq C_n r\int_B g(y)\,dy.
\end{equation}
The weak derivative of $h$ vanishes a.e. outside $f(G)$ and satisfies
$Dh(y)=[Df(h(y))]^{-1}$ a.e. on $f(G)$.  In particular, $h$ has finite
distortion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix $B=B(a,r)\Subset\Omega'$ and $y_0\in B$ satisfying the gateway
properties.  If $y_1\in B$ and
$z=(y_1-y_0)/|y_1-y_0|$, then the segment $[y_0,y_1]$ lies in $B$.
Property (L), followed by (C) on $E=f^{-1}(B)$, gives after integration on a
target sphere centered at $y_0$
\begin{align*}
 &\int_{B\cap\partial B(y_0,s)}|h(y)-h(y_0)|\,d\mathcal H^{n-1}(y)\\
 &\qquad\leq C_n s^{n-1}
 \int_{f^{-1}(B)}|\adj Dq_{y_0}(x)|\,dx\\
 &\qquad\leq C_n s^{n-1}
 \int_{f^{-1}(B)}
 \frac{|\adj Df(x)|}{|f(x)-y_0|^{n-1}}\,dx .
\end{align*}
The last inequality is the adjugate chain-rule estimate for radial
projection.  Integrating in $s$ over the part of each ray contained in $B$
and using $s\le2r$ yields
\[
 |h(y_0)-h_B|
 \leq C_n\int_{f^{-1}(B)}
 \frac{|\adj Df(x)|}{|f(x)-y_0|^{n-1}}\,dx .
\]
Integrate this in $y_0\in B$ and use
\[
 \int_B |z-y_0|^{1-n}\,dy_0\leq C_n r
 \qquad(z\in\R^n).
\]
We obtain the critical Poincar\'e estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:source-poincare}
 \int_B|h-h_B|\,dy
 \leq C_n r\int_{f^{-1}(B)}|\adj Df(x)|\,dx.
\end{equation}

Finite distortion makes the integrand zero on $\{J_f=0\}$.  By (M), the
area formula on the countable Lipschitz decomposition of the approximately
differentiable set gives
\[
 \int_{f^{-1}(B)}|\adj Df|,dx
 =\int_B g(y)\,dy,
 \quad
 g(y)=\frac{|\adj Df(h(y))|}{J_f(h(y))}\mathbf1_{f(G)}(y).
\]
Moreover, $g\in L^1_{\rm loc}$ because its integral is bounded by
$C_n\int |Df|^{n-1}$.  Thus \eqref{eq:source-poincare} is
\eqref{eq:poincare}.  The standard $1$-Poincar\'e characterization of
$W^{1,1}$ now gives $h\in W^{1,1}_{\rm loc}$, with $|Dh|\leq C_ng$ a.e.
In particular $Dh=0$ off $f(G)$.

On each Lipschitz piece of $G$, the inverse differentiation identity gives
$Dh(y)=[Df(h(y))]^{-1}$ for a.e. $y\in f(G)$.  Hence $J_h>0$ wherever
$Dh\ne0$, while both vanish off the good image.  This is finite distortion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What remains for the original conjecture]
In the source's supercritical argument, strong $(INV+)$ and nested
topological images yield (L), while surface Lusin $(N)$ yields (C) and is also
used to obtain (M).  At $p=n-1$, continuous surface Sobolev maps can fail
Lusin $(N)$.  Theorem~\ref{thm:gateway} therefore removes the false critical
Morrey step and shows that the unresolved issue is structural: prove these
three consequences from finite distortion plus $(INV)$, or construct a map
where a critical exceptional surface creates singular inverse variation.
\end{remark}

\section{An exact endpoint family}

\begin{proposition}[Radial cavitation and core collapse]
Let $f:B_R\to B_S$ be radial,
\[
 f(x)=\rho(|x|)\frac{x}{|x|},
\]
where $\rho$ is nondecreasing and absolutely continuous away from the origin,
$f\in W^{1,n-1}$, and $f$ has finite distortion.  Allow either a point
cavity $\rho(0+)=b>0$ or a collapsed core $\rho^{-1}(0)=[0,r_0]$.
Then the natural radial generalized inverse is in $W^{1,1}(B_S;\R^n)$ and
has finite distortion.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Where $\rho>0$, the eigenvalues of $Df$ are $\rho'$ in the radial direction
and $\rho/r$ in the $n-1$ tangential directions.  Finite distortion forces
$\rho'>0$ a.e. there.  Hence the inverse radius $\sigma$ is absolutely
continuous on $(b,S)$ and the inverse derivative has eigenvalues
$\sigma'(s)$ and $\sigma(s)/s$.  Set $\sigma=0$ in the cavity; at the single
collapsed-core image point choose any value.  Since $0\leq\sigma\leq R$,
\begin{align*}
 \int_{B_S}|Dh|
 &\leq C_n\int_b^S
 \left(\sigma'(s)+\frac{\sigma(s)}s\right)s^{n-1},ds\\
 &\leq C_n\left(RS^{n-1}+
 \int_b^S\sigma(s)s^{n-2},ds\right)<\infty.
\end{align*}
The inverse eigenvalues are all positive off the constant cavity region and
the exceptional target point.  Thus the generalized inverse has finite
distortion.
\end{proof}

\section{Limitations and novelty status}

The theorem is conditional and does not prove that endpoint $(INV)$ implies
strong $(INV+)$, surface Lusin $(N)$, or a canonical fiber selection.  A
direct logarithmic-spike test shows that no local $W^{1,n-1}$ oscillation
estimate can repair the source proof.  Attempts to turn the source's slab
jump example into a finite-distortion counterexample via a Ponomarev
homeomorphism fail for topological reasons: a homeomorphism cannot map a null
set onto the open bad slab.  A subtler cell-like construction is not ruled
out.

No later primary source located in bounded searches through August 13, 2026
claims the full endpoint.  Priority is not asserted, and specialist review of
the gateway hypotheses and the $1$-Poincar\'e characterization is recommended.
