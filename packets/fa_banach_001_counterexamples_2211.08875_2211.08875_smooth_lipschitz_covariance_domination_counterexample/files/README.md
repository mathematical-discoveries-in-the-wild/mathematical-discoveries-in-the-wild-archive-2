# Smooth covariance-domination counterexample

This packet gives a candidate full counterexample to the natural
smoothness-only interpretation of the covariance-bound question on page 31 of
arXiv `2211.08875`.

Even with a compactly supported, injective-covariance design `X` in `ell_2`,
a bounded noiseless response `Y=f(X)`, and a globally Lipschitz Frechet-smooth function
`f:ell_2->R` whose derivatives of every order are bounded, one can have

`C_XY C_YX` not dominated by any finite multiple of `C_XX^2`.

Independent centered bounded noise can be added without changing the
conclusion.

Status: candidate full counterexample, likely valid subject to human review.
