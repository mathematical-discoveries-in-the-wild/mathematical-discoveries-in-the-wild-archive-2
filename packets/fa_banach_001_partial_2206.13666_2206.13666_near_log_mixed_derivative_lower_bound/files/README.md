# Near-logarithmic quantitative Ornstein lower bound

Source: Krystian Kazaniecki and Michał Wojciechowski, *On Bernstein type
quantitative estimates for Ornstein non-inequalities*, arXiv:2206.13666.

Status: candidate substantial partial result; complete proof in `main.tex` and
`solution_packet.pdf`.

Let `K_N` be the least constant such that every trigonometric polynomial of
coordinate degree at most `N` on the two-torus satisfies

```text
||f_xy||_1 <= K_N (||f_xx||_1 + ||f_yy||_1).
```

The source proves `K_N >= c sqrt(log N)`, recalls `K_N <= C log N`, and
conjectures that the logarithmic exponent one is sharp.  This packet proves

```text
K_N >= c log N / log log N
```

for all sufficiently large `N`.

The construction uses the Riesz product generated by

```text
a_k = (M^k, (-1)^k M^k),   M = n.
```

After inverting the `xx` derivative on its Fourier support, the `xy`
multiplier is approximately `(-1)^k` on the `k`-th Riesz block, whereas the
`yy` multiplier is approximately one.  A blockwise analytic expansion and
the `L1` Bernstein inequality bound the total multiplier error by `O(n/M)`.
Meyer lacunary transference and the Latała finite-Riesz-product inequality
give size `cn` for the alternating mixed-derivative main term.  Since the
degree is at most `2 n^n`, inversion yields the stated near-logarithmic bound.
