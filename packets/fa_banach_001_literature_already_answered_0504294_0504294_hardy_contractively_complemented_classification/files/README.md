# Hardy-space 1-complemented subspaces: later classification

Status: `literature_already_answered`

The closing question on printed page 8 of arXiv:math/0504294 asks whether
every nontrivial 1-complemented subspace of `H^p`, `1 <= p < infinity`,
`p != 2`, is isometric to one of the model spaces `H^psi_{p,Lambda}`.

Fu--Guo--Li, arXiv:2412.14808v4, cite the 2005 paper and provide a complete
classification. Their Theorem 1.2 describes every rank-greater-than-one
contractive projection, and Corollary 1.4 states that every nontrivial
1-complemented subspace of `H^p` is isometric to `H^p`. Taking `psi(z)=z`
and the full congruence class `Lambda=N_0` gives `H^psi_{p,Lambda}=H^p`, so
the source question has an affirmative answer.

The rank-one exception is excluded by the source context: the question occurs
in its discussion of infinite-dimensional ranges, after the paper has already
shown that no finite-dimensional range of dimension greater than one exists.
