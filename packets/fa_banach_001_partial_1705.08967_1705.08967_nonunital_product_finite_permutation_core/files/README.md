# Finite permutation-core reduction for the nonunital product question

This packet addresses the open question after Proposition 5.1(E) of
arXiv:1705.08967.  It does **not** claim a complete answer.  It gives an exact
finite-SGT amenability criterion, exhaustive counterexample exclusion through
order three, a large order-four self-product audit, and the precise obstruction
encountered by the natural full iterated-mean proof.

Files:

- `solution_packet.pdf` — standalone mathematical/computational note.
- `source_paper.pdf` — locally compiled source paper.
- `main.tex` — packet source.
- `verification.md` — mathematical, computational, build, and visual checks.

Reproducible search and verification programs are in
`runs/fa_banach_001/attempts/` with prefix `1705.08967_`.
