# Norm-resolvent convergence for singular rotating-wave models

The 2022 paper arXiv:2210.15267 asks whether its strong-resolvent cutoff
approximation in the renormalized rotating-wave regime can be improved.  The
later paper arXiv:2502.04876 proves norm-resolvent convergence for precisely
this interaction: its spin matrix is 2-nilpotent, one of the two structural
classes in the later theorem.
