# Partial solution packet for arXiv:2310.10120

This packet addresses the question whether the lower bound in Theorem 6 of
Brandolini--Colzani--Travaglini is sharp for `1 < p < 2`.

Main outcomes:

- A rigorous one-parameter family of equal-weight lower bounds interpolates
  between the source estimate and an `N^{-1-1/d}` fixed-norm endpoint.
- For every fixed `F = ||f||_p`, the optimal power of `N` in the normalized
  equal-weight probability setting is `N^{-1-1/d}`; a matching stratified
  construction is included.
- The optimal joint dependence on `N` and `F` remains open because the
  localization route encounters an uncontrolled multiscale mass split.
- Read literally with arbitrary nonnegative weights, the source inequality is
  fully sharp: a one-active-weight approximate-delta construction attains all
  of its displayed exponents.

The candidate mathematics is in `solution_packet.pdf`. The exact source
statement is shown in `figures/open_problem_crop.png`. The source PDF and the
May 2026 energy-distance comparison paper are included for audit.
