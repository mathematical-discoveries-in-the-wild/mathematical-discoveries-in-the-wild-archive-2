# Dense-common-domain weak Trotter--Kato theorem

Status: `literature_implied_answer (partial subcase)`.

Eisner--Serény, arXiv:0805.1027, Question 2.6 on PDF page 3 asks whether weak
convergence of every resolvent power at one point is equivalent to weak
convergence of the semigroups uniformly on compact time intervals.

Young Seop Lee, *A Convergence of C0 Semigroups in the Weak Topology*, Korean
J. Math. 22 (2014), 349--354, Theorem 2 on PDF page 3, proves the forward
implication for contraction semigroups on every Banach space under the extra
density assumption

`D={x in intersection D(A_n): sup_n ||A_n x||<infinity}` dense in `X`.

The source powers hypothesis implies the resolvent convergence required by
Lee, so this is a direct literature-implied answer to that subcase. Lee cites
Eisner--Serény and explicitly frames the theorem as a weak Trotter--Kato
convergence result, but does not claim the unrestricted question. The general
case without common-domain control remains open in this packet's search.
