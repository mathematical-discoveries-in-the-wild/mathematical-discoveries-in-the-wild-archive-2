\textbf{Status.} \texttt{literature\_implied\_answer (partial subcase)}.
The unrestricted Banach-space question is not claimed solved.

\textbf{Source question.} Eisner and Ser\'eny, \emph{On the weak analogue of
the Trotter--Kato theorem}, arXiv:0805.1027, Question 2.6 on PDF page 3, consider
$C_0$-semigroups $T_n,T$ with a common exponential bound and generators
$A_n,A$.  For one (equivalently every) $\lambda$ in the common right
half-plane, their property (1) is
\[
 R(\lambda,A_n)^k x \rightharpoonup R(\lambda,A)^k x
 \qquad(x\in X,\ k\in\N).
\]
They ask whether this is equivalent to $T_n(t)x\rightharpoonup T(t)x$
uniformly for $t$ in compact intervals \cite{ES}.

\textbf{Supporting theorem.} Lee, \emph{A Convergence of $C_0$ Semigroups in
the Weak Topology}, Theorem 2 on PDF page 3, proves the following.  Let
$T_n,T$ be contraction $C_0$-semigroups on an arbitrary Banach space.  If the
resolvents converge weakly to the resolvent of $A$ and
\[
 D=\left\{x\in\bigcap_{n\ge1}D(A_n):
       \sup_{n\ge1}\lVert A_nx\rVert<\infty\right\}
\]
is dense in $X$, then $T_n(t)x\rightharpoonup T(t)x$ uniformly on bounded
time intervals \cite[Theorem 2]{Lee2014}.

\textbf{Identification.} The source paper observes immediately after
property (1) that convergence of all powers at one $\lambda$ implies weak
resolvent convergence locally uniformly throughout the common right
half-plane.  It therefore satisfies Lee's resolvent hypothesis.  Lee's theorem
gives an affirmative answer to Question 2.6 for contraction semigroups having
the displayed dense common-domain boundedness property.  This implication is
an identification of the two statements, not a new theorem; Lee explicitly
cites Eisner--Ser\'eny and presents the result as weak Trotter--Kato
convergence.

\textbf{Why the extra hypothesis matters in the proof.} For $x\in D$,
\[
 \lVert T_n(h)x-x\rVert
 \le \int_0^h\lVert T_n(s)A_nx\rVert\,ds
 \le h\sup_n\lVert A_nx\rVert .
\]
Thus scalar orbits are equicontinuous uniformly in $n$ on a dense set.
Weak convergence of their Laplace transforms then upgrades to uniform weak
convergence on bounded time intervals, and density extends it to every
$x\in X$.  This pinpoints the missing ingredient in the unrestricted problem.

\textbf{Scope and search status.} The theorem does not remove the density
condition and is stated for contractions.  Bounded searches through
2026-08-13, using the exact question wording, title/authors, and the terms
``weak Trotter--Kato'' and ``weak convergence of powers of resolvents'', found
the Hilbert-space result of Marinoschi (2012), Lee's uniformly-convex-dual
extension (2013), and the general-Banach dense-domain theorem above, but no
unrestricted proof or counterexample.  Eight direct upgrade attempts are
recorded in the accompanying run attempt; their common obstruction is uniform
Yosida approximation without equicontinuity.

\textbf{Human-review recommendation.} Verify the quantifier on $\lambda$ in
Lee's Theorem 2 and the source paper's resolvent-series implication.  Then
accept this as a literature-implied partial subcase only, retaining the
unrestricted question as open.
