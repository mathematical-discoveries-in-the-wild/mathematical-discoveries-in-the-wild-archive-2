\noindent\textbf{Status.}
\texttt{literature\_implied\_answer (full negative answer)}.  The decisive
later classification is known; the short implication below is included to
match it precisely to the older conjecture.  It is not claimed as a new
counterexample.

\section*{Source question}

Kasso A. Okoudjou, \emph{Preconditioning techniques in frame theory and
probabilistic frames}, arXiv:1504.02023, Section 3.2, PDF page 29, uses
\[
  \FP_{p,M}(\Phi)=\sum_{k,\ell=1}^{M}
  |\langle\varphi_k,\varphi_\ell\rangle|^p
\]
for collections of unit vectors.  For $N\ge3$ it records the conjecture that,
with
\[
  q_N:=\frac{\log\!\bigl(N(N+1)/2\bigr)}{\log N},
\]
every $\Phi\subset S^{N-1}$ of cardinality $N+1$ satisfies
\[
  \FP_{q_N,N+1}(\Phi)\ge N+3,
\]
with equality only for an orthonormal basis plus one repeated vector or an
equiangular finite unit-norm tight frame.

\section*{Supporting theorem}

Zhiqiang Xu and Zili Xu, \emph{The minimizers of the $p$-frame potential},
arXiv:1907.10861, define the lifted ETF $L_k^d$ in equation (6), PDF page 3.
Their Theorem 1.2, PDF pages 3--4, completely determines the minimizers among
$d+1$ unit vectors in $\R^d$ for every $0<p<2$.  Their convention sums only
over $i\ne j$, which differs from Okoudjou's convention by the constant
$d+1$.

The theorem answers a later lifted-ETF phase-diagram conjecture and does not
explicitly label the exact 2015 sentence above as a target.  The following
identification extracts its direct consequence for that sentence.

\section*{Identification and strict counterexample}

Fix $N\ge3$.  In the first two coordinates of $\R^N$ set
\[
 u_1=(1,0),\qquad
 u_2=(-\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}),\qquad
 u_3=(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}),
\]
and adjoin $e_3,\ldots,e_N$.  This is the lifted ETF $L_2^N$ of Xu--Xu:
a planar Mercedes triple plus an orthonormal basis of its orthogonal
complement.  The only nonzero off-diagonal inner products are the six ordered
pairs among $u_1,u_2,u_3$, each of absolute value $1/2$.  Consequently
\[
  \FP_{q_N,N+1}(L_2^N)=N+1+6\,2^{-q_N}.
\]

It remains only to compare exponents.  Put $a=\log_2(3/2)$.  For
$h(t)=(t+1)/(2t^a)$, one has $h(2)=1$ and
\[
  \operatorname{sign} h'(t)=\operatorname{sign}\bigl((1-a)t-a\bigr).
\]
Since $a<2/3$, the latter expression is positive for $t\ge2$.  Hence
$(N+1)/2>N^a$ for every integer $N\ge3$.  Taking logarithms base $N$ gives
\[
  q_N=1+\log_N\!\left(\frac{N+1}{2}\right)
      >1+a=\log_2 3.
\]
Therefore $6\,2^{-q_N}<2$, and so
\[
  \boxed{\FP_{q_N,N+1}(L_2^N)<N+3}
\]
for every $N\ge3$.  The conjecture is false in every dimension in which it
was posed.

\section*{Provenance and scope}

The later paper proves more than this strict counterexample: it classifies the
global minimizers throughout $0<p<2$.  The present status note contributes
only the direct identification of its known lifted configuration with the
older critical exponent.  No part of the source conjecture remains open.
