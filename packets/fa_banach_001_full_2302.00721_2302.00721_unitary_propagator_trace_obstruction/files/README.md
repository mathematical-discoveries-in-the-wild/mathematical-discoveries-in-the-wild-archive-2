# arXiv:2302.00721 — exact unitary-propagator trace obstruction

Status: candidate full answer to the trace problem in Remark 3.5, likely valid, pending human review.

With the source paper's distribution-function convention, the modulus of the classical propagator `U_t = exp(itL)` is the identity. Therefore its distribution trace is `tau(I)` for thresholds below 1 and zero at or above 1. Its weak noncommutative Lorentz norm is infinite on infinite-trace algebras and is a time-independent constant on finite-trace algebras. Under the alternate literal spectral-measure reading, the projection only measures the resonant eigenspace `exp(itL) = 1`. Neither quantity detects oscillatory dispersion.
