# Literature resolution: arXiv:2409.03531

This packet identifies the direct follow-up arXiv:2409.17807 as a complete
negative literature answer to the nonmetrizable residue of the finite-index
expectation question restated in arXiv:2409.03531.

The follow-up's incidence cover gives a continuous unital subhomogeneous
commutative C(X)-algebra with no conditional expectation at all. The packet
states the construction, explains the expectation-to-continuous-selection
reduction, and separates this negative theorem from the known positive
metrizable and extremally disconnected cases.
