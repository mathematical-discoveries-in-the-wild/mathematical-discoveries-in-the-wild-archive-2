Exact answer location: Chapter 2, *Ruum ell_1 direct-sum_p R*, opening paragraph
and Theorem 2.1, printed page 14 (PDF page 14).  The opening paragraph explicitly
identifies Haller--Leo--Zavarzina's `p=2` article and says the thesis generalizes
it to arbitrary real `p>1`.  Theorem 2.1 states that for every
`p in (1,infinity)`, the unit ball of `ell_1 direct-sum_p R` is plastic.

The English abstract on PDF page 2 independently states the same result.
