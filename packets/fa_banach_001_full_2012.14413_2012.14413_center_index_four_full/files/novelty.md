amenability constants, center/centre index four, and the value `3/2`.  They

Primary-source searches were then run on arXiv for the exact question and
close variants, including:

- `Fourier algebra amenability constant center index four locally compact`;

published in 2026.  Its Theorem 1.4(iii) proves the center-index-four result
only for discrete groups.  Its concluding Question 5.3 asks whether the new
upper bound extends to all locally compact virtually abelian groups and says
that a positive answer would settle Choi's Question 6.2 in full generality.
No source found in the bounded search proves the nondiscrete case or uses the
central spectral splitting in this packet.

Novelty verdict: plausibly new as of the search date, with high but not
exhaustive confidence.  Specialist literature review remains required.
