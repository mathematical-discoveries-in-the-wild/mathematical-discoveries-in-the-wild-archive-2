# Finite-dimensional logmodular classification already answered

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Source: Vern I. Paulsen and Mrinal Raghupathi, *Representations of Logmodular
Algebras*, arXiv:0806.0284.  After Corollary 2.3 on PDF page 4, the authors say
they do not know whether every logmodular subalgebra of `M_n` is block upper
triangular, but believe this is the case.

Answer: Kate Juschenko, *A description of the logmodular subalgebras in the
finite dimensional C*-algebras*, arXiv:1003.3123.  The abstract and
introduction explicitly identify the Paulsen--Raghupathi conjecture, and
Theorem 2.6 on PDF page 5 proves that every logmodular subalgebra of
`M_n(C)` is unitarily equivalent to a block upper-triangular algebra.

The match is exact and explicit.  Juschenko's Corollary 2.7 also gives the
representation-theoretic consequence: every contractive unital homomorphism
of a finite-dimensional logmodular algebra is completely contractive.

Scope limitation: this does not answer the source's separate infinite-
dimensional questions about `H^infinity`, upper-triangular operators on
`ell_2`, completely distributive CSL algebras, or its final abstract operator
algebra `C`.
