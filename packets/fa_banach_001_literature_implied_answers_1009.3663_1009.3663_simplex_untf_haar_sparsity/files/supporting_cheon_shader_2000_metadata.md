# Decisive supporting theorem: Cheon--Shader (2000)

- Gi-Sang Cheon and Bryan L. Shader, *Sparse orthogonal matrices and the Haar
  wavelet*, Discrete Applied Mathematics 101 (2000), 63--76.
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-218X(99)00182-1>
- Publisher record: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166218X99001821>

The paper's main extremal result says that the minimum number of nonzero
entries in an `m x m` real orthogonal matrix with a full row (equivalently a
full column) is

`f(m) = (floor(log2(m))+3)m - 2^(floor(log2(m))+1)`.

It characterizes the extremizers through Haar-wavelet matrices. The formula is
also restated explicitly on page 213 of G.-S. Cheon, S.-G. Hwang, S.-H. Rim,
B. L. Shader, and S.-Z. Song, *Sparse orthogonal matrices*, Linear Algebra and
its Applications 373 (2003), 211--222, where it is attributed to the 2000 Haar
paper.
