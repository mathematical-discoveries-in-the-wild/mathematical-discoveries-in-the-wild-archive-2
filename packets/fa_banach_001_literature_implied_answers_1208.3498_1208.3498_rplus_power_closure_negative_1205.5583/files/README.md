# The scaled-power closure question has a negative answer

Status: `literature_implied_answer (negative answer; contextual
band-irreducible strengthening)`.

On PDF page 23 of arXiv:1208.3498, Gao and Troitsky ask whether the
norm-closed semigroup `R_+ T` generated by positive scalar multiples of the
powers of an order-continuous positive operator `T` must consist entirely of
order-continuous operators.

Hadwin, Kitover, and Orhon provide an independent construction in
arXiv:1205.5583v2 that answers this negatively. Their Example 11 constructs a
positive order-continuous operator `A`, and their Remark 15 (PDF page 15)
states that `A^n` converges in operator norm to a projection that is not
order-continuous. By Gao--Troitsky's definition, that projection belongs to
`R_+ A`.

The packet also records a stronger implication of the same Example 11 that
matches the band-irreducible context of the source section. The example's
operators can be combined as

`B_epsilon = A + epsilon L T`,

where `B_epsilon` is positive, order-continuous, and band irreducible. Taking
`epsilon` still smaller makes the restriction to the invariant ideal
`J={x:x(0)=0}` have spectral radius below one. Then `B_epsilon^n` converges
in norm to a rank-one projection `u tensor delta_0`, which is not
order-continuous because `delta_0` is not order-continuous. Thus the answer
remains negative even with band irreducibility.
