# References and search record

## Primary source

Joseph Thirouin, *On the Growth of Sobolev Norms of Solutions of the
Fractional Defocusing NLS Equation on the Circle*, arXiv:1506.04181;
*Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré C, Analyse non linéaire* 34 (2017),
509–531. Local copy: `source_paper.pdf`. Appendix B, equation (33), proves the
unit-time periodic `L4` estimate at `gamma=1/4-alpha/8` and asks whether it
holds at `gamma_0=1/4-alpha/4`.
