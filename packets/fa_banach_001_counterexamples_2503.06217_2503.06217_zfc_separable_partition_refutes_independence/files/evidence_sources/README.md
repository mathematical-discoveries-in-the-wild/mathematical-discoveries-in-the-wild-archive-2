# Evidence sources

## Original source

- Daniel L. Rodríguez-Vidanes and Juan Carlos Sampedro, *Isometric
  classification of the L^p-spaces of infinite dimensional Lebesgue measure*.
- arXiv:2503.06217.
- Banach Journal of Mathematical Analysis 20 (2026), article 7.
- DOI: 10.1007/s43037-025-00473-y.
- Local copy: `../source_paper.pdf`.

## Decisive locations in the source

- Lemma 2.3: finite-measure measurable subsets have separable `L^p`.
- Section 3, proof of its main theorem: a continuum-sized family `{C_A}` of
  measure-one rectangles with pairwise null intersections.
- Definition 4.2: `kappa`-separable partition.
- Theorem 4.3: such a partition implies the `ell^p`-sum isometry.
- Conjecture 4.6, printed page 19: a model of ZFC with no `c`-separable
  envelope.
- Appendix A: relative nonatomicity of the measure space.

No external mathematical theorem beyond Zorn's lemma and elementary measure
theory is used in the candidate proof.
