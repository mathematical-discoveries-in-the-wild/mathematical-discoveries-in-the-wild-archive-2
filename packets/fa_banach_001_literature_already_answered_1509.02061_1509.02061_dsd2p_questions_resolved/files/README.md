# DSD2P questions from arXiv:1509.02061: three positive literature answers

## Source

- Julio Becerra Guerrero, Ginés López-Pérez, Abraham Rueda Zoca,
  *Diametral diameter two properties in Banach spaces*, arXiv:1509.02061.
- Location: Section 4, Questions 1, 3, and 4, PDF page 24.

The selected questions ask whether DSD2P implies the Daugavet property,
whether `X` has DSD2P exactly when `X*` has weak-star DSD2P, and whether
DSD2P is stable under `ell_1`-sums.

## Status

`literature_already_answered (three full positive answers)`

Vladimir Kadets, arXiv:2008.07258, Theorem 2.3 (PDF pages 3–4), proves that
DSD2P is exactly the Daugavet property. His introduction explicitly says this
answers the source's Questions 4.1, 4.3, and 4.4 positively. Rainis Haller,
Katriin Pirk, and Märt Põldvere, arXiv:1603.08082, had already proved the
`ell_1`-sum assertion directly and explicitly identifies it as the open
problem from arXiv:1509.02061.

## Scope

This does not settle DLD2P versus DD2P, the analogous weak-star implication,
or DD2P/weak-star-DD2P duality. ArXiv:2607.04370 (July 2026) still states that
DLD2P implies DD2P—or even D2P—is open.
