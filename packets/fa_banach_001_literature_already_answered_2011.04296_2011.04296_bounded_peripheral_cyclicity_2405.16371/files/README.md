# arXiv:2011.04296 — bounded peripheral cyclicity answered later

Status: `literature_already_answered (bounded peripheral-cyclicity regime)`

Original source:

- Sahiba Arora and Jochen Glück, *Spectrum and convergence of eventually
  positive operator semigroups*, arXiv:2011.04296.
- Exact location: contributions paragraph, source PDF page 2.

Answering source:

- Sahiba Arora, *Eventually positive semigroups: spectral and asymptotic
  analysis*, arXiv:2405.16371v2.
- Exact locations: definition of uniform asymptotic positivity, PDF page 8;
  Theorem 4.7, page 12; Corollary 4.8 and the following remark, page 13.

The original paper says it is unknown whether the generator of a uniformly
eventually positive semigroup has cyclic peripheral spectrum under the usual
positive-semigroup conditions.  The later paper proves that every uniformly
asymptotically positive semigroup has cyclic peripheral spectrum.  A bounded
uniformly eventually positive semigroup is uniformly asymptotically positive,
so this directly answers the bounded regime relevant to the original
convergence theorem.  With norm continuity at infinity, the later Corollary
4.8 makes the spectral bound dominant and its following remark explicitly
weakens the original convergence results from uniform eventual positivity to
uniform asymptotic positivity.

This is deliberately scoped.  It does not answer the original paper's
separate questions about merely individually eventually positive semigroups or
about removing the first-order-pole hypothesis in its Niiro--Sawashima theorem.
