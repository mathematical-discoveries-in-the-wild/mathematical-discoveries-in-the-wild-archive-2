\section{Original conjecture}

D. Frymark and C. Liaw, \emph{Perspectives on General Left-Definite Theory},
arXiv:2012.01014, state the following as Conjecture~3 on arXiv PDF
page~10~\cite{source}.

\begin{conjecture}
Let $A$ be a semibounded self-adjoint operator in a Hilbert space $H$. If
$\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A$ of multiplicity $m$, then $\lambda$ is an
eigenvalue of the $r$-th left-definite operator $A_r$ in $H_r$, again of
multiplicity $m$.
\end{conjecture}

In the native normalization of general left-definite theory, $A\geq kI$ for
some $k>0$, $H_r=\operatorname{dom}(A^{r/2})$ with
\[
 \langle x,y\rangle_r=\langle A^{r/2}x,A^{r/2}y\rangle_H.
\]
A merely semibounded operator is handled by the standard positive scalar
shift used in the source.

\section{Earlier theorem and the implication}

L. L. Littlejohn and R. Wellman, \emph{On the Spectra of Left-Definite
Operators}, prove in Theorem~6.2(iii), PDF page~11, that $A$ and every $A_r$
are unitarily equivalent~\cite{support}.  More precisely,

\begin{theorem}
The map $U_r=A^{r/2}:H_r\to H$ is unitary, maps
$\operatorname{dom}(A_r)$ bijectively onto $\operatorname{dom}(A)$, and
satisfies
\[
 U_r A_r U_r^{-1}=A.
\]
\end{theorem}

For any $\lambda\in\mathbb R$, the intertwining identity gives
\[
 U_r\bigl(\ker(A_r-\lambda I)\bigr)
   =\ker(A-\lambda I).
\]
Indeed, $A_r x=\lambda x$ if and only if
$A(U_rx)=U_r(A_rx)=\lambda U_rx$, and the converse follows using $U_r^{-1}$.
Thus the two eigenspaces are linearly isomorphic, so their dimensions are
equal.  Since eigenvalue multiplicity for a self-adjoint operator is the
dimension of its eigenspace, Conjecture~3 follows in full.

For an operator only bounded below, choose $c$ so $B=A+cI\geq kI>0$ and apply
the theorem to $B$.  The same unitary intertwines the correspondingly shifted
left-definite realization of $A$ with $A$; subtracting $cI$ restores the
eigenvalue $\lambda$ and does not change eigenspace dimensions.

\section*{Provenance and scope}

The supporting paper predates the source conjecture, so it does not explicitly
claim to answer it.  The source cites this paper for spectral stability but
does not state the above multiplicity consequence.  This packet therefore
records an agent-identified direct implication, not a new proof and not an
author-explicit later answer.

A bounded search through 9 August 2026 checked the run indexes, exact
conjecture wording, the source's citations, and arXiv/web searches for
left-definite eigenvalue multiplicity and unitary equivalence.  The decisive
source was Littlejohn--Wellman Theorem~6.2.  The separate informal question
about interlacing for two different self-adjoint extensions is not settled by
this identification.
