# FUNTF connectivity and generic full spark were settled by arXiv:1311.4748

Status: `literature_already_answered`.

Alexeev, Cahill, and Mixon state on PDF page 19 of arXiv:1110.3548 that it
was unknown whether the real or complex variety of finite unit-norm tight
frames is connected in general.  Their motivating goal was to deduce that
full-spark unit-norm tight frames are dense.

Cahill, Mixon, and Strawn, arXiv:1311.4748, explicitly cite *Full Spark
Frames* and completely resolve the frame-homotopy problem.  Theorems 1.1 and
1.2 prove path-connectedness for every complex FUNTF space and for the real
spaces in the genuinely redundant range `N>=d+2`.  The excluded real
`N=d+1` spaces have multiple components, so the blanket real connectedness
question has a qualified negative answer.  Theorem 1.6 proves the source's
stronger practical objective directly: generic real and complex FUNTFs are
full spark for every `N>=d`.
