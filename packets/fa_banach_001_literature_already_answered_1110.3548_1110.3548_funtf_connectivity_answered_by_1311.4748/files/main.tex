\section*{Source question}

In Section 3 of \emph{Full Spark Frames}, Alexeev, Cahill, and Mixon
\cite{ACM} seek density of full-spark unit-norm tight frames (FUNTFs).  On
page 19 of the source PDF they write:
\begin{quote}
``It is unknown whether the variety of $M\times N$ unit norm tight frames is
connected in general.''
\end{quote}
They explain that connectedness, together with nonsingularity where
available, would yield irreducibility and hence the desired density.  At that
point they can only show that every FUNTF is close to a full-spark tight frame
whose column norms are close to one.

\section*{Decisive later answer}

Cahill, Mixon, and Strawn \cite{CMS} explicitly cite \emph{Full Spark Frames}
and state that they completely resolve the frame-homotopy problem.  In their
notation $\mathcal F^{\mathbb F}_{N,d}$ is the space of $N$-element FUNTFs in
$\mathbb F^d$.  On supporting-PDF page 2, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 prove
\[
 \mathcal F^{\mathbb C}_{N,d}\text{ is path-connected for every }N\ge d,
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal F^{\mathbb R}_{N,d}\text{ is path-connected for }
 d\ge2,\quad N\ge d+2.
\]
The low-redundancy qualification is real: the same paper records that
$\mathcal F^{\mathbb R}_{d+1,d}$ has $2^d$ connected components.  Thus the
source's unrestricted real connectedness question is false, while the whole
genuinely redundant range $N\ge d+2$ is affirmative.  The complex question
is affirmative without exceptions.

More directly, supporting-PDF page 3, Theorem 1.6, proves that a generic frame
in $\mathcal F^{\mathbb C}_{N,d}$ and in
$\mathcal F^{\mathbb R}_{N,d}$ is full spark for every $N\ge d$.  This settles
the density objective that motivated the source question, including the real
exceptional spaces where connectedness itself fails.

\section*{Provenance, scope, and search}

This is an explicit literature answer rather than an agent-derived
implication: the supporting paper cites Alexeev--Cahill--Mixon, uses its
definition of full spark, and announces both a complete homotopy resolution
and generic full spark.  The packet does not address unrelated deterministic
RIP or complexity questions elsewhere in the source paper.

The run registry and local sources were searched for arXiv:1110.3548,
``unit norm tight frames connected,'' ``FUNTF connectivity,'' and
``full spark.''  A bounded web search through 13 August 2026 located the
decisive arXiv:1311.4748 paper and later symplectic/topological confirmations.
