\section*{Source question}
G\l odkowski and Koszmider ask in Question 32, on source PDF page 15 \cite{Source}:
\begin{quote}
``Assume the continuum hypothesis CH. Are the algebras $\Q(\ell_2)$ and $\Q(\Q(\ell_2)\otimes\K(\ell_2))$ isomorphic?''
\end{quote}
Here $\Q(A)=\M(A)/A$ denotes the corona algebra.  Put
\[
 Q=\B(\ell_2)/\K(\ell_2),
 \qquad
 C=\M(Q\otimes\K)/(Q\otimes\K).
\]

\section*{Supporting theorem and identification}
For every $C^*$-algebra $A$, the stable multiplier algebra $\M(A\otimes\K)$ has vanishing $K$-theory:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:vanish}
 K_0(\M(A\otimes\K))=K_1(\M(A\otimes\K))=0.
\end{equation}
This is the standard stable-multiplier theorem; Thiel invokes it on supporting PDF page 5 and, in Corollary 3.3 and Example 3.4 on pages 5--6, applies the resulting six-term exact sequence specifically to $Q\otimes\K$ \cite{Thiel}.

First apply the six-term sequence to the Calkin extension
\[
 0\longrightarrow\K\longrightarrow\B(\ell_2)
 \longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow0.
\]
Since $\B(\ell_2)=\M(\K)$ has zero $K$-theory by \eqref{eq:vanish}, while
$K_0(\K)\cong\mathbb Z$ and $K_1(\K)=0$, its boundary maps give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:q}
 K_0(Q)=0,
 \qquad
 K_1(Q)\cong\mathbb Z.
\end{equation}
This is also the familiar Fredholm-index computation of the $K$-theory of the Calkin algebra.

Now apply the six-term sequence to
\[
 0\longrightarrow Q\otimes\K
 \longrightarrow\M(Q\otimes\K)
 \longrightarrow C\longrightarrow0.
\]
Stability of $K$-theory and \eqref{eq:vanish} make both boundary maps isomorphisms of degree one:
\[
 K_0(C)\cong K_1(Q\otimes\K)\cong K_1(Q)\cong\mathbb Z,
 \qquad
 K_1(C)\cong K_0(Q\otimes\K)\cong K_0(Q)=0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 (K_0(Q),K_1(Q))=(0,\mathbb Z)
 \quad\text{but}\quad
 (K_0(C),K_1(C))=(\mathbb Z,0).
\]
An isomorphism of $C^*$-algebras induces degree-preserving isomorphisms on $K_0$ and $K_1$.  Therefore $Q$ and $C$ are not isomorphic.  The computation is a theorem of ZFC and is valid in every model, so CH is irrelevant.
