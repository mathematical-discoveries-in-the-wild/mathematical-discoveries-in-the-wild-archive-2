# arXiv:2107.05806 — C*-uniqueness question answered later

Status: `literature_already_answered (Question 9.2: necessary and sufficient
conditions for C*-uniqueness)`

Mori asks for a useful necessary-and-sufficient condition for a `*`-algebra
to have a unique C*-norm. An and Gao, JMAA 526 (2023), article 127341,
explicitly reproduce this as their Question 2 and answer it affirmatively.

Their Theorems 4.1, 4.4, 4.6, and 5.6 characterize C*-uniqueness of a
pre-C*-algebra using representations, ideals of its enveloping C*-algebra,
its primitive ideal space, and weak containment. Theorem 4.7 gives a concrete
test: for a unital pre-C*-algebra, invertibility of every self-adjoint element
must be independent of the faithful representation.
