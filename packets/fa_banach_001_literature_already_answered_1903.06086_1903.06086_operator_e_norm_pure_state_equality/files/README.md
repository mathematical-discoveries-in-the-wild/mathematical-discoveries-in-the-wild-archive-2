# arXiv:1903.06086 operator E-norm question — affirmative literature answer

Status: `literature_already_answered`

Original source:

- M. E. Shirokov, *On extension of quantum channels and operations to the
  space of relatively bounded operators*, arXiv:1903.06086.
- Exact location: equation (13) and the paragraph immediately following it,
  original arXiv PDF page 5.

Answering source:

- M. E. Shirokov, *Operator E-norms and their use*, arXiv:1806.05668v6;
  Sbornik: Mathematics 211 (2020), 1323–1353.
- Exact location: Section 5, Proposition 6, original arXiv PDF page 20.

The question asks whether the mixed-state definition of the operator
E-norm agrees, for arbitrary positive `G`, with the supremum over a single
subnormalized vector for every `sqrt(G)`-bounded operator. Proposition 6
answers this affirmatively without a discreteness assumption. Its part A
identifies the pure-vector and mixed-state definitions for every
`sqrt(G)`-bounded operator, and part B explicitly permits vectors of norm at
most one subject to the energy constraint—the exact expression asked about.

- Exact location: Lemma 2.19(2),(4), formulas (2.33)–(2.34), arXiv PDF pages
  15–16.
