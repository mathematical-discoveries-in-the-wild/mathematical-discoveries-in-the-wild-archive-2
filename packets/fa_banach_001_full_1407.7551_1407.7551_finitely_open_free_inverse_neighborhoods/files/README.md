# Finitely-open free inverse neighborhoods

Candidate full affirmative answer to the question following Proposition 5.5 of
arXiv:1407.7551.

The packet proves that the levelwise inverse neighborhoods can be chosen
finitely open and G-free for every case in the source (`G = GL` or `O`, over
the stated real/complex fields).  The key is a compact orthogonal/unitary
induction by matrix size, based on pairwise block-derivative compatibility;
the GL cases then follow by taking the full similarity envelope.

Files:

- `main.tex`: self-contained proof and literature scope.
- `attempts.md`: eight progressively stronger investigation attempts.
- `verification.md`: proof and rendering checks.
- `source_paper.pdf`: the source arXiv paper.
- `figures/open_problem_crop.png`: full-width source evidence from page 19.
- `solution_packet.pdf`: compiled packet.
