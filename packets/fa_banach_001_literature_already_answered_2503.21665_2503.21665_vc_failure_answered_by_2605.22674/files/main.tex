\section*{Source question}

Bj\"orn, Bj\"orn, and Mal\'y, \emph{Non-quasicontinuous Newtonian functions
and outer capacities based on Banach function spaces}, arXiv:2503.21665,
study the following properties of a Newtonian space $N^1X(\Pcal)$:
\begin{align*}
 (A)&:\ \text{every Newtonian function is quasicontinuous},\\
 (B)&:\ C_X\text{ is an outer capacity},\\
 (D)&:\ \text{weak quasicontinuity implies quasicontinuity},\\
 (F)&:\ \text{every Newtonian class has a quasicontinuous representative},\\
 (G)&:\ \text{every Newtonian function is weakly quasicontinuous}.
\end{align*}
Their Theorem 1.5 proves $(B)$ for proper $\Pcal$ and $(D)$ for locally
compact $\Pcal$ when $X$ has the Vitali--Carath\'eodory property.  Immediately
afterward (PDF page 4), they ask whether the remaining properties can fail
under that property.  In the locally compact situation, the genuinely
remaining quasicontinuity assertions are $(A),(F),(G)$.

\section*{Exact later answer}

Bj\"orn and Bj\"orn, \emph{Quasicontinuity of $N^{1,\infty}$ functions and
the Vitali--Carath\'eodory property on general metric spaces},
arXiv:2605.22674, answer this existential question affirmatively.  Their
Theorem 1.2 states that, under Vitali--Carath\'eodory for $L^\infty(\Pcal)$,
$\Cinf$ is outer and
\[
 (A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(F)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(G),
\]
but examples exist in which all three assertions fail.

Their Example 3.3 makes the answer completely explicit:
\[
 \Pcal=\{0,2^{-n}:n\ge1\},\qquad
 \mu=\delta_0+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}\delta_{2^{-n}},
 \qquad X=L^\infty(\Pcal).
\]
The space is compact, hence proper and locally compact.  Nevertheless,
$\chi_{\{0\}}\in\Ninf(\Pcal)$ is not weakly quasicontinuous and has no
quasicontinuous representative.  Thus $(A),(F),(G)$ fail even though
$L^\infty(\Pcal)$ has the Vitali--Carath\'eodory property.

\section*{Why the answer is exact}

The source asks an existence question over function spaces having the
Vitali--Carath\'eodory property.  The later example uses $L^\infty$, which is
a Banach function space in this atomic setting, and the metric space is even
compact.  It therefore supplies precisely the counterexample requested for
the remaining properties, rather than merely weakening one of the source
hypotheses.

This answer has limited scope.  It does not settle the separate implication
$(F)\Rightarrow(E)$ for Banach function spaces, nor the general removal of
properness in Theorem 1.5.  The later paper does remove properness for the
special case $X=L^\infty$.

\section*{Proof Intuition}

The construction is a convergent sequence with positive mass at every
point.  Positive point masses make essential suprema genuine pointwise
suprema; this yields the Vitali--Carath\'eodory property.  The space has no
nonconstant curves, and every nonempty set has $\Cinf$-capacity one, so the
capacity is outer and there are no nonempty capacity-zero exceptional sets.

But $0$ is a nonisolated point.  Hence $\chi_{\{0\}}$ is discontinuous there.
Because an exceptional set of capacity less than one must be empty, weak
quasicontinuity is just continuity.  Likewise, equality quasi-everywhere is
literal equality everywhere.  Consequently no representative can repair
$\chi_{\{0\}}$, forcing the simultaneous failure of $(A),(F),(G)$.
